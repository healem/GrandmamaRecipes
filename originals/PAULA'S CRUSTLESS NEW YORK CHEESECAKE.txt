                  PAULA'S CRUSTLESS NEW YORK CHEESECAKE

Recipe By     :PAULA MARTINEZ
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Cakes                           Cheesecake
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 LB  RICOTTA CHEESE
  16                OZ  CREAM CHEESE
     1/2                JUICE OF LEMON
     1/4           TSP  VANILLA
  3              TBLSP  CORNSTARCH
  3              TBLSP  FLOUR
     1/2           cup  BUTTER -- MELTED
  1               pint  SOUR CREAM
  4              whole  EGGS
  1 1/2           cups  SUGAR

BLEND RICOTTA AND CREAM CHEESE TOGETHER
BEAT IN EGGS, ONE AT A TIME
ADD VANILLA, LEMON, FLOUR, CORNSTARCH, SUGAR AND BUTTER
MIX WELL
FOLD IN SOUR CREAM
BLEND WELL UNTIL SMOOTH

GREASE 10 INCH SPRING FORM PAN
COVER THE OUTSIDE OF THE SPRING FORM PAN, WHOLE BOTTOM AND UP THE SIDES
WITH ONE PIECE OF FOIL (VERY IMPORTANT NOT TO HAVE ANY BREAKS IN THE FOIL
POUR MIXTURE INTO SPRING FORM PAN
PLACE SPRING FORM PAN INTO A LARGER PAN
PLACE IN COLD OVEN

TURN ON OVEN TO 325

BOIL A KETTLE OF WATER
WHEN BOILED, POUR WATER INTO THE LARGER PAN

BAKE 1 1/2 HOUR

DO NOT OPEN THE OVEN DOOR

TURN OFF OVEN
LEAVE CAKE IN FOR 2 HOURS TO REST

TOP WITH ANY GLAZE IF DESIRED
