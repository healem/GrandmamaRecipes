                        Maddy's Baked Tomato Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Luncheon                        Maddy's Salads
                Salads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4                     tomatoes, sliced
  1                cup  feta cheese
  1               tbls  marjoram
     1/8           tsp  salt
     1/8           tsp  pepper
     1/2           cup  grated cheese and bread crumbs

In a small, greased baking dish
Layer each ingredient
Top with cheese and bread crumbs
Bake for 30 minutes at 350
