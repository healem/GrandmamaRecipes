                               Tartar Sauce

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Fish                            Sauces
                Seafood

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1         tablespoon  fresh lemon juice
     1/4           cup  mayonnaise
     1/4      teaspoon  dry mustard
  2        tablespoons  gherkins -- chopped
  2        tablespoons  fresh parsley
  2        tablespoons  capers -- drained
                        Tabasco sauce

Mix lemon juice, mustard, and mayonnaise in a small bowl.  Gradually stir
in chopped pickles, parsley, and capers. Season to taste with Tabasco
sauce.  Cover and chill for 1/2 to 1 hour to blend flavors.
