                     Maddy's Roquefort Salad Dressing

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :0:10
Categories    : Maddy's Salad Dressings         Salad Dressings

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  sour cream
  6                 oz  roquefort cheese, crumbled
     1/2           cup  small curd low fat cottage cheese
  1                tsp  worcestershire sauce
     1/2           tsp  garlic powder
     1/2           tsp  hot Pepper Sauce
     1/3           cup  capers, drained and chopped
                        salt and pepper

Using electric mixer, beat all ingredients except capers in large bowl to
blend
Stir in capers
Season to taste with salt and pepper
