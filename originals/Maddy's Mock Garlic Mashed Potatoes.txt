                   Maddy's Mock Garlic Mashed Potatoes

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Maddy's Low Carb                Maddy's Vegetables
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1             medium  head cauliflower
  1               tbls  cream ceese, softened
     1/4           cup  parmesan, grated
     1/2           tsp  minced garlic
     1/8           tsp  chicken base or bouillon
     1/8           tsp  black pepper
     1/2           tsp  chopped fresh or dry chives, for garnish
  3               tbls  butter

Set a pot of water to boil over high heat

Clean and cut cauliflower into small pieces
Cook in boiling water for about 6 minutes, or until well done
Drain well (do not let cool)
Pat cooked cauliflower very dry between several layers of paper towels

In a bowl with an immersion blender, or in a food processor, puree the hot
cauliflower with the cream cheese, parmesan, garlic, chicken base and
pepper until almost smooth
Garnish with chives, and serve hot with pats of butter
