                   Maddy's Crunchy Garbonzo Bean Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Beans                           Healthy And Hearty
                Maddy's Salads                  Salads
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              13 oz  can garbonzo beans (3 cups cooked) or any -- white bean
  1                     tomato, diced
  1                     european cucumber, peeled, seeded, diced
  1                     fennel bulb (or 1 1/2 cups celery) diced
  2                     carrots, peeled, diced
     1/4           cup  pine nuts, toasted
     1/4           cup  shaved parmesan cheese
  1              clove  garlic, minced
  3               tbls  olive oil
  3               tbls  balsamic vinegar
  1                 ts  kosher salt
     1/2            ts  black pepper
  2               tbls  basil leaves, fresh, chopped
  2               tbls  parsley leaves, fresh, chopped

Mix beans, tomato, cucumber, fennel, carrots, pine nuts and cheese in
large salad bowl
Separately mix olive oil, vinegar, basil, parsley, garlic, salt and pepper
and blend.
Pour dressing over salad ingredients and mix thoroughly
Set in refrigerator to cool
