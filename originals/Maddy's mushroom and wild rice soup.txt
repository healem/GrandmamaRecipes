                   Maddy's mushroom and wild rice soup

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Maddy's Soups
                Soups & Stews                   Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1             medium  onion, finely chopped
  1         tablespoon  butter
     1/3           cup  wild rice
  7               cups  vegetable stock
  4             cloves  garlic, chopped
  18                oz  mushrooms, mixed (or 1 oz dried)
  1                     carrot, peeled, cut into matchstick strips
                        salt and pepper to taste

Saute the onion and wild rice for 2 minutes in the butter in a medium
sized saucepan
Stir in the vegetable stock
Cover and simmer 25 minutes, stirring occasionally

Add chopped garlic, cover again, and simmer another 15 minutes, until rice
is almost tender
Add mushrooms and simmer, covered, for another 10 minutes
(You can make the soup up to this point a day ahead and refrigerate)

Just before serving, stir the carrot strips into the soup and simmer for 2
minutes until the carrots are crisp and tender
Season with salt and pepper
