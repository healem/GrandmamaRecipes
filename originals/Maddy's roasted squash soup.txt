                       Maddy's roasted squash soup

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Maddy's Soups                   Soups & Stews

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4                     butternut squash, peeled and chunked
                        EVOO for brushing
                        Salt & pepper to taste
  28                oz  chicken stock
  28                oz  tomatoes in can, swuished
  3                 lg  apples, peeled and quartered
  1                 lb  carrots, peeled and chunked
  1                     onion, peeled and chunked
  1                cup  celery, chunked
  1               tbls  nutmeg
  1                     cinnamon stick

Preheat oven to 400
Place squash, carrots, celery, onion and apples on a baking sheet
Add enough olive oil to cover vegetables 
Roast vegetables until sharp knife penetrates the flesh easily, about 1 hr
Transfer vegetables to a large crockpot, add cinnamon stick and nutmeg,
stock and tomatoes
Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring occasionally until all
vegetables are done
Remove cinnamon stick
Use immersion blender to puree until smooth
If soup is too thick, add more stock
Season with salt and pepper
Ladle soup into warmed bowls 
Serve immediately
Serves 6-8
