                    Maddy's Bacon Wrapped Breadsticks

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 24    Preparation Time :0:45
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  parmesan cheese, grated
  2                tsp  garlic powder
  12            slices  bacon
  24                    4 1/2 inch sesame breadsticks (1 pkg)

Preheat oven to 350

In a mixing bowl, combine cheese and garlic powder and set aside
Cut bacon slices in half so they are approximately 5 inches long
Wrap each bread stick with 1 slice of bacon, starting at one end and
ending at the other
Place wrapped bread sticks on a cookie sheet lined with parchment paper
Bake for 15 minutes or until bacon is browned
Remove from oven and immediately roll bread sticks in cheese mixture
Let cool and serve at room temperature
