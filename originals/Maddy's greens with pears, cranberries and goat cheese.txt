          Maddy's greens with pears, cranberries and goat cheese

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Luncheon                        Maddy's Salads
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  6                 oz  baby greens (mesclun)
  2               firm  pears (preferably bosc) cored and diced
     1/4           cup  dried cranberries
  4                 oz  crumbled goat cheese
                        balsamic vinaigrette or raspberry
                        vinaigrette to taste

Combine ingredients in a serving bowl, toss well and serve

                                    - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

calories from fat); 9g Protein; 1g Carbohydrate; trace Dietary Fiber; 30mg
Cholesterol; 98mg Sodium.  Exchanges: 1 Lean Meat; 0 Fruit; 1 Fat.
