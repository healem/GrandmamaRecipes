                              Maddy's Hummus

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:20
Categories    : Appetizers                      Ethnic
                Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1         tablespoon  sesame oil
  1              small  onion -- chopped
  1              clove  garlic -- minced
  2        tablespoons  sesame seeds -- toasted lightly
     1/2           cup  sesame butter
  1                can  garbanzo beans -- drained
     1/4           cup  fresh lemon juice
     1/2      teaspoon  turmeric
     1/4      teaspoon  salt

     Saute onions and garlic in sesame oil until just tender.  Mash beans
in food processor.  Add remaining ingredients and blend until desired
consistency.  More sesame oil may be added if mixture is too thick.
     Garnish with parsley and additional toasted sesame seeds, if desired.
