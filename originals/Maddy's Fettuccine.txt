                            Maddy's Fettuccine

Recipe By     :Madelne M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Chicken                         Luncheon
                Maddy's Main Meals              Maddy's Pasta Dishes
                Main Dishes                     Pasta

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2           handfuls  sun-dried tomatoes (whole)
  2                lbs  fresh or dried fettucine
  2                lbs  skinless boneless chicken breast
                        white wine to taste
                        cajun seasoning
                        oregano to taste
  3               tbls  finely chopped garlic
                        pepper to taste
  16                oz  pine nuts
                        olive oil
  3                     green peppers thinly sliced
  2                     red pepper thinly slices
  1                     eggplant chopped
  24                oz  combined basil, olive oil, pine nuts, parm
                        esan cheese and garlic blended very well -- = pesto sauce
  1              bunch  cilantro
                        margarine
  4               tbls  curry powder

soak sun dried tomatoes in warm water until soft
boil the water and add fettucccine
place chicken in flat bottomed baking dish and cover with wine
bake at 350 for 30 minutes or until done
strain wine from chicken and season chicken with cajun seasoning, oregano,
1 tbls garlic and pepper.  Cover until pasta is finished.
Toast pne nuts in frying pan unil browned with ts of garlic and olive oil.
 Reserve pine nuts in bowl
In same fry pan, saute additional olive oil, 2 ts garlic, thinly sliced
green and red peppers, and chopped eggplant until soft and moist.
Add to the bowl wit pine nuts.
In same fry pan, mix pesto sauce with chopped cilantro, while adding olive
oil, margarine, the remaining garlic and curry to taste.
Let sit for a while on extremely low heat and simmer for 3 minutes
Add reserved pine nuts, peppers, and eggplant.
Once pasta is cooked and strained, toss into the hot sauce over low heat
while adding in the sundried tomatoes.
Place in a serving dish and slice the chicken over the top.
