                          Crusty Potato Pancakes

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:45
Categories    : Ethnic                          Potatoes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  6             medium  potatoes -- peeled
  1              small  onion -- finely chopped
  3                     eggs
     3/4           cup  flour
  1           teaspoon  salt
  2        tablespoons  butter
  1         tablespoon  cooking oil

     Grate potatoes into a bowl filled with cold water.  In another bowl,
combine onion, egg, flour, and salt.  Drain potatoes, pressing out all
liquid.  Beat potatoes into batter. Melt butter with oil in large skillet.
 Spoon heaping tablespoons of batter into skillet, spreading batter with
the back of spoon into 4 inch rounds.  Brown on both sides Drain on paper
towels.
