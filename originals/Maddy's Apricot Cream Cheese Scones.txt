                   Maddy's Apricot Cream Cheese Scones

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Breakfast
                Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3 1/4           cups  flour
     1/2           cup  sugar
  2 1/2           tsps  baking powder
     1/2           tsp  salt
  8                 oz  package cream cheese (cold)
     1/2           cup  ( 1 stick) cold butter
  2               cups  sliced or slivered apricots
  1              large  egg
  2               tsps  vanilla
     1/4           cup  milk
                        sugar for sprinkling on top

In medium mixing bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder and
salt.
Cut in the cream cheese and butter until it resembles coarse cornmeal
Stir in the apricots

In a separate bowl whisk together egg, vanilla and milk

Combine liquid and dry ingredients
Stir until the dough becomes cohesive
Turn dough out onto a floured work surface
Fold it over several times, until it holds together.
Pat the dough into a 3/4 inch thick rectangle.

Cut scones to whatever shape desired.  
Brush tops with milk
Sprinkle with sugar

Place scones about 2 inches apart on an ungreased baking sheet
Bake at 425 for 8 minutes.
Turn off oven, leave the door closed for another 8 minutes, until scones
are light golden brown
