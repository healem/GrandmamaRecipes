                            Maddy's Tea Breads

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Breakfast
                Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        NUT BREAD
                        	
  2 1/2           cups  flour
  1                cup  sugar
  3 1/2            tsp  baking powder
  1                tsp  salt
  3              tblsp  oil
  1 1/4           cups  milk
  1                     egg
  1                cup  nuts, finely chopped

Heat oven to 350.  Grease and flour 9x5x3-inch loaf pan.  Place all
ingredients into large mixer bowl.  Beat on medium speed 1/2 minute,
scraping sides and bottom of bowl constantly.  Pour mixture into pan.
Bake for 55 to 65 minutes or until wooden pick inserted in the center
comes out clean.  Remove from pan.  Cool thoroughly before slicing.

VARIATIONS:
Apricot-Nut:  Decrease milk to 1/2 cup; add 4 tsp grated orange peel, 3/4
cup orange juice and 1 cup finely chopped dried apricots.

Banana-Nut:  Decrease milk to 3/4 cup; add 1 cup mashed ripe bananas (2 or
3 medium)

Date-Nut:  Add 1 cup cut-up dates

Orange-Nut:  Decrease milk to 1/2 cup; add 4 tsp grated orange peel and
3/4 cup orange juice

Prune-Nut:   Decrease milk to 1/2 cup; add 3/4 cup prune juice and 1 cup
drained chopped cooked prunes.
