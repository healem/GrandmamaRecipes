                    Maddy's peasant sytle green beans

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables              Vegetables
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2                lbs  green beans
  2                 lg  new potatoes, (about 1 lb) peeled, chunked
  4             medium  carrots, cut into small chunks
  5        tablespoons  olive oil
  2                     garlic cloves, sliced

Trim ends of green beans and halve crosswise
Pull strings from any that are a little tough or oversized

In a stockpot, bring 2 quarts lightly salted water to a boil over high
heat.  Add the beans
Cook, uncovered over medium-high heat for about 15 minutes, until tender
Using slotted spoon, lift beans from the water and transfer to a colander
to drain

Add the potatoes and carrots to the pot, and return the water to a boil
Cook potatoes and carrots over medium heat for about 15 minutes, until
they are fork tender
Drain the vegetables (You can reserve the cooking liquid to make a light
soup with small pasta pieces)
Transfer the beans, potatoes and carrots to a serving dish

In a small saucepan, heat the oil over medium heat.
Add the garlic and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until it is golden (do not let
it burn)
Pour the hot oil and garlic over the vegetables, toss them gently and
serve them immediately
