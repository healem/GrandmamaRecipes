                            Lemon Pudding Cake

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:15
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3 1/2           cups  flour
  1 1/2      teaspoons  baking powder
     1/4      teaspoon  baking soda
  1 3/4           cups  sugar
  8             ounces  lemon yogurt, lowfat
     1/4      teaspoon  salt
  4              whole  eggs
  2        tablespoons  lemon jiuce
  1                cup  water
     3/4           cup  oil
  2               pkgs  instant lemon pudding -- 3.4 ounces each
                        	
               TOPPING  	
     3/4           cup  lemon juice
  4               cups  confectioner's sugar

Beat eggs until thick
Add all other ingredients
Beat at medium speed for 10 minutes
Pour into greased tube pan with removable bottom
Bake at 350 for about 50 minutes

TOPPING
When cake is almost done, combine lemon juice and sugar in a pan 
Heat to the boiling point
While cake is still hot, loosen it from the sides of the pan
Using a 2-tined fork, prick holes in top of cake
Drizzle glaze in holes and over top of cake(let it drip down the sides
too)
After cake has cooled down, remove it from the pan
