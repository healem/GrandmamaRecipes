                         Oriental Grilled Chicken

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Ethnic                          Healthy And Hearty
                Main Dishes                     Poultry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2                     boneless skinless chicken breasts
     1/2           cup  soy sauce
  2          teaspoons  brown sugar
     1/2           cup  rice wine
  1         tablespoon  sesame oil
  1         tablespoon  scallions -- chopped
  1         1/2" slice  ginger root slice -- chopped

     Cut chicken breasts in half.  Mix remaining ingredients together in a
shallow pan large enough to hold breasts in one layer.  Place breasts in
marinade, turning to coat and rubbing marinade into breasts.  Marinate at
least one hour and as long as overnight.
     Grill chicken over hot coals or under broiler.  Strain marinade,
bring it to a boil, and serve it with chicken.
