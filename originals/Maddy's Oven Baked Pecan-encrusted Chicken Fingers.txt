            Maddy's Oven Baked Pecan-encrusted Chicken Fingers

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Chicken                         Maddy's Main Meals
                Main Dishes                     Poultry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  pecan pieces
     1/2           cup  bread crumbs
  1 1/2    tablespoons  (Emeril's creole seasoning)  *Essence
  2              large  eggs
     1/4           cup  olive oil
  2                lbs  chicken breasts, boneless, skinless, cut
                        lengthwise into strips
  1           teaspoon  parsley, chopped
                        	
                        Emeril's Creole Seasoning
  2 1/2    tablespoons  paprika
  2        tablespoons  salt
  2        tablespoons  garlic powder
  1         tablespoon  black pepper
  1         tablespoon  onion powder
  1         tablespoon  cayenne
  1         tablespoon  oregano, dried leaf
  1         tablespoon  thyme, dried

*Essence  (Emeril's Creole Seasoning)
Combine all ingredients thoroughly and store in an airtight container 

Preheat oven to 375 
Lightly grease a large baking sheet

In a food processor, combine the pecan pieces, bread crumbs and 1
tablespoon of Essence.  Pulse for 1 minute to combine
Pour into a shallow dish

In a bowl, beat eggs, olive oil and remaining 1/2 tablespoon Essence
One at a time, dip the chicken into the egg mixture, then dredge in the
pecan mixture, shaking to remove excess
Transfer to the baking sheet and bake, turning once, until the chicken is
cooked through and the crust is golden, 15 to 20 minutes.

Remove the chicken from the oven and transfer to 4 plates
Garnish with parsley and serve with honey mustard dipping sauce on the
side
