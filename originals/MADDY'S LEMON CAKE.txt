                            MADDY'S LEMON CAKE

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:50
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               PKGS  YELLOW CAKE MIX
  2               PKGS  INSTANT LEMON PUDDING
  2 1/2           cups  WATER
  6             medium  EGGS
     1/2           cup  OIL
                        	
                        TOPPING
  1                cup  LEMON JUICE
  1 1/2           cups  CONFECTION SUGAR

MIX ALL CAKE INGREDIENTS UNTIL WELL BLENDED
MIX FOR SEVERAL MORE MINUTES
GREASE ANGEL FOOD CAKE PAN
POUR MIX INTO PAN
BAKE AT 325 FOR ABOUJT 1 1/2 HOURS OR UNTIL PROBE COMES OUT DRY

ADD LEMON JUICE AND SUGAR TO SMALL SAUCEPAN
COOK OVER MEDIUM  HEAT UNTIL BOILING

WHILE CAKE IS STILL HOT POKE HOLES IN CAKE AND POUR SAUCE INTO HOLES ALL
AROUND CAKE
POUR REMAINING MIXTURE OVER TOP
