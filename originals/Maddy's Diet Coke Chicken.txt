                        Maddy's Diet Coke Chicken

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Chicken                         Maddy's Main Meals

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 lb  chicken tenders (or 4 chicken breasts)
     1/2           cup  diet coke
  1                can  cream of mushroom soup
  2        tablespoons  onion flakes
  1         tablespoon  parsley
                        salt & pepper to taste

Peheat oven to 350
Spray baking dish
Place chicken pieces in baking dish
Combine all other ingredients
Pour over chicken
Bake 1 - 1 1/2 hours until cooked through
