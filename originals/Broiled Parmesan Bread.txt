                          Broiled Parmesan Bread

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                     loaf French bread
     1/2           cup  butter -- softened
  6             ounces  Parmesan cheese -- grated
  1         tablespoon  caraway seed

   Cut bread in half lengthwise. Combine cheese, butter, and caraway
seeds. Spread over cut surfaces of bread. Place bread cheese side up on a
baking sheet. Broil 5 minutes or until cheese is melted.
