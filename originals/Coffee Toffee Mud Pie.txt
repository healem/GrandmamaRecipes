                          Coffee Toffee Mud Pie

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Desserts                        Maddy's Desserts
                Pies & Pastry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  butter -- 2 sticks
  1                cup  sugar
  2          teaspoons  lemon juice
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/4      teaspoon  almond extract
     1/4      teaspoon  vanilla
  2 1/2           cups  walnuts -- toasted, chopped
  4 1/2         ounces  bittersweet chocolate -- finely chopped
                        	
                 CRUST  	
  3        tablespoons  butter -- melted
  15                    oreo cookes -- crushed
                        	
               FILLING  	
  1               pint  coffee ice cream -- softened
  1               pint  chocolate ice cream -- softened
     1/2          pint  whipped cream

TOFFEE
Line rimmed cookie sheet with foil, butter foil
Combine first 7 ingredients in large heavy saucepan
Stir over low heat until sugar dissolves
Increase heat to medium
Cook until candy thermometer registers 300 (frequently stir and brush down
sides of pan with pastry brush dipped in water) about 20 minutes
Mix in nuts.  Pour onto cookie sheet.  Let stand 2 minutes
Sprinkle chocolate over top.  Let stand until chocolate melts, about 3
minutes
Using back of spoon, spread chocolate over toffee
Freeze toffee until firm about 1 hour
Chop into small pieces.  Store in refrigerator

CRUST
Preheat oven to 350
Grind cookies and butter in processor until moist crumbs form
Press crumb mixture onto bottom (not up the sides) of a 9-inch springform
pan, with 2 3/4 inch high sides
Bake 10 minutes.  Transfer to rack.  Cook completely

FILLING
Place coffee ice cream in medium bowl
Mix in half of toffee
Spoon mixture onto crust
Freeze 2 hours

Place chocolate ice cream in medium bowl
Mix in rmaining toffee
Spoon over coffee ice cream, smooth top
Cover with plastic wrap and freeze overnight

Release pan sides from ice cream
Spoon whipped cream into pastry bag fitted with star tip
Pipe rosettes of cream decoratively atop ice cream
