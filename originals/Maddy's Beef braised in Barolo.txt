                      Maddy's Beef braised in Barolo

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey (Emeril Lagase)
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :3:00
Categories    : Beef                            Maddy's Main Meals
                Main Dishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/4           cup  olive oil
  3                 lb  boneless beef chuck roast, patted dry
  2                tsp  kosher salt
  1                tsp  black pepper
     1/4           cup  panecetta or bacon, chopped
  2               cups  chpped onions
  1                cup  chopped carrots
  1                cup  chopped celery
  1               tbls  minced garlic
  1 1/2        bottles  barolo, or other dry red wine
  4               cups  beef stock
  4              whole  cloves
  2             sprigs  rosemary
  2                     bay leaves
  1                     cinnamon stick
     1/4           cup  chopped parsley for garnish

In a large dutch oven, heat oil over medum-high heat
Sprinkle beef with salt and pepper
Add beef and cook, turning to brown on all sides
Remove from pan
To the fat in the pan, add the pancetta and cook unti browned, about 3
minutes
Add the onions, carrots, and celery and cook until caramelized, about 10
minutes
Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds
Return the beef to the pan and add the wine, 2 cups of stock, cloves,
rosemary, bay leaves and cinnamon stick
Bring to a boil
Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer until meat is tender, 2 1/2
to 3 hours, occasionally turning the meat and skimming any foam that forms
on the surface
Add the remaining  2 cups of stock as needed to keep the meat covered with
liquid

Remove meat from pan and tent with foil to keep warm

Strain sauce into a saucepan and place over high heat
Cook until sauce is reduced to a consistency thick enough to coat the back
of a spoon, about 10 minutes
Adjust seasoning to taste

Thinly slice the beef across the grain into 1/4 inch slices
Garish with parsley
Serve the beef ladled with the sauce
