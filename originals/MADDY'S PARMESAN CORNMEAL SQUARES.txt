                    MADDY'S PARMESAN CORNMEAL SQUARES

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 16    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Breakfast
                Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  12             TBLES  CORNMEAL
  3               cups  CHICKEN BROTH
  1                cup  GRATED PARMESAN CHEESE
                        OLIVE OIL

WHISK CORNMEAL IN BOWL
BRING CHICKEN BROTH TO BOIL IN HEAVY MEDIUM SAUCEPAN, OVER MEDIUM HIGH
HEAT
GRADUALLY WHISK IN CORNMEAL
REDUCE HEAT TO MEDIUM LOW,
WHISK CONSTANTLY UNTIL MIXTURE BOILS AND BECOMES VERY THICK, ABOUT 5
MINUTES
REMOVE FROM HEAT
WHISK IN 1/2 CUP PARMESAN, SEASON WITH SALT AND PEPPER 
POUR INTO 8X8X2 INCH BAKING PAN
SMOOTH TOP
COOL UNTIL FIRM, AT LEAST 30 MINUTES, OR COVER AND CHILL OVERNIGHT

(RECIPE MAY BE PREPARED 1 DAY AHEAD.  COVER AND REFRIGERATE)

PREHEAT BROILER
CUT CORNBREAD INTO 16 SQUARES
BRUSH GENEROUSLY WITH OIL
ARRANGE CORNBREAD, OILED SIDE DOWN, ON SMALL BAKING SHEET
BRUSH TOP WITH OIL
BROIL UNTIL HEATED THROUGH AND GOLDEN BROWN, ABOUT 3 MINUTES EACH SIDE (OR
5 MINUTES IF REFRIGERATED)
TRANSFER TO PLATTER
SPRINKLE WITH 1/2 CUP PARMESAN
