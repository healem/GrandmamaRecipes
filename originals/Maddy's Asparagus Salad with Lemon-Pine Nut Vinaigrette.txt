         Maddy's Asparagus Salad with Lemon-Pine Nut Vinaigrette

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Maddy's Salads                  Salads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2             lb  asparagus, woody ends removed
     1/8           tsp  finely chopped garlic
     1/4           cup  chopped fresh parsley
  2                tsp  finely grated lemon zest
  2               tbls  fresh lemon juice
  2               tbls  toasted pine nuts
  2                tsp  olive oil
                        pinch sugar
                        salt and pepper to taste

In a large skillet, bring 2 cups of salted water to a boil
Add asparagus and cook until crisp-tender, about 2 minutes
Drain and plunge into a bowl of ice water to cook it quickly
Remove from ice water and set aside

Place remaining ingredients in a food processor and puree

Toss asparagus with lemon dressing

Store in refrigerator for up to 2 days until ready to serve
