                            MADDY'S SPICE CAKE

Recipe By     :Madeline
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  6               cups  flour
  1         tablespoon  baking soda
  2          teaspoons  salt
  1 1/2      teaspoons  ground cloves
  1 1/2           cups  salad oil
  4 1/2           cups  sugar
  1         tablespoon  cinnamon
  1 1/2      teaspoons  nutmeg
  3               cups  milk
  6              whole  eggs

Mix all ingredients together with electric beaters
Pour into greased 15 x 10 pan
Bake at 350 for about an hour or until tine comes out clean

Use half recipe for angel cake pan
