                   Maddy's Honey Mustard Dipping Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:20
Categories    : Maddy's Sauces                  Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  mayonnaise
  2        tablespoons  honey
  2        tablespoons  creole mustard (or other hot whole grain
                        mustard)
                 pinch  salt
                 pinch  cayenne pepper

In a small bowl, combine all ingredients and stir well
Cover tightly with plastic wrap and refrigerate until ready to use

Approximately 3/4 cup

10mg Cholesterol; 157mg Sodium.  Exchanges: 2 Fat; 1/2 Other
Carbohydrates.
