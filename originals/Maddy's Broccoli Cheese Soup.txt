                       Maddy's Broccoli Cheese Soup

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Soups                   Maddy's Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              bunch  broccoli, 1 1/4 lbs, washed and trimmed
  2               cups  sliced onions
  5               tbls  butter
  7               cups  chicken broth
  1                tsp  dried leaf oregano, crumbled
     1/4           cup  flour
  2               tbls  dijon mustard
     1/8           tsp  pepper
  1                cup  milk
  2               cups  shredded sharp cheddar cheese

Cut florets into small sections
Simmer in lightly salted water for 2 minutes
Drain and immerse in cold water to stop cooking
Set aside

Cut stalks into small pieces
Place in large saucepan
Add sliced onions and 3 tbles butter
Saute over medium low heat for 5 minutes
Add 3 cups chicken broth and oregano
Simmer 20 to 30 minutes
Use immersion blender to puree

Melt remaining 2 tblsp butter in saucepan
Stir in flour and cook, stirring constantly until smooth and bubbly
Stir in mustard and pepper
Gradually stir in processed mixture and remainoing 4 cups chicken broth
Continue to cook, stirring, until mixture begins to bubble
Add milk and cheese
Stir until smooth and cheese is melted
Add remainng broccoli florets and heat through
