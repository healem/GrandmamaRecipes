                         Maddy's vegan cornbread

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Maddy's Breads
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/4           cups  yellow corn meal (stone-ground if possible
  1 1/4           cups  unbleached white flour
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
  1         tablespoon  baking powder
  2        tablespoons  brown sugar
  1 1/2           cups  boiling water
  2        tablespoons  canola oil
                        oil to grease pan

Heat oven to 425
Lightly oil cast iron skillet 
Heat pan in oven while you mix the cornbread batter

Put water and canola oil in saucepan and bring to boil

In large bowl, stir together the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, brown
sugar and salt
Add the boiling water and stir to mix.  Do not overbeat
Add additional water if needed to make a light batter

Remove hot skillet from oven
Spoon batter into the hot skillet.  (Batter should sizzle when it contacts
the skillet)
Bake 30 minutes or until the cornbread is light brown on top ad springs
back to the touch

Cut into wedges and serve
