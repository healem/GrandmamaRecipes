                             Eggs Florentine

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:20
Categories    : Breakfast                       Eggs
                Luncheon

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  20            ounces  frozen spinach -- chopped
  8                     poached eggs
  1                cup  Mornay sauce
     1/4           cup  Parmesan cheese -- grated

     Steam the spinach according to package directions.  Drain throoughly,
squeezing out as much moisture as possible.
     Poach eggs. Place spinach in a alayer on bottom of a buttered baking
dish;top with the poached eggs.Pour Mornay sauce over all and sprinkle
with cheese.
Broil for 5-10 minutes, or until top is brown.
