                             Zurich Potatoes

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:45
Categories    : Potatoes                        Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4              whole  potatoes
     1/2           cup  butter
  1              whole  white onion
  2        tablespoons  fresh parsley -- minced
  2        tablespoons  green pepper -- finely chopped
                        paprika -- to taste

Peel potatoes and grate them into cold water. Let stand for 5 minutes.
Drain and squeeze all liquid out by handfuls. Dry on clean toweling. 

Melt half the butter in a large skillet, add grated onion and pepper, and
saute for two minutes. Add the drained potatoes. Sprinkle thickly with
paprika, cover tightly and cook over very low heat for 15 minutes. Check
occasionally to be sure the potatoes are not sticking to the bottom of the
pan, but do not stir or disarrange. Add extra butter, if needed, by
lifting potatoes carefully with a spatula. 

Carefully slide potatoes onto a warm plate. Melt remaining butter in
skillet and invert potatoes into skillet. Continue to cook another 10
minutes.
