                            Maddy's Tuna Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Fish                            Luncheon
                Maddy's Salads                  Salads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/4           cup  olive oil
  2        tablespoons  red wine vinegar
  1           teaspoon  oregano
  1           teaspoon  salt
     1/2      teaspoon  black pepper
     1/4      teaspoon  crushed red pepper flakes
  2               cups  tomatoes, cubed
     1/2            lb  mushrooms, sliced
  2        6-7 oz cans  tuna fish, drained well

combine oil, vinegar, oregano, salt, pepper and pepper flakes in a bowl
shake or stir until salt dissolves
In a medium bowl, combine tomatoes, mushrooms and tuna
add the dressing and stir to coat all the ingredients and break up the
tuna a little
Serve as a salad on lettuce leaves
or pack into pita bread
