                            Banana Split Cake

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                 CRUST  	
  2               cups  vanilla wafers -- crushed
  1              stick  butter -- softened
                        	
               FILLING  	
  2               cups  confectioner's sugar
  1              stick  butter
  2              whole  eggs -- beaten 5 minutes
  1            package  instant chocolate pudding
                        	
               TOPPING  	
  1                box  strawberries or blueberries -- large box
  1                cup  nuts -- chopped
  4              whole  bananas -- sliced
  1                can  pineapple, large can crushed -- drained
  1              large  cool whip Lite®

CRUST
Mix wafers and butter.  Spread in 9 x 13 baking dish

FILLING
Prepare instant chocolate pudding according to directions
Mix confectioner's sugar, butter and eggs
Pour over crust
Spread chocolate pudding over all

TOPPING
Add bananas and 1/2 of the berries
Add nuts and pineapple
Cover with cool whip
Sprinkle remaining berries over top

Refrigerate for 1 day.  Serve
