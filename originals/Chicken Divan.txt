                              Chicken Divan

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Main Dishes                     Poultry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/4           cup  butter
     1/4           cup  all-purpose flour
  1               pint  chicken broth
     1/2           cup  heavy cream
     1/3           cup  sherry
                        salt
                        black pepper
  2         10 oz pkgs  frozen broccoli -- cooked
  12                    chicken slices -- cooked
     1/4           cup  Parmesan cheese

     Melt butter.  Mix in flour.  Add chicken broth.  Bring to a boil,
stirring, and cook until thick.  Remove from heat and add cream, sherry,
salt, and pepper.  Drain cooked broccoli and place in baking dish.  Pour
half of the sherry sauce over the broccoli.  Arrange slices of chicken on
top.  Pour remaining sauce over chicken and sprinkle with cheese.  Broil
for 5 minutes.
