                  Maddy's cornbread and sausage stuffing

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Chicken                         Maddy's Stuffing
                Poultry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3               cups  chicken broth
  1                cup  diced apples
  4               tbls  olive oil
  1                 lg  onion, chopped
  3                     celery stalks, chopped
  3                     carrots, chopped
  1                 lb  mushrooms, chopped
  1                 lb  sausages
  1               loaf  corn bread, crumbled
  2               tbls  sage
  1                 ts  tyme
  2               tbls  parsley, fresh and chopped
                        salt & pepper to taste

Make a loaf of corn bread, using directions on box
In small saucepan, over medium heat, warm stock until steam begins to rise
Add cranberries and set aside

In saucepan, boil sausages until cooked through
Remove sausages and place on broiler pan, make a slice in skins of each
one, and place in oven at 400 until browned
Remove from oven, remove skins and place in food processor until chopped
fine

In fry pan, place oil and vegetables and cooked until done

In large mixing bowl, place bread crumbs, sausages and vegetables.
Mix well
Add broth and cranberries and mix again
Adjust seasongs
Stuff poultry (or place in casserole and cook at 350 for 1 hour)
