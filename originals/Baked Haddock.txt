                              Baked Haddock

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:45
Categories    : Fish                            Main Dishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4                     haddock fillets
     1/2           cup  butter -- melted
     1/4           cup  lemon juice
  2        tablespoons  Worcestershire sauce
     1/4      teaspoon  ground chili peppers
  1           teaspoon  salt
     1/4      teaspoon  black pepper
  2              whole  garlic cloves -- minced

     Mix salt, pepper and garlic together thoroughly and rub on both sides
of fish fillets. Place in a buttered shallow baking dish. Combine butter,
lemon juice, and Worcestershire sauce. Bake at 375 degrees for 30 minutes
or until fish flakes easily with fork. Baste with butter mixture 2-3 times
during baking.
