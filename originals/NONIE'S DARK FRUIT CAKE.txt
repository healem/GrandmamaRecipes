                         NONIE'S DARK FRUIT CAKE

Recipe By     :Noreen Rial
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :3:00
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        	
                        #1
  3               cups  mixed candied fruit
  2               cups  dates, cut in half
  2               cups  raisins
  3               cups  candied cherries and pineapple
  4               cups  nuts (almonds, brazils, pecans, walnuts) -- measure generously
                        #2
     1/2           cup  honey
     1/4           cup  blackstrap molasses
  1                cup  wine (sherry, marsala, madeira, brandy or -- bourbon)
                        #3
  1                cup  butter
  1 1/2           cups  dark brown sugar
  4              large  eggs
                        #4
  3               cups  all-purpose flour
  1                tsp  baking soda
  1                tsp  salt
  1                tsp  cinnamon
  1                tsp  allspice
     1/2           tsp  ginger
     1/2           tsp  nutmeg

PREPARE BATTER:pour all fruit and nuts in bowl (#1).  Mix until blended
Combine honey, molasses and wine (#2)
Pour mixture over the fruit and nuts (cover #1 with #2)
Cover tightly and store overnight at room temperature

Beat butter, sugar and eggs together until creamy (#3)
Drain liquid from fruit mixture, and add liquid to the egg mixture

Sift dry ingredients together (#4) and mix with butter mixture (#3)
(Add 1/4 cup additional wine if batter is too stiff to mix easily)

Transfer fruit to large pan
Add batter
Turn over and over to mix, using your hands or wide paddle

PREPARE PANS:  (Three pans 9 1/4 x 5 1/4 x 2 1/2 -  or  -  Four pans 8 1/2
x 4 1/2 x 2 1/2).   Grease loaf pans generously with any unsalted fat. 
(DO NOT USE OIL)  
Line with heavy brown paper, one piece lengthwise, one crosswise so that
bottom has double thickness.
Paper should project an inch above pan all around
Grease papers generously

Pack mixture into prepared pans
Level tops with spatula
Bake 1 1/2 - 2 hours at 275'
Remove from oven and cover loosely with foil
Turn cakes out of pans when cool.  Peel off paper

Wrap cakes in cloth saturated with wine, brandy, etc.
Wrap in double thickness of foil, then in plastic
Store in cool place

After 4-5 days, unwrap, resaturate cloths and rewrap
Wait at least 10-14 days before using

Cakes will keep indefinately in freezer, up to 6 months in refrigerator
Occasionally, unwrap, resaturate cloths and rewrap
