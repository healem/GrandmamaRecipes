                    Maddy's cheese and bacon biscuits

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Breakfast
                Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3               cups  flour
  4          teaspoons  baking powder
     3/4      teaspoon  salt
  8        tablespoons  butter, cold, unsalted
  1                cup  cheese, finely shredded
     1/2           cup  chives, chopped fresh
  6             slices  bacon, fried until crisp, chopped
  1 1/2           cups  milk

Position rack in lower third of oven
preheat to 400
lightly spray biscuit pan with nonstick cooking spray
In a large bowl, sift together flour, baking powder and salt
Using a pastry blender or 2 knives, cut in butter until it resenbles
coarse meal
Add cheese, chives and bacon
Using a fork, stir until evenly distributed
Add milk, using a rubber spatula, stir until just combined
Divide batter among wells of prepared pan
Bake until golden and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out
clean, about 30 minutes.
Let cool on wire rack 15 minutes
Invert pan onto rack, lift off pan
Cool 5 mintes before serving
Makes 7 biscuits
