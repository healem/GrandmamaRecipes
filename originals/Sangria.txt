                                 Sangria

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :5:00
Categories    : Beverages

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              whole  orange -- unpeeled, sliced
  1                cup  orange juice
  1              whole  lemon -- unpeeled, sliced
     1/2           cup  lemon juice
     1/2        gallon  Burgundy
  3        tablespoons  brandy
  10            ounces  sliced peaches -- with juice
     1/2           cup  strawberries -- sliced
  1              whole  banana -- sliced

Put all ingredients into large pitcher, stir, and refrigerate at least 4
hours or (preferably) overnight. Can be served from jar with ladle.
