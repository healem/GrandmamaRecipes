                       Maddy's No Cook Pasta Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Maddy's Sauces                  Sauces
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  8             medium  tmatoes, vine-ripened, peeled, seeded and
                        chopped
  2        tablespoons  shallots, minced
  1         tablespoon  garlic, minced
  8        tablespoons  parsley, fresh, finely chopped
     1/4           cup  lemon juice
  1 1/4           cups  Consorzio Dipping Oil
                        salt & peper to taste

Mix all ingredients in bowl
Let rest at room temperature for 15-20 minutes
