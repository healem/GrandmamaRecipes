            Maddy's Bean Salad (using lentils or black beans)

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Beans                           Maddy's Salads
                Salads                          Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        juice of 1 lemon
     2/3           cup  olive oil
  1           teaspoon  Italian seasoning
  1              clove  garlic -- mashed
  2               cups  dried lentils/black beans
     1/2           cup  diced celery
  2              whole  red ripe tomatoes -- chopped
                        salt & pepper
     1/2           cup  onion, chopped
  1                 ts  tarragon

Wash dried beans.  Place in pot and cover with water.  
Boil until just tender, about 30 - 50 minutes, depending on the beans
used.  Drain. 
Whisk together lemon juice, italian seasoning, garlic, tarragon, salt,
pepper and olive oil. Pour dressing over beans while they are hot.  
Cool to room temperature.  Add celery, onions and tomatoes.  Serve
immediately or chill and serve cold.
