                  Maddy's Bulgur Pilaf with Two Cheeses

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cheese                          Healthy And Hearty
                Maddy's Vegetables              Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                tbs  olive oil
  1                cup  onion, finely diced
  2             cloves  garlic, minced
  1                cup  bulgur wheat
  2                     tomatoes, skinned, diced
  1 3/4           cups  chicken broth or water
     1/2            ts  salt or to taste
     1/2           cup  edam or mozzarella cheese, shredded
                        parmesan cheese, grated to garnish

Place oil in saucepan and heat
Stir in onion and cook for 5 minutes, until onion is translucent
Stir in garlic and bulgur wheat and cook for 2 minutes
Stir in tomato, broth and salt and bring to a boil
Lower the heat and simmer for 20 minutes until the liquid is absorbed
Stir in edam cheese
Garnish individual portions with parmesan cheese
