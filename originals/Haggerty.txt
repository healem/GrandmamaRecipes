                                 Haggerty

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:45
Categories    : Ethnic                          Potatoes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3                     medium potatoes -- peeled
  1                     onion
  2        tablespoons  bacon fat
     3/4           cup  cheddar cheese -- shredded
                        salt -- to taste
                        black pepper -- to taste

Cut potatoes into paper thin slices and pat dry in a towel. Slice onion
very thin. Heat half of bacon fat in a heavy frying pan and fill pan with
alternate layers of potatoes and onion, and cheese, finishing with
potatoes. Sprinkle each layer with salt and pepper. dot the top layer of
potatoes with remainder of bacon fat. Cook over moderate heat until
potatoes are almost tender. Turn them carefully onto a platter, slip it,
top side down, back into the pan and continue cooking until done.  To
serve, cut into wedges.
