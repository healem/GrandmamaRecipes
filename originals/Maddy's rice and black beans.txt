                       Maddy's rice and black beans

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Main Meals              Maddy's Vegetables
                Main Dishes                     Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  toasted sesame oil
     1/8           cup  brown sugar
     1/8           cup  soy sauce
  2             cloves  garlic, minced
     1/2           cup  unsalted nuts
  3               cups  cooked arborio rice (1 cup dry)
     1/2           cup  green vegetable, cut into small pieces
     1/2           cup  black beans, cooked
  2              green  onions, thinly sliced
  2                     carrots, shredded
  1           teaspoon  crushed red pepper

Combine sesame oil, brown sugar and soy sauce in a skillet or wok and stir
until well mixed
Add minced garlic and nuts
Saute on medim heat until the sugar has melted.  Take care not to scorch
the sugar

Add cooked rice
Stir mixture until each kernel is coated with the sauce
Add green vegetable, black beans, green onions and shredded carrots to
rice and heat throughout

Add crushed red pepper and mix thoroughly with rice before serving
