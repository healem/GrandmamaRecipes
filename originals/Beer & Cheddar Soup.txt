                           Beer & Cheddar Soup

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:40
Categories    : Soups & Stews

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              quart  beer
  1              quart  chicken stock
  1                cup  heavy cream
  3                     egg yolks
  1                cup  cheddar cheese, shredded
  1           teaspoon  paprika

     Bring beer and chicken stock to a boil over moderate heat. Simmer for
25 minutes. Beat egg yolks until thick and pale yellow. Beat in cream.
Slowly add a little of the hot beer to egg mixture, stirring constantly to
prevent curdling. Add egg mixture and cheese to remaining soup. Heat for 5
minutes, but do not allow the soup to boil.
