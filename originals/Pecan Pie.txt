                                Pecan Pie

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:20
Categories    : American                        Desserts
                Pies & Pastry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3              whole  eggs -- beaten
  1                cup  dark corn syrup
  1                cup  sugar
  1           teaspoon  vanilla extract
     1/4      teaspoon  cinnamon
  1                cup  pecans
  1                     9 inch pie crust -- unbaked

     Combine all ingredients and pour into unbaked pie shell.  Bake at 325
degrees for one hour.

Cholesterol; 315mg Sodium.  Exchanges: 1 Grain(Starch); 1/2 Lean Meat; 4
