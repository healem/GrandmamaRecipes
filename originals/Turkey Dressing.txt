                             Turkey Dressing

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Poultry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2         quarts  soft bread crumbs -- toasted
  1 1/2         quarts  cornbread stuffing
  2          teaspoons  salt
  1                cup  cooked rice
  2        tablespoons  fresh parsley -- minced
  2                     eggs -- beaten
     1/2      teaspoon  thyme
  1                     onion -- chopped
  1           teaspoon  white pepper
     3/4           cup  butter
  2          teaspoons  sage
     1/2      teaspoon  baking powder
  2              whole  celery stalks -- chopped
  2        tablespoons  bacon drippings
                        turkey giblets stock

Combine salt, sage, thyme, baking powder, pepper, bread crumbs, and eggs.
Melt butter in skillet on low heat; add onion, celery and parsley. Simmer
for about 15 minutes. Mix together the butter and crumb mixtures. Soften
to your taste with stock made from stewed giblets, strengthened with bacon
drippings.
