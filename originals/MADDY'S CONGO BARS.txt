                            MADDY'S CONGO BARS

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 1     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cookies                         Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2 2/3           cups  flour
  1 1/2             ts  baking powder
     1/2            ts  salt
  1                cup  butter
  2               cups  brown sugar
  3              whole  large eggs
  1                cup  chopped nuts
  1          12 oz pkg  chocolate morsels
  1           6 oz pkg  butterscotch morsels
  1           6 oz pkg  white chocolate morsels
  1              tblsp  vanilla

Combine flour, baking powder and salt in medium bowl
Beat butter, brown sugar and vanilla in large bowl until creamy
beat in eggs
Gradually beat in flour mixture
Stir in half of the morsels and nuts
Spread in greased 15 x 10 x 1 pan
Sprinkle with remaining morsels
Cook at 350' for 25 to 30 minutes
Cool and cut
