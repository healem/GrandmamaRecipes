                  Maddy's creamy baked four-cheese pasta

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 1     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Main Meals              Maddy's Pasta Dishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        Topping
  1 1/2           cups  bread crumbs
     1/4           cup  (1/2 oz) grated parmesan cheese
     1/4      teaspoon  salt
     1/8      teaspoon  pepper
                        	
                        Pasta & Cheese
  4                 oz  fontina cheese, shredded (1 cup)
  3                 oz  gorgonzola cheese, crumbled (3/4 cup)
     1/2           cup  pecorino Romano cheese, grated (1 oz)
     1/4           cup  parmesan cheese, grated (1/2 oz)
  1                 lb  penne pasta
  1         tablespoon  plus 1/4 teaspon salt
  2          teaspoons  unsalted butter
  2          teaspoons  all purpose flour
  1 1/2           cups  heavy cream
     1/4      teaspoon  black pepper

Topping
Mix bread crumbs, chese, salt and pepper in a bowl, set aside

Pasta & Cheese
Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 500

Combine cheeses in large bowl, set aside

Bring 4 quarts of water to rolling boil in stockpot
Add pasta and 1 tbls salt to boiling water
Stir to separate

Melt butter in small saucepan over medium-low heat
Whisk flour into butter until no lumps remain (30 seconds)
Gradually whisk in cream
Increase heat to medium and bring to boil, stirring occasionally
Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer 1 minute to ensure flour cooks
Stir in remaining 1/4 ts salt and pepper
Cover cream mixture to keep hot and set aside

When pasta is very al dente (when bitten into, pasta should be opaque and
sligtly underdone at very center) drain about 5 seconds, leaving pasta
slightly wet.

Add pasta to bowl with cheeses
Immediately pour cream mixture over pasta, cover bowl with foil or plate
and let stand 3 minutes
Uncover bowl and stir with rubber spatula, scraping bottom of bowl, until
cheeses are melted and mixture is thoroughly combined

Transfer pasta to 13x9 inch baking dish
Sprinkle evenly with reserved bread crumbs, pressing down lightly
Bake until topping is golden bown (7 minutes)
Serve immediately
