                         Maddy's Mushroom Chowder

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Maddy's Soups
                Soups & Stews                   Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  onion, chopped
  1               tbls  olive oil
  2                lbs  mushrooms, fresh, sliced
  2              whole  potatoes, diced
  1 1/2           cups  celery, finely chopped
  2               cups  carrots, diced
  3               cups  chicken broth
  1                cup  cream
     1/4           cup  parmesan cheese, grated
                        salt & pepper to taste

Saute onions in oil until tender
Add vegetables, salt & pepper
cover and simmer for about 20 minutes until vegetables are tender
Add chicken stock and simmer another 10 minutes
Stir in milk and parmesan cheese
Cover and heat (do not boil)
Garnish each serving with additional parmesan cheese
