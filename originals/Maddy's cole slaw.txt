                            Maddy's cole slaw

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Maddy's Salads                  Maddy's Vegetables
                Salads                          Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     3/4           cup  mayonnaise
     1/4           cup  Dijon mustard
     1/4           cup  light brown sugar, packed
  3        tablespoons  apple cider vinegar
  2        tablespoons  buttermilk
  4          teaspoons  celery seeds
  1           teaspoon  salt
     1/4      teaspoon  black pepper, freshly ground
     1/8      teaspoon  cayenne
  3               cups  green cabbage, shredded (about 1/2 head)
  3               cups  red cabbage, shredded (about 1/2 head)
  1                     green pepper, finely diced
  1              large  carrot, peeled and shredded
     1/2           cup  yellow onion, grated
     1/4           cup  fresh parsley, minced

In a bowl, combine the mayonnaise, mustard, sugar, vinegar, buttermilk,
celery seeds, salt, pepper and cayenne
Whisk well to dissolve the sugar

In a large bowl, combine the remaining ingredients
Toss with the dressing until evenly coated
Adjust seasoning to taste
Place in refrigerator, covered, to chill slightly before serving
