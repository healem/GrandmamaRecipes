                   Maddy's Scallop - Asparagus Stir Fry

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Casseroles                      Maddy's Main Meals

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     3/4           cup  chicken broth
  1               Tbls  cornstarch
  1                tsp  soy sauce
                        	
     3/4           cup  scallops, cut in half
  1                     clove garlic, minced
  1                cup  mushrooms, sliced
  1                tsp  sesame oil
                        	
     3/4            lb  cooked asparagus, 2 inch pieces
  1                cup  cherry tomatoes, halved
  3                     green onions, sliced diagonally

Combine chicken broth, cornstarch and soy and set aside
Place sesame oil in saute pan
Add scallops, mushrooms, garlic and liquids above
Stir fry for 4 minutes until sauce is thick
Add asparagus, tomato halves andonion
Heat through and serve
