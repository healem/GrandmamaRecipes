                              Pineapple Pie

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :3:00
Categories    : Desserts                        Fruit
                Maddy's Desserts                Pies & Pastry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                can  pineapple -- chunked & drained
  1                cup  nuts -- chopped
  1                can  eagle condensed milk
     1/4           cup  lemon juice
  1              large  Cool Whip Lite®
     1/2           jar  cherries -- drained & halved
  2                     pie shells

Place pineapple, nuts, milk and lemon juice in bowl
Mix together
Add cool whip and cherries
Mix together
Spoon into pie shells
Refrigerate for 2 hours
Serve
