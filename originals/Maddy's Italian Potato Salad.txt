                       Maddy's Italian Potato Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Salads                  Potatoes
                Salads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  15             large  red potatoes (about 5 lbs)
                        salt & pepper
     1/2           cup  parsley, freshly chopped
     1/2           cup  green onions, chopped
  3              large  garlic cloves, thinly sliced
     1/2           tsp  dry mustard
  1         scant tbls  sugar
  1               tbls  worcestershire sauce
  1                cup  olive oil
     1/2           cup  tarragon vinegar

Place potatoes in a large pot and cover with water
Bring to boil
Boil until tender, approximately 30-40 minutes
Drain and let stand until cool enough to handle
Peel potatoes if desired
Cut into 1-inch pieces
Place cut potatoes in a large bowl
Sprnkle parsley and green onins over potatoes
Make the sauce by mixing the rest of the ingredients together in another
bowl
Pour sauce over potatoes
Stir well
Let stand all day, or at least for 4 hours, stirring every hour
Do not refrigerate
Serve at room temperature
