              MADDY'S MEATLOAF WITH MUSTARD AND DRIED FRUIT

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 2     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Beef                            Main Dishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  10                OZ  LEAN GROUND BEEF
     1/2           cup  BREADCRUMBS
     1/4           cup  CHOPPED MIXED DRIED FRUITS
                        OR APRICOTS
  1              large  EGG
  3               TBLS  CHOPPED ONION
  2               TBLS  DIJON MUSTARD
  1                 TS  SAGE
     1/2            TS  SALT
     1/4            TS  PEPPER
  2               TBLS  APRICOT PRESERVES

PREHEAT OVEN TO 425
COMBINE MEAT, BREADCRUMBS, FRUITS, EGG, ONION, 1 TBLS
MUSTARD, SAGE, SALT AND PEPPER IN LARGE BOWL
MIX THOROUGHLY
SHAPE MIXTURE INTO 2 OVAL LOAVES, EACH 1 INCH THICK
PLACE ON SMALL BAKING SHEET
BAKE LOAVES 10 MINUTES
MIX PRESERVES AND REMAINING 1 TBLS MUSTARD IN SMALL BOWL
BRUSH PRESERVED MIXTURE GENEROUSLY OVER LOAVES
CONTINUE TO BAKE UNTIL LOAVES ARE COOKED THROUGH, ABOUT 8 MINUTES
