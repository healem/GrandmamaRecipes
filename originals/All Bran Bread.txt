                              All Bran Bread

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1            package  active dry yeast
     1/2           cup  warm water
     1/2           cup  All-Bran® Cereal
     1/3           cup  sugar
     1/2           cup  vegetable shortening
     1/4      teaspoon  salt
     1/2           cup  boiling water
  1                     egg
  3               cups  all-purpose flour

     Dissolve yeast in warm water.  Mix All-Bran cereal, sugar,
shortening, and salt.  Pour boiling water over bran mixture.  Let cool,
then add yeast.
      Add egg and half of flour.  Beat well.  Add remaining flour.  Cover
and let rise until double in bulk.  Punch down.  Form into 2 small 
loaves, place in buttered pans, and let rise again until double.  Just
before baking, brush tops of loaves with melted butter.  Bake at 350
degrees for 30-35 mins.

NOTES : After first rising, dough may be punched down and put in
        the refrigerator overnight, or it may be mixed and put
        right in refrigerator and both risings done second day. 
        If refrigerated, allow to stand at room temperature for a
        couple of hours before proceeding.
