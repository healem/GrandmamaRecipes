                            Potatoes Duchesse

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Potatoes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4              whole  potatoes
     1/2           cup  hot milk
  2        tablespoons  butter -- melted
  1           teaspoon  salt
     1/4      teaspoon  black pepper
     1/4      teaspoon  nutmeg
  2                     eggs
  2                     egg yolks

Boil potatoes until tender.  Mash with 1/4 cup milk, butter, salt, pepper,
and nutmeg.  Beat eggs and egg yolks together, then beat in remaining
milk.  Beat egg mixture into potatoes until fluffy.  Serve at once.
