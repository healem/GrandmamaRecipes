                   MADDY'S HOT AND GOOEY CHOCOLATE CAKE

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:30
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  vegetable oil -- plus 1 tablespoon
  4              whole  eggs
  1                cup  sugar
  2          teaspoons  baking powder
     1/8      teaspoon  baking soda
     1/2           cup  milk
  1           teaspoon  vanilla
  1                cup  cake flour
     1/2           cup  cocoa
                        	
               TOPPING  	
                        Whipped cream
                        hot fudge sauce (see Maddy's hot
                        fudge sauce)

Preheat oven to 350
Grease and flour 9-inch tube pan or bundt pan, shaking out excess
Beat oil, eggs and sugar using medium speed of electric mixer until
mixture is light and lemon colored
Resift flour with cocoa, baking powder and soda
Add to egg mixture with milk and vanilla
Beat at low speed until blended
Pour into prepared pan
Bake until toothpick inserted in center of cake comes out clean, about 1
hour
Cool 10 to 20 minutes in pan
invert onto platter

Serve warm with hot fidge sauce and whipped cream
