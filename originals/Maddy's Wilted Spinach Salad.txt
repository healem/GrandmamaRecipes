                       Maddy's Wilted Spinach Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Maddy's Salads                  Salads
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2            lbs  spinach, tough stems trimmed -- (about 6 cups)
  1              large  yellow bell pepper, cut into strips
  1                cup  tomato, diced
  1                 lb  white mushrooms, trimmed and sliced -- (about 5 cups)
     1/2           cup  low-fat Italian dressing
  3                     scallions -- (about 1/2 cup)

In a large salad bowl, combine spinach, bell pepper, tomatoes and 2 cups
mushrooms

Coat a large nonstick skillet with cooking spray
Add the remaining muchrooms 
Cook over medium high heat, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes or until
soft
Stir in the dressing and scallions

Spoon dresing over the spinach and toss gently until evenly coated
Serve hot
