           Maddy's Pesto Cheese Blossoms (Paula on foodtv.com)

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 15    Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  8                 oz  sliced provolone cheese
                        CREAM CHEESE LAYER
  16                oz  cream cheese, room temperature
  20                    pistachios, shelled
  1              clove  garlic
  2               tbls  olive oil
                        PESTO LAYER
  2             cloves  garlic
     1/2           cup  fresh basil leaves
     1/2           cup  fresh parsley leaves
     1/4           tsp  salt
     1/8           tsp  freshly ground pepper
     1/2           cup  pine nuts
  2               tbls  olive oil
                        TOMATO LAYER
  3                 oz  oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes
                        small amount of oil from tomatoes

Line a medium bowl with plastic wrap, leaving enough overhang to cover the
top
Reserving 3 slices of provolone, line the bottom and sides of the bowl
with remaining provolone, overlappng slices

For the cream cheese layer:  process all ingredients in a food processor
until blended
Scrape mixture into a bowl and set aside

For the pesto layer:  process basil, parsley, pine nuts, and 1 garlic
clove in the food processor until blended
Dissolve the salt and pepper in the olive oil and mix well
With the machine running, add the olive oil in a fine stream
Scrape this mixture into a second bowl and set aside

For the tomato layer:  drain the tomatoes, reserving the oil
Puree the tomatoes with a small amount of reserved oil in a food processor

Spread 1/3 of the cream cheese mixture over the cheese slices lining the
bowl
Spread the pesto mixture over the cream cheese layer
Spread the second 1/3 of the cream cheese mixture over the pesto
Spread the sun-dried tomato mixture over the cream cheese
Spread the remaining cream cheese mixture over the tomato layer
Cover with the remaining 3 slices of provolone

Bring the edges of the plastic wrap together over the top and secure with
a twist tie
Place back in the bowl
Place in refrigerator overnight or until firm
Remove the plastic wrap and invert the mold onto a serving platter
Serve with party crackers

This will keep in the refrigerator for up to 3 months
