                          Chilled Zucchini Soup

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :6:00
Categories    : Soups & Stews                   Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                     onion -- chopped
     1/2           cup  butter
  2               cups  chicken broth
  1              large  zucchini -- cut in chunks
  1              bunch  celery tops -- 1 bunch
  2                     bay leaves
     1/4           cup  chives -- chopped
  3        tablespoons  fresh parsley -- chopped
  1           teaspoon  salt
  2               cups  buttermilk
  1               dash  cayenne
                        curry to taste

     Saute onion in butter.  Add broth, zucchini, celery, bay leaves,
chives, and parsley.  Simmer until very soft.  Remove bay leaves, and
adding small amounts to blender, blend until smooth.  Before serving add
buttermilk, salt, cayenne, and curry to taste.  Chill and serve cold.
