                          MADDY'S ARTICHOKE DIP

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                can  ARTICHOKES
  1                cup  MAYONNAISE
  1                cup  PARMESAN CHEESE -- GRATED
  1                cup  MOZZARELLA CHEESE, -- SHREDDED

DRAIN ARTICHOKES, CHOP
MIX ARTICHOKES, MAYONNAISE AND PARMESAN CHEESE
PLACE IN BAKING DISH
BAKE AT 350 UNTIL BROWN AND BUBBLY

SPRINKLE MOZZARELLA CHEESE OVER TOP
BAKE UNTIL BROWN AND BUBBLY
