                                 Brownies

Recipe By     :Madeline
Serving Size  : 25    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cookies                         Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4             ounces  unsweetened chocolate
  1              stick  butter
  4              whole  eggs
     1/4      teaspoon  salt
  1           teaspoon  vanilla
  2               cups  sugar
  1                cup  flour

Melt chocolate and butter together
Let cool

Beat eggs, salt and vanilla until frothy
Add sugar slowly, beating constantly
Stir chocolate mixture into egg mixture slightly
Add flour, stirring thoroughly

Grease 9 x 13 baking dish
Pour mixture into dish
Bake at 350 for 25 minutes
