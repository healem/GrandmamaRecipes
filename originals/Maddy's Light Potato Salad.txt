                        Maddy's Light Potato Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Salads                  Potatoes
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2            lbs  potatoes, peeled, cooked and chunked
     1/3           cup  plain non-fat yogurt
  1         tablespoon  canola or olive oil
  1         tablespoon  vinegar (should be white or will change
                        color of dressing)
  1         tablespoon  mayonnaise
  2        tablespoons  onion, chopped
  1           teaspoon  sugar
     1/2         clove  garlic, minced
                        salt & pepper to taste

Mix oil, vinegar and flavorings together in a bowl
Whisk in the yogurt and mayo

Add hot, cooked potatoes to bowl and mix gently

Place in refrigerator to cool or serve warm
