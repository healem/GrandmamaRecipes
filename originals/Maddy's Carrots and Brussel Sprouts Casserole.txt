              Maddy's Carrots and Brussel Sprouts Casserole

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables              Vegetables
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  sliced carrots -- 4 medium
  2               cups  brussel sprouts -- 1/2 lb
     1/2           cup  sour cream
  2              tblsp  finely chopped onion
  2              tblsp  prepared horseradish
     1/4           tsp  salt
  1               dash  pepper
     1/2           cup  fresh bread crumbs
  1              tblsp  melted butter
  2              tblsp  chopped parsley

heat oven to 350 degrees
in saucepan, cook carrots in boiling water for 10 minutes
add brussel sprouts and cook another 10 minutes or until crisp-tender
drain, reserving 1/4 cup cooking liquid

Place vegetables and reserved liquid in 1 1/2 quart baking dish
toss lightly to combine

in small bowl, combine sour cream, onion, horseradish, salt and pepper.
spoon over vegetables
mix bread crumbs and butter
sprinkle over vegetables and sauce

bake uncovered for 20 minutes or until thoroughly heated
garnish with parsley
