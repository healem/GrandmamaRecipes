                          Seafood Bouillabaisse

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:15
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Seafood
                Soups & Stews

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2        tablespoons  butter
  2        tablespoons  olive oil
     1/4           cup  flour
  1                cup  onion -- chopped
     1/2           cup  celery -- chopped
  1              clove  garlic -- minced
  4               cups  fish stock or clam juice
  12            ounces  tomatoes -- canned
     1/2           cup  white wine
  2        tablespoons  parsley
  1         tablespoon  lemon juice
  1              whole  bay leaf
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/2      teaspoon  cayenne pepper
     1/2      teaspoon  saffron
  2             pounds  fish fillets -- cut in 1-1/2" chunk
  1               pint  oysters -- reserve liquid
     1/2         pound  shrimp -- peeled and deveined
  1                cup  crab meat

Melt butter with olive oil in a large pot. Sprinkle with flour. Add onion,
celery, garlic; stir and cook until vegetables are tender. Stir and cook
to light brown. Stir fish stock or clam juice with reserved oyster liquid
slowly. Add remaining ingredients except fish. Simmer on low heat for 1
hour. Add fish, cook 10 minutes. Add shrimp, oysters, and crab meat, and
cook 5 more minutes.

Vegetable; 1/2 Fruit; 1 1/2 Fat.

Serving Ideas : Serve over rice.
