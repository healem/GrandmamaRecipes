                 MADDY'S SOUR CREAM POPPER DIPPING SAUCE

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 1     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers
                Maddy's Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2           cups  SOUR CREAM
  1                TSP  MINCED GARLIC
  1                TSP  MINCED GINGER
  1                TSP  MINCED CILANTRO
  1              TBLSP  SUGAR
     1/4           TSP  SALT
                        JUICE FROM 1/2 LIME
     1/4           TSP  PEPPER

MIX ALL INGREDIENTS TOGETHER IN BOWL
LEAVE FOR AT LEAST AN HOUR TO BLEND FLAVORS
DIP HOT CHEESE STUFFED JALEPINOS
