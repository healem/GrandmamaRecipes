                   Maddy's tomato and mozzarella salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Maddy's Salads
                Salads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  SMALL ROUND MOZZARELLA CHEESES
  1                cup  TOMATOES, CHOPPED
  1                cup  BLACK OLIVES
  1                cup  GREEN OLIVES
     1/2           cup  CELERY, CHOPPED
     1/4           cup  RED ONION, CHOPPED
  1                     LEMON, JUICED
     1/4           cup  OLIVE OIL
                        SALT & PEPPER TO TASTE

MIX LEMON JUICE AND OLIVE OIL TOGETHER
PLACE ALL OTHER INGREDIENTS IN A BOWL
POUR JUICE AND OIL OVER TOP
MIX TOGETHER
SALT AND PEPPER TO TASTE
