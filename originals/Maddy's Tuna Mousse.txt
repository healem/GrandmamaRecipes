                           Maddy's Tuna Mousse

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Appetizers                      Cheese
                Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  7                 oz  tuna, packed in olive oil, drained
  1 1/3           tbls  butter, at room temp
  1               tbls  heavy cream
  2                tsp  balsamic vinegar
  2                tsp  soy sauce
  2                tsp  fresh lemon juice
                        salt & pepper

Put the tuna in a food processor
Pulse to break up the fish
With the machine running, add the butter, cream, balsamic vinegar and soy
sauce
Blend until smooth
Stop machine
Scrape down sides of the bowl
Add the lemon juice and pulse again
Season, to taste with salt & pepper
Blend again

Serve at room temperature, or cover nd refrigerate for up to 4 days
If refrigerated, return the mousse to room temp before serving

Makes 3/4 cup
