                    Maddy's Chocolate Almond Meringues

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Cookies                         Desserts
                Diabetic                        Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  sugar, divided - or splenda
     1/4           cup  almonds, ground
  1               tbls  cocoa, unsweetened
  1                 ts  cornstarch
  2                     egg whites
     1/8            ts  cream of tartar
     1/2            ts  vanilla (or 1/4 ts almond extract)

Preheat oven to 250
Spray a foil-lined baking sheet with non-stick spray
In small bowl, mix 2 tbls sugar with almonds, cocoa and cornstarch
In bowl, beat egg whites with electric mixer until frothy
Add cream of tartar and beat on high speed until soft peaks form
Gradually add flavoring and remaining sugar
Beat until stiff and shiny
Gently fold cocoa mixture into meringue

Drop cookie mixture from teaspoon onto baking sheet to form small mounds
Leave 2 inches between each mound
Bake at 250 for 40 minutes
Cookies should be dry and slightly browned
Cool completely
Store in a tightly covered container

Makes about 2 dozen
