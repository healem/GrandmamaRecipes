                        Fluffy Chocolate Frosting

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:20
Categories    : Fillings & Frostings

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  butter -- softened
     1/2      teaspoon  vanilla
  1              pound  powdered sugar
     1/2           cup  cocoa
  2                     egg white -- unbeaten
     1/4           cup  boiling water

     Cream butter with vanilla and 1/2 cup of powdered sugar.  Add cocoa
and egg whites.  Continue beating, adding powdered sugar and boiling water
alternately until desired consistency is reached.
