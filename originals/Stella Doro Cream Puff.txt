                          Stella Doro Cream Puff

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :0:20
Categories    : Cookies                         Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1            package  stella doro cookies -- split
  8             ounces  cream cheese -- whipped
  8             ounces  Cool Whip Lite®
  3        tablespoons  sugar
  1          large can  crushed pineapple in water

Drain pineapple
Mix cream cheese, pineapple, cool whip and sugar
Spread mixture on cookie half, and place second cookie half on top
