                   MADDY'S CURRIED MACARONI AND CHEESE

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Casseroles                      Maddy's Pasta Dishes
                Main Dishes                     Pasta
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2           TBLS  CORNSTARCH
  1 1/2             TS  DRY MUSTARD
  1 1/2             TS  CURRY POWDER
  2 1/4           cups  WHOLE MILK
  6               TBLS  BUTTER
  2 1/4           cups  GRATED SHARP CHEDDAR CHEESE (10 OZ)
  8                 OZ  SMALL ELBOW MACARONI
  2 1/2           cups  BREADCRUMBS

PLACE 6 CUPS WATER IN SAUCE PAN OVER MEDIUM HIGH HEAT
WHEN BOILING, ADD ELBOW MACARONI AND COOK UNTIL TENDER
PREHEAT OVEN TO 350
BUTTER 8X8X2 GLASS BAKING DISH
COMBINE CORNSTARCH, 1 TS DRY MUSTARD AND 1 TS CURRY POWDER IN HEAVY LARGE
SAUCEPAN
GRADUALLY WHISK IN MILK
ADD 2 TBLS BUTTER
WHISK OVER MEDIUM HIGH HEAT UNTIL SAUCE THICKENS AND BOILS, ABOUT 1 MINUTE
REMOVE FROM HEAT
ADD CHEESE AND WHISK UNTIL SMOOTH
MIX IN MACARONI
SEASON WITH SALT AND PEPPER
TRANSFER TO PREPARED BAKING DISH
MELT REMAINING 4 TBLS BUTTER IN HEAVY LARGE SKILLET OVER MEDIUM HEAT
MIX IN REMAINING 1/2 TS DRY MUSTARD AND 1/2 TS CURRY POWDER
ADD BREADCRUMBS AND STIR UNTIL CRUMBS ARE CRISP AND GOLDEN, ABOUT 8
MINUTES
SPRINKLE CRUMB MIXTURE OVER MACARONI AND CHEESE
BAKE UNTIL WARMED THROUGH AND BUBBLING AT EDGES, ABOUT 30 MINUTES
SERVE HOT
