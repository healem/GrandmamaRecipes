         Maddy's Great Brownies - Low Carb (Barefoot Contessa &)

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey (Low carb on foodtv)
Serving Size  : 20    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cookies                         Maddy's Desserts
                Maddy's Low Carb

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 lb  unsalted butter
  1                 lb  semi sweet chocolate chips
  2               cups  semi sweet chocolate chips
  6                 oz  unsweetened chocolate
  6            x large  eggs
  3               tbls  instant coffee
  3               tbls  vanilla extract
  2 1/4           cups  splenda
  1                cup  soy flour
     1/4           cup  soy flour
  1               tbls  baking powder
  1                tsp  salt
  3               cups  macadamia nuts

Preheat oven to 350
Grease and flour 13 x 18 by 1 1/2 inch sheet pan

Melt together 1 lb butter, 1 lb chocolate chips and 6 oz unsweetened
chocolate in microwave (or top of double boiler)
Cool slightly

Stir together the eggs, instant coffee, vanilla and splenda in large bowl
Add the warm chocolate mixture and cool to room temperature

Stir together 1 cup soy flour, baking powder and salt.  Add to cooled
chocolate mixture

Combine macadamia nuts and 2 cups chocolate chips in bowl.  Coat with 1/4
cup soy flour
Add nuts and chocolate chips to chocolate mixture

Pour into prepared pan

Bake for 30 minutes or until tester just comes out clean
Halfway through the baking, rap the pan against the oven shelf to allow
air to escape from between the pan and the brownie dough.
DO NOT OVERBAKE!

Cool thoroughly 
Refrigerate well and cut into squares
