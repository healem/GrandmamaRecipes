                 Maddy's pasta with tomato and brie sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Pasta Dishes            Pasta

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2 1/2           tbls  pine nuts
  1                 lb  pasta of choice
  12                oz  triple cream brie cheese
  1                cup  extra virgin olive oil
  2               cups  hand chopped roma tomatoes
  3                tsp  chopped garlic
  2               tbls  hand chopped parsley or cilantro
  6              large  fresh basil leaves, roughly chopped
  1                cup  parmesan or romano cheese

Bring a large pot of slightly salted water to a boil.
Add the pasta and cook until it is al dente
Keep cooked pasta covered and warm in a slight bit of its water
In a small, ungreased frying pan, toast pine nuts until they are golden
brown.  DO NOT BURN OR THE DISH WILL BE BITTER
Cut the outer rind from the brie
Throw away the rind and set the cheese aside
Heat a large frying pan to medium-high and add all of the olive oil
After 30 seconds or so add the tomato, garlic, parsley/cilantro and basil
Let this saute for about 1 minute
HAVE ALL INGREDIENTS READY AND NEXT TO THE STOVE BECAUSE THIS SAUCE WORKS
FAST
Add the brie and toasted pine nuts
Toss or slowly stir over low heat for two minutes or until the cheese is
completely incorporated into the sauce
You should have a medium thick sauce with a beautifully rich pink color
with splashes of green
Drain the remaining water off the pasta in a colander
Place a nice portion of pasta on a decorative plate
Ladle a fair amount of brie sauce over the top.
Cover with cheese
