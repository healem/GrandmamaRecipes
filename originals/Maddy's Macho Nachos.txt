                           Maddy's Macho Nachos

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:20
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                can  refried beans
  1             lg bag  white corn tortilla chips
  1             medium  onion, chopped
  1                cup  shredded pepperjack cheese
  1                     jalapeno pepper, sliced crosswise
  1                 lb  ground meat of your choice
  1                cup  shredded cheddar cheese
  2               tbls  chili powder
                        	
                        TOPPING
  1                cup  sour cream
  1                cup  green onions, chopped
  1                     tomato, diced
  1                     jalapeno pepper, sliced crosswise
  1                     avacado, peeled, sliced thin

Preheat oven to 350
In a small saucepan, over low heat, cook refried beans until they are
loose enough to spoon onto nachos
In a small fry pan, cook ground meat and chili powder until done
Drain fat from pan and dispose of it

REFRIED BEANS AND GROUND MEAT SHOULD BE HOT BEFORE ASSEMBLING NACHOS

On a large ovenproof platter, spread out a layer of tortilla chips
Quickly put a tsp of refried beans on each tottilla chip
Sprinkle with some onions, jack cheese and jalapeno slices
Spoon on ground meat 
Top with cheddar cheese
Repeat this process until ingredients are used up
Place platter in the oven to bake until cheese has melted, about 5 to 10
minutes
Remove from oven and place on a trivet or heatproof surface.
Top nachos with sour cream, green onions, diced tomato, avacado slices and
jalapeno slices
Serve hot
