                            Blueberry Streusel

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:45
Categories    : Breakfast                       Cakes
                Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  butter
     1/2           cup  sugar
  1                     egg
  2               cups  all-purpose flour
  2          teaspoons  double-acting baking powder
     1/2      teaspoon  baking soda
     1/4      teaspoon  salt
     1/2           cup  buttermilk
  2               cups  fresh blueberries
     1/2           cup  flour
  1           teaspoon  cinnamon
     1/2      teaspoon  ground nutmeg
     1/2           cup  sugar
     1/4           cup  butter -- cold

     Cream butter and sugar. Beat in egg. Sift dry ingredients together
and add, alternately with milk, to creamed mixture. Pour into well-greased
9 x 13 pan. Top with blueberries. Mix sugar, flour, and spices. Cut butter
into tablespoons and blend into dry ingredients with a pastry blender. 
Mixture should be crumbly. Sprinkle over blueberries. Bake at 350 degrees
for 35 minutes.
