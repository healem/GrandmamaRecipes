                             Vermicelli Salad

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :6:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Pasta
                Salads                          Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  12            ounces  vermicelli -- one package
     1/2           cup  sweet pickle juice
  6             ounces  French salad dressing
  2                     garlic cloves -- mashed
  1         tablespoon  poppy seeds
  1           teaspoon  celery seed
  1           teaspoon  caraway seed
     1/4      teaspoon  cayenne pepper
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/2         pound  diced celery
  1              bunch  green onions -- chopped
     1/4           cup  fresh parsley -- chopped

Break and cook vermicelli according to package directions. Mix pickle
juice, French dressing, and seasonings and pour over warm vermicelli. 
Chill. One hour before serving, add celery, green onions and parsley.
