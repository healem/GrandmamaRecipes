                       Maddy's Instant Pesto Torta

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        Vegetable spray
  4             pieces  sun dried tomatoes
  4             leaves  fresh basil
  3          6 oz tubs  soft garlic and herb cheese -- Alouette
     1/4           cup  sun dried tomato tapenade
     1/4           cup  prepared basil pesto

Line small shallow dish or bowl with plastic wrap or waxed paper
Coat wrap with vegetable spray
Arrange a pattern in bottom of the dish using the sun dried tomatoes and
basil
Add 1 1/2 tubs garlic and herb cheese (about 1/2 lb) to the dish and
smooth over
Layer the sun dried tomato spread and the pesto
Spread the remaining chese on top of the pesto
Place a serving plate over the bowl and keep in the refrigerator for at
least an hour
Invert the bowl
Hold onto the edges of the waxed paper or wrap to release the cheese
Remove waxed paper or plastic wrap

Arrange the sliced bread along one side of the torta
Arrange carrots and tomatoes on the opposite side
