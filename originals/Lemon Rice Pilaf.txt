                             Lemon Rice Pilaf

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:20
Categories    : Rice

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2        tablespoons  butter
     1/2           cup  chopped onions
  1                cup  long-grain brown rice
  2        tablespoons  orzo
  2 1/4           cups  chicken stock
     1/4           cup  fresh lemon juice
  1         tablespoon  lemon zest -- grated

     In a heavy saucepan, saute onion in butter until soft.  Stir in rice
and orzo and cook two minutes, stirring to coat rice and orzo with butter.
 Add chicken stock and lemon juice.  Bring to a boil, cover, turn heat
low, and simmer 50 minutes.  Fluff up rice with a fork,  turn into a
heated serving bowl, and garnish with grated lemon zest.
