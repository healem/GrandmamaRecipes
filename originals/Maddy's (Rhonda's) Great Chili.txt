                      Maddy's (Rhonda's) Great Chili

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Luncheon
                Maddy's Main Meals              Soups & Stews

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2            lbs  hamburger
                        garlic to taste
  1              small  onion chopped
  1                can  progresso red kidney beans
  1             packet  el paso chili seasoning
  1                can  pastene italian crushed tomatoes 28 oz
     1/8           tsp  crushed red pepper flakes
  1                pkg  taco cheese (finely grated)

brown hamburger with onion and garlic
drain all grease
add crushed tomatoes and add 1/2 can water
add chili seasoning package and stir
add kidney beans and bring to a simmer
add crushed red pepper flakes and simmer (the longer it simmers, the
hotter it gets) probably about 1/2 hour or longer

Serve and top with taco cheese

calories from fat); 23g Protein; 58g Carbohydrate; trace Dietary Fiber;
66mg Cholesterol; 732mg Sodium.  Exchanges: 3 1/2 Grain(Starch); 1 1/2
Lean Meat; 3 1/2 Fat.
