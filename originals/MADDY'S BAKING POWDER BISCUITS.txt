                      MADDY'S BAKING POWDER BISCUITS

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  FLOUR
  1              TBLES  SUGAR
  1              TBLES  CORNSTARCH
  2                TSP  BAKING POWDER
     1/2           TSP  BAKING SODA
     1/2           TSP  SALT
     1/4            LB  BUTTER, CUT IN PIECES (1/2 STICK)
     3/4           cup  BUTTERMILK

PREHEAT OVEN TO 450
SIFT FIRST 6 INGREDIENTS INTO LARGE BOWL
ADD BUTTER AND RUB IN WITH FINGERTIPS UNTIL MIXTURE RESENBLES COARSE MEAL
ADD BUTTERMILK AND STIR UNTIL DOUGH FORMS
TURN DOUGH OUT ONTO FLOURED SURFACE
KNEAD UNTIL SMOOTH, ABOUT 8 TURNS
ROLL OUT DOUGH TO 1 INCH THICKNESS
USING 2 INCH DIAMETER BISCUIT CUTTER, CUT OUT BISCUITS
GATHER SCRAPS
ROLL OUT TO 1 INCH THICKNESS AND CUT OUT ADDITIONAL BISCUITS
TRANSFER BISCUITS TO LARGE UNGREASED BAKING SHEET
BAKE UNTIL GOLDEN BROWN, ABOUT 12 MINUTES
