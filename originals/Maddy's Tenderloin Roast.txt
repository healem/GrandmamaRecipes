                         Maddy's Tenderloin Roast

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:30
Categories    : Beef                            Maddy's Main Meals

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                     Tenderloin
  3        tablespoons  evoo
  4             cloves  garlic, fresh, sliced
  4          teaspoons  kosher salt
  1         tablespoon  pepper
                        	
                        Garlic-Herb Butter
  1         tablespoon  sage, chopped fresh
  1         tablespoon  parsley, chopped fresh
  1 1/2      teaspoons  thyme, chopped fresh
  1              small  garlic clove, minced
                        salt & pepper to taste
  4        tablespoons  butter, softened

Dry-age beef:  place paper towels on baking sheet.  place wire racks over
baking sheet.  Pat the roast dry with paper towels, then set roast on rack
and put it on the lowest shelf in refrigerator, uncovered, for 1 to 3 days

Bring tenderloin to room temperature
Preheat oven to 450


Cut slits in meat, and tuck in pieces of garlic
Rub evoo over the whole tenderloin
Mix salt and pepper, and season all surfaces of tenderloin
Cut tenderloin in half

Place in baking sheet, with room to spare so that heat can surround each
piece
Place in lower-middle position of oven
Cook at 450 for 20 minutes
Remove from oven and let sit for 20 minutes (leaving the oven on)
Cook at 450 for 20 minutes
Remove from oven and let sit for at least 20 minutes before slicing
Medium rare: internal temperature is 130 to 135

While meat roasts, mix together ingredients for garlic-herb butter and set
aside until needed
After roast has rested for the last time, spread butter evenly over
surface of roast
With sharp carving knife, cut into 1/4 inch slices
