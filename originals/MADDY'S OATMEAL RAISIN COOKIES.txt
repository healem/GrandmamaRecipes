                      MADDY'S OATMEAL RAISIN COOKIES

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 25    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cookies                         Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  shortening
  1                cup  dark brown sugar
  1                cup  white sugar
  2              whole  eggs
     1/2           cup  molasses
  2          teaspoons  vanilla
  2               cups  flour
     1/2      teaspoon  baking powder
  1           teaspoon  salt
  1 1/2      teaspoons  cinnamon
  1                cup  raisins
  1                cup  nuts -- chopped
  2               cups  rolled oats
  1           teaspoon  baking soda

Cream shortening and sugars
Add eggs, molasses and vanilla
Sift together flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt and cinnamon
Add to shortening mixture
Fold in oats, raisins and nuts
Let stand a few minutes or overnight
Drop by teaspoonfuls on ungreased cookie sheets
Press down slightly
Bake at 325 to 350 for 10 to 12 minutes, until lightly browned
Allow to stand barely half a miinute before removing from cookie sheet
