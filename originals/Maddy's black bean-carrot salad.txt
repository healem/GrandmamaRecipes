                     Maddy's black bean-carrot salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 7     Preparation Time :1:40
Categories    : Beans                           Healthy And Hearty
                Maddy's Salads                  Salads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 LB  BLACK BEANS, cooked
  1                cup  CARROTS, SLIVERED
     1/2           cup  RED ONION, CHOPPED
     1/2           cup  CELERY, CHOPPED
  1                cup  TOMATOES, CHOPPED
  1                cup  GREEN PEPPER, CHOPPED
  1              whole  LEMON, JUICED
     1/4           cup  OLIVE OIL
                        SALT & PEPPER

PLACE ALL vegetables IN BOWL
MIX LEMON JUICE AND OLIVE OIL AND POUR OVER VEGETABLES
MIX TOGETHER
ADD SALT & PEPPER TO TASTE
