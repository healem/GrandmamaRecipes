                              Anchovy Toast

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Appetizers                      Food Processor Cooking

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  14                    anchovies -- (2 tins)
  3                     garlic cloves -- chopped
     1/8      teaspoon  mustard powder
  1         tablespoon  chopped onions
  2        tablespoons  capers
  1                cup  olive oil
  3        tablespoons  Parmesan cheese -- grated
  1                     loaf Italian bread
  1                     sweet red pepper -- thinly sliced

     With a mortar and pestle, mash anchovies, garlic, mustard, onions,
and capers.  Turn into a mixing bowl and vigorously beat in olive oil  (or
combine all ingredients in food processor or blender and blend until
anchovies are mashed). Slice loaf of Italian bread lengthwise.   Spread
anchovy mixture on top of one slice of bread.  Lay slices of red pepper
over anchovy mixture.  Sprinkle with parmesan cheese.  Top with second
slice of bread.  Wrap in foil, place in preheated 400 oven for 10 minutes.
 Slice and serve.
