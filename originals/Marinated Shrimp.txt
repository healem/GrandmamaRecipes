                             Marinated Shrimp

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:20
Categories    : Appetizers                      Seafood

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2             pounds  fresh shrimp -- cooked & peeled
  1         tablespoon  onion -- grated
  1              clove  garlic -- minced
  3        tablespoons  fresh parsley -- minced
  1               pint  Italian salad dressing
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/4      teaspoon  black pepper
  4        tablespoons  paprika
  2               jars  artichoke hearts -- drained
  2               jars  button mushrooms
  1          small can  black olives -- pitted and drained
  1              whole  green pepper -- minced

Place shrimp, artichoke hearts, mushrooms, and olives in a deep bowl. Mix
remaining ingredients thoroughly and pour over shrimp mixture. Cover and
marinate in refrigerator for 24 hours before serving, stirring
occasionally.
