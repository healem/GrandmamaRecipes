                           Maddy's Tartar Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Sauces                  Salad Dressings
                Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  Mayonnaise
     1/2           cup  Gourmet Sour Gherkins, minced
  1                 ts  Capers, minced
  1                tbs  Parsley, fresh, minced
  1                tbs  Red onion, minced
  1              clove  garlic, minced
  1                 ts  Dijon mustard
  2                tbs  Tarragon vinegar
  1              pinch  Cayene
                        Salt, to taste

Place all ingredients in bowl.
Blend well
Set aside for an hour to incorporate flavors

Will keep about a week in the refrigerator
