                           Giant Spinach Shells

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:45
Categories    : Pasta                           Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  24             whole  giant shells
  1         tablespoon  vegetable oil
  1                     small onion -- minced
  2        tablespoons  butter -- melted
  10            ounces  frozen spinach -- chopped
  1              pound  cottage cheese
  1                     egg -- beaten
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/4      teaspoon  black pepper
  30            ounces  tomato sauce
  1                cup  mozzarella cheese -- shredded

     Cook shells in boiling, salted water for 9 minutes, drain. Gently
toss shells in oil and reserve. Thaw spinach and drain, squeezing out all
water.
     Saute onions in butter until soft. Stir in spinach. Remove from heat
and empty into a bowl. Add cottage cheese, egg, onion, salt and pepper.
Fill each shell with mixture. Pour half of sauce into a 9 x 13 inch baking
dish. Arrange stuffed shells in dish and cover with remaining sauce. Bake
at 350 degrees for 30 minutes. Sprinkle with Mozzarella cheese and return
to oven until cheese is melted and bubbly, about 3 minutes.
