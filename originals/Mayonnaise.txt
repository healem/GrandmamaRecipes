                                Mayonnaise

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 16    Preparation Time :0:10
Categories    : Salad Dressings

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2                     egg yolks
  2               cups  olive oil
                        juice of one lemon or more

Beat egg yolks until thick.  Very slowly add olive oil and lemon juice. 
Beat at high speed.  When oil is absorbed, season to taste.
