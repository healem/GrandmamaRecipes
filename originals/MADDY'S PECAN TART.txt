                            MADDY'S PECAN TART

Recipe By     :MADELINE M. HEALEY
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Cookies                         Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              stick  BUTTER
  3                 OZ  CREAM CHEESE
  1                cup  FLOUR
                        	
               FILLING  	
     3/4           cup  LIGHT KARO
     1/2           cup  BROWN SUGAR
  1                TSP  VANILLA
  1              whole  EGG
     1/2           cup  PECANS -- CHOPPED

SOFTEN BUTTER AND CREAM CHEESE TOGETHER
ADD FLOUR
DIVIDE DOUGH IN 1/2 AND MAKE 12 BALLS FROM EACH 1/2
YOU WILL NEED TWO TINY MUFFIN PANS
BUTTER THEM WELL
PLACE ONE BALL IN EACH WELL
USING YOUR THUMB AND MIDDLE FINGER, SPREAD THE DOUGH OVER THE BOTTOM AND
UP THE SIDES OF THE WELL

BLEND ALL INGREDIENTS FOR FILLING AND SPOON INTO UNCOOKED DOUGH LINED
MUFFIN TINS

BAKE AT 350 FOR 15 MINUTES OR UNTIL GOLDEN
