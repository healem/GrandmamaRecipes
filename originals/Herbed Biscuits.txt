                             Herbed Biscuits

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  all-purpose flour
  1         tablespoon  double-acting baking powder
     1/2      teaspoon  baking soda
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/2           cup  butter -- cut into 8 pieces
     1/2           cup  buttermilk
  1                     egg -- lightly beaten
     1/2           cup  fresh herbs -- * chopped

     Sift flour with baking powder, baking soda, and salt.  Cut in butter
with a pastry blender until mixture resembles cornmeal.  Stir buttermilk
into beaten egg.  Pour egg mixture into flour mixture and stir until
mixture holds together in a ball.
     Turn out onto lightly floured surface.  Knead in herbs.  Pat or roll
to 1/2" thickness.  Cut into two inch rounds with a biscuit cutter.  Place
bisquits on lightly greased baking sheet, at least one inch apart.  Bake
at 400 degrees for 15 minutes.
