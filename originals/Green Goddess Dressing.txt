                          Green Goddess Dressing

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Salad Dressings

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1         tablespoon  cider vinegar
     1/2           cup  mayonnaise
     1/2           cup  sour cream
     3/4           cup  buttermilk
  2              clove  garlic -- minced
     1/2           cup  fresh chives -- chopped
     1/4           cup  fresh parsley -- chopped
  1         tablespoon  fresh tarragon -- chopped
  1         tablespoon  fresh basil -- chopped
  2        tablespoons  capers

     Beat together vinegar, mayonnaise, sour cream, and half of
buttermilk.  Stir in herbs, salt and pepper, if desired, and remaining
buttermilk.

                                    - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - 

Per Serving (excluding unknown items): 142 Calories; 15g Fat (89.5%
calories from fat); 2g Protein; 2g Carbohydrate; trace Dietary Fiber; 12mg
Cholesterol; 131mg Sodium.  Exchanges: 0 Vegetable; 0 Non-Fat Milk; 1 1/2
Fat; 0 Other Carbohydrates.

NOTES : May be made quickly in blender: begin by chopping herbs,
        adding vinegar as herbs are chopped.  Blend in mayonnaise
        and sour cream.  Slowly add buttermilk while blending,
        until desired consistency is reached.
