                        Maddy's Zucchini Casserole

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:30
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  6               cups  zucchini, diced
                        olive oil
  1                 lg  onion, chopped
  4               tbls  butter
     1/2           cup  sour cream
  1                tsp  house seasoning
  1                cup  cheddar cheese, grated
  1                cup  butter crackers, crushed -- Ritz
                        	
                        HOUSE SEASONING
  1                cup  salt
     1/4           cup  black pepper
     1/4           cup  garlic powder

Preheat oven to 350

Saute sqush in a little olive oil over medium low het until broken down,
bout 155 to 20 minutes
Line a colandar with a clean tea towel
Place cooked zucchini in lined colandar
Squeeze excess moisture from squash
Set aside

In medium skillet, saute onion in butter for 5 minutes
Remove from pan and mix all ingredients together except crcker crummbs
Pour mixture into a buttered casserole dish and top with cracker crumbs
Bake for 25 to 30 minutes

HOUSE SEASONING
Mix ingredients together and store in an airtight container for up to 6
months 
Yield:  1 1/2 cups
