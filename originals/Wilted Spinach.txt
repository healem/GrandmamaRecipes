                              Wilted Spinach

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Vegetables                      Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4             slices  bacon
  1              small  onion -- chopped
  1                     carrot -- shredded
  1         tablespoon  flour
     1/4           cup  vinegar
  2        tablespoons  sugar
  1               dash  pepper
     1/2           cup  water
  1              pound  spinach
  1                     hard-boiled egg -- chopped

Fry bacon until crisp.  Remove and crumble.  Saute onion and carrot in
bacon fat until just tender.  Sprinkle with flour and cook one minute.  
Add vinegar, sugar, dash of pepper, and water.  Bring to boil.  Pour over
spinach and top with crumbled bacon and chopped egg.
