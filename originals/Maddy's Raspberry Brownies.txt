                        Maddy's Raspberry Brownies

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 25    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cookies                         Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2            squares  unsweetened chocolate
     1/2           cup  butter -- unsalted
  1                cup  sugar -- granulated
  2              whole  eggs -- slightly beaten
     1/2           cup  flour -- sifted
  1                cup  nuts (of your choice) -- chopped
  1         tablespoon  raspberry liqueur
     1/2           cup  raspberry jam

Melt chocolate, and allow to cool
Allow butter to soften
Cream butter and sugar
Add beaten eggs and blend well
Gradually stir in cooled chocolate
Add flour and stir to mix
Stir in nuts
Add raspberry liqueur
Put batter in 9-inch glass pie pan
Lightly beat the raspberry jam
Drop teaspoons of jam at random on top of the batter
Swirl the jam with a fork to incorporate it into the batter
Bake for 25 minutes at 350 until toothpick inserted comes out almost clean
DO NOT OVERBAKE
