                               Cream Sauce

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:10
Categories    : Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3        tablespoons  butter
  3        tablespoons  all-purpose flour
  1                cup  milk -- scalded
                        salt
                        black pepper

     Melt butter over low heat.  Whisk flour in gradually.  Cook one
minute, whisking constantly.  Slowly add milk, whisking constantly to
prevent lumps.  Continue to cook and stir until sauce just boils.  Season
with salt and pepper to taste.

                                    - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - 

Per Serving (excluding unknown items): 135 Calories; 11g Fat (70.6%
calories from fat); 3g Protein; 7g Carbohydrate; trace Dietary Fiber; 32mg
Cholesterol; 118mg Sodium.  Exchanges: 1/2 Grain(Starch); 0 Non-Fat Milk;
2 Fat.

NOTES : A basic sauce that can be "dressed up" in endless ways.
        It can be made thicker or thinner by varying the amount of
        milk used.  May be made with fats other than butter and/or
        liquids other than milk.
