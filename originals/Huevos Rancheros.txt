                             Huevos Rancheros

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Breakfast                       Eggs
                Luncheon                        Mexican

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  6                     eggs
  2        tablespoons  onion -- finely chopped
  1              clove  garlic -- minced
  2        tablespoons  green pepper -- finely chopped
  1         tablespoon  picante sauce
  2        tablespoons  mild salsa
  3        tablespoons  butter

      Melt butter in large frying pan.  Cook onions, garlic, and pepper
until soft.  Beat eggs until light and pour into frying pan.  Cook over
very low heat, stirring constantly.  When eggs begin to harden, add
picante sauce and salsa.  Continue to cook and stir until set.
      Serve immediately.

Serving Ideas : Serve with flour tortillas,grated cheese,additional salsa.

NOTES : A great, easy brunch dish, especially with
        Grapefruit-Avocado Salad, and Tequila Sunrises.
