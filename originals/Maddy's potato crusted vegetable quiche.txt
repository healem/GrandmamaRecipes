                 Maddy's potato crusted vegetable quiche

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Main Meals              Maddy's Vegetables
                Main Dishes                     Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  water
     1/4           cup  sun dried tomatoes
     1/2            lb  asparagus or broccoli florets
     1/2        medium  onion, chopped
  2             cloves  garlic, sliced
  2        tablespoons  olive oil
  1                     carrot, cut in small cubes
  5                     mushrooms, sliced
  1         tablespoon  basil, fresh chopped (or 1 ts dried)
  1         tablespoon  parsley, fresh, chopped
     1/8      teaspoon  chili flakes
     1/8      teaspoon  nutmeg, freshly grated
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
  1           teaspoon  black pepper, freshly ground
  3              small  red potatoes, washed and thinly sliced
     1/2           cup  grated cheese, pepper jack or swiss
     1/4           cup  milk
     1/2           cup  sour cream, low fat
  6                     eggs
  1             medium  tomato, sliced (seeds squeezed out)
  3        tablespoons  parmesan cheese, freshly grated

Preheat oven to 375
Boil the water, pour over the sun dried tomatoes and allow to soak for
about 15 minutes, until they become soft and plump
Strain off any remaining liquid, and coursely chop

Cut off 1 inch of the coarse ends of the asparagus stalks and discard
Cut the remaining stalks into 6 pieces or chop coarsely.  If you are using
broccoli, cut into florets
Blanch by boiling them in a medium pot of water for 2 minutes 
Vegetable should be bright green, but firm to the touch
Rinse in cold water and drain in colander

Saute onions and garlic in the olive oil over low heat, until the onions
are transparent, about 10 minutes
Add the carrots, mushrooms, basil, parsley, chili flakes, nutmeg, saltand
pepper
Continue to cook for 5 more minutes.  Remove from heat

Lightly grease the bottom and sides of a 9 inch pie pan
Line the bottom with the potato slices, overlapping them lightly
Whisk together the cheese, milk, sun-dried tomatoes, sour cream and eggs
in a large bowl
Mix in the sauteed vegetables, coating everything with the cheese, milk
and egg liquid
Pou into the potato lined pie pan
Arrange the tomato slices on top and sprinkle with parmesan cheese

Bake for 1 hour, covering after 45 minutes if top browns
Completely baked quiche should be very firm
Let cool 15 minutes before slicing and serving
Leftovers can be wrapped and kept in the refrigerator for up to 3 days
