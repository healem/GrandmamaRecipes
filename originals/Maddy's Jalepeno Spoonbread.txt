                       Maddy's Jalepeno Spoonbread

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Breakfast
                Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               tbls  butter
  4                     eggs, separated
  1                cup  cornmeal, stone-ground
  3               cups  whole milk
  1                tsp  salt
                 pinch  sugar
     1/2           cup  red onion, minced
  1                     jalepeno pepper, seeded & minced

Preheat oven to 350
Grease a deep (3 inch) 1 1/2  quart baking or souffle dish or cast iron
skillet
In a large bowl, whip the egg whites until medium stiff peaks form
Set aside
In a mixing bowl, whisk togethr the cornmeal and 1 cup milk
In a medium saucepan, heat the remaining milk until steaming hot
Add the cornmeal mixture to the saucepan and cook, whisking constantly,
over medium low heat until the mixture thickens (you'll begin to see the
bottom of the pan as you whisk) about 10 minutes
Remove from the heat
Stir in the salt, sugar and butter
Add the yolks one at a time, whisking to combine after each addition
Add the red onion and jalepeno pepper, if using, and stir to combine
Gently fold in the egg whites
Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish
Bake until the spoon bread is puffed, with a golden brown crust, and a
toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean, about 1 hour
Serve with soft butter
