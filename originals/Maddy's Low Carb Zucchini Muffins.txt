                    Maddy's Low Carb Zucchini Muffins

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Maddy's Breads
                Maddy's Low Carb

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4               tbls  wheat bran
  1 1/2           cups  soy flour, plus 2 tbls
     1/2           cup  small diced zucchini, skin on
  3                     eggs
     3/4           cup  heavy cream
     1/2           cup  club soda
     1/3           cup  splenda
  1 1/2            tsp  baking powder

Preheat oven to 375
Grease a 12 cup muffin pan with non-stick spray or butter
In a small bowl, mix wheat bran and 2 tbls soy flour 
Distribute evenly in each cup
In a large bowl, whisk together the remaining ingredients until completely
blended
Fill each cup with mix, leaving room for them to rise
Bake for 20-25 minutes or until tops are ligtly browned and a wooden
toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
Cool before taking out of the pan
