                             Cream Puff Cake

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:45
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  butter
  1                cup  water
  1                cup  all-purpose flour
  4                     eggs
  8             ounces  cream cheese -- softened
  4               cups  milk
  3           packages  instant vanilla pudding mix -- 3 ounces each
  1               pint  cream -- whipped
                        chocolate syrup

     Melt butter in water.  Add flour and cook, stirring constantly, until
it forms a ball.  Turn into a bowl and beat in eggs one at a time.  Spread
flat on a 9" x 13" greased pan.  Bake at 400 degrees for 35-40 minutes. 
Cool.  Mix together cream cheese and milk and beat until smooth.  Add
instant pudding, one package at a time.  Spread over cooled shell.  Top
with whipped cream and drizzle chocolate syrup on top.  Chill at least one
hour before serving.
