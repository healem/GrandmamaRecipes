                              Cheese Rarebit

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:25
Categories    : Appetizers                      Cheese
                Luncheon

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              pound  sharp cheddar cheese -- shredded
  2        tablespoons  butter
  1                cup  beer
  1                     egg -- beaten
  1           teaspoon  dry mustard
  1           teaspoon  paprika

Melt butter in top of double boiler and add cheese. Place over hot water
and stir until cheese begins to melt. Add beer gradually, stirring
constantly until cheese is melted and mixture is smooth. Stir in egg,
mustard, and paprika.
