                          Butternut Squash Soup

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:30
Categories    : Soups & Stews                   Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              large  butternut squash
  6                     whole cloves
  2                     cinnamon sticks
  6                     allspice berries
     1/4           cup  maple syrup
  1               pint  light cream
  1                cup  heavy cream
                        ground cinnamon
                        ground nutmeg

     Peel, seed, and cube squash.  In a large soup pot, place squash with
enough water to cover.  Tie cloves, cinnamon sticks, allspice berries in a
cheesecloth bag and add bag to pot.  Bring to a boil and simmer over low
heat for 40 minutes.  Remove spice bag.  Drain squash, reserving liquid. 
Return cooked squash to pot and mash.  For a smoother soup, squash may be
pureed in blender or food processor before returning to pot. Stir in
creams and maple syrup. 
      Cook over low heat for 15 minutes, stirring often to prevent
sticking.   Add additional spices, if desired.  Thin with reserved liquid,
if necessary.  Cook 15 minutes more.  Serve hot.
