                        Maddy's French Onion Soup

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Maddy's Soups                   Soups & Stews
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2         pounds  yellow onions -- thinly sliced
  2        tablespoons  butter
  1         tablespoon  olive oil
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
                        	
  2        tablespoons  flour
  2             quarts  chicken broth -- heated to boiling
     1/2           cup  white wine
  6                     croutons
  1                cup  Swiss cheese -- grated
     1/2           cup  Parmesan cheese -- grated
  2                     beef bouillon cubes

Melt butter and oil in 5 quart saucepan over moderate heat.
Add onions, sprinkle with salt
Cook uncovered over low heat, stirring occasionally, until onions are
golden brown (approsimately 2 hours)
Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 2 minutes.
Add hot broth, wine and bouillion cubes.
Simmer partially covered for 30-40 minutes, skimming occasionally.
Pour into individual ovenproof soup crocks. 
Sprinkle with grated cheese.  Broil until cheese melts.
Rub large croutons with garlic and toast.  Serve separately.
