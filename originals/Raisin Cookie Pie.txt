                            Raisin Cookie Pie

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:45
Categories    : Pies & Pastry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                     9 inch pie crust
  1 1/8           cups  flour
     1/2      teaspoon  baking soda
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/2           cup  butter
     1/3           cup  sugar
     1/3           cup  brown sugar, packed
     1/2      teaspoon  vanilla extract
  1                     egg
     1/2           cup  raisins
     1/2           cup  walnuts -- chopped
                        vanilla ice cream

     Sift together flour, baking soda, and salt; set aside.  In a large
bowl cream butter with sugars.  Beat in egg and vanilla.  Gradually add
flour mixture and mix well.  Stir in raisins and nuts.  Spread into pie
crust.  Bake for 20 to 25 minutes at 375 degrees.  Cool and serve with ice
cream.
