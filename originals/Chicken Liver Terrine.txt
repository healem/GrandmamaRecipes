                          Chicken Liver Terrine

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 1     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Appetizers                      Poultry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  butter
  1              small  onion -- chopped
  1              clove  garlic -- minced
     1/4           cup  diced celery
  1              pound  chicken livers
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
  1              pinch  cayenne pepper
     1/2      teaspoon  ground nutmeg
     1/4      teaspoon  ground cloves
  2        tablespoons  Cognac

     Melt 1/4 cup butter in a skillet.  Saute onions, garlic, and celery
until just soft.  Add livers and cook until done.  Drain liquid and cool.
     In a blender or food processor, puree liver mixture.  Add seasonings
and remaining butter.  Process until well combined.  Pack into 1-1/2 pint
terrine and chill thoroughly.
