              Maddy's Chocolate Stuffed Peanut Butter Scones

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Breakfast
                Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  flour
     1/2           cup  dark brown sugar
  2 1/2           tsps  baking powder
     1/4           tsp  salt
     1/4           cup  cold butter
     3/4           cup  creamy peanut butter
     1/4           cup  milk
  2              large  eggs
  2               tsps  vanilla
  2               cups  semi sweet chocolate chips

Preheat oven to 375

In large bowl, stir together baking powder, flour, sugar and salt 
Using pastry blender, cut butter into dry ingredients until the mixture
resembles coarse crumbs.  
Add chocolate chips

In small bowl, stir together peanut butter, milk, eggs, and vanilla

Add wet mixture to dry mixture and knead until combined

Pat dough out to 3/4 inch thickness.  
Cut scones to whatever shape desired

Place scones about 2 inches apart on an ungreased baking sheet.
Bake for 17 to 19 minutes or until lightly browned.

Cool on a wire rack for 5 minutes.  
Serve warm or cool completely and store in an airtight container.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
-------------------
VARIATIONS:
Make the dough as above, omitting bittersweet chocolate, substituting 1/2
cup unsalted whole peanuts for the chopped peanuts and keanding in 3/4
cups of semisweet chocolate chips at the same time.  Pat the dough into a
9 inch diameter circle on a baking sheet.
With a serrated knife, cut into 8 wedges.  Bake for 20 to 22 minutes, or
until a cake tester or toothpick inserted into the center of a scone comes
out clean.  Cool as above and recut into wedges.
