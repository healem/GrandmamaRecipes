                             Oatmeal Cookies

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 72    Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Cookies

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  butter -- melted
  2               cups  all-purpose flour
  1           teaspoon  cinnamon
  1           teaspoon  baking soda
  1           teaspoon  double-acting baking powder
  1                cup  brown sugar
  1                cup  sugar
  2                     eggs -- beaten slightly
  3               cups  rolled oats
     1/2           cup  seedless raisins

Melt butter and cool to room temperature
Sift flour, cinnamon, baking soda, and baking powder together
Add sugars to butter and stir well
Add eggs, sifted dry ingredients, oats, and raisins
Drop by spoonfuls onto well greased cookie sheets
Bake at 350 degrees for 10 minutes
Makes about 72 cookies.
