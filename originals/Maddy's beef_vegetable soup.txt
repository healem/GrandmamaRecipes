                       Maddy's beef_vegetable soup

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :3:00
Categories    : Maddy's Soups                   Soups & Stews

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2              tblsp  vegetable oil
  3                lbs  boneless chuck roast
  4                qts  cold water
  28                oz  can diced tomatoes
  1 1/2           cups  chopped onion
  3              tblsp  dried parsley
  2              tblsp  beef bouillon granules
  1              tblsp  dried italian seasoning
  1              tblsp  house seasoning (recipe follows)
  1              tblsp  seasoned salt
  1              tblsp  worcestershire sauce
  1                tsp  celery salt
  1                tsp  garlic powder
     1/2           tsp  black pepper
  2                     bay leaves
  1                cup  thinly sliced carrots
  1                cup  diced celery
  1                cup  sliced green beans, fresh
  1                cup  frozen black-eyed peas
  1                cup  cut okfa, fresh
  1                cup  diced potatoes
  1                cup  sliced asparagus
     1/2           cup  uncooked elbow macaroni
                        chopped fresh parsley
                        	
                        HOUSE SEASONING
  1                cup  salt
     1/4           cup  black pepper
     1/4           cup  garlic powder

heat oil in large skillet over medim heat
place roast in skillet and cook until browned on both sides, about 5
minutes per side
remove roast from skillet and cut it into 1 1/2 to 2 inch cubes
discard the fat
place beef cubes in large stockpot

Add water, tomatoes, onions, dried parsley, beef bouillon, dried italian
seasoning, house seasoning, seasoned salt, worcestershire sauce, celery
salt, garlic powder, black pepper and bay leaves
bring to a boil over high heat
cover pot, reduce heat so that the liquid simmers, and cook for 1 1/2 to 2
hours or until meat is very tender
Add remaining vegetables and macaroni and return the soup to a boil,
stirring to distribute ingredients
reduce heat and simmer for 45 minutes
just before serving, season with salt and pepper and add fresh chopped
parsley
(to remove excess fat from the surface of the soup, swirl a lettuce leaf
around the surface--it will pick up  lot of the fat)
