                             Lemon Tea Bread

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3        tablespoons  butter -- melted
  1                cup  sugar
  2                     eggs
  1 1/2           cups  flour
  1           teaspoon  salt
     3/4           cup  fresh lemon juice
  2        tablespoons  lemon zest -- grated
     1/2           cup  powdered sugar -- sifted

     Stir sugar into melted butter. Beat in eggs.  Stir in flour and salt
until just blended.   Add 1/2 cup lemon juice and one tablespoon lemon
zest.  Pour batter into a greased and floured loaf pan.  Bake at 350 for
50 minutes.  Remove from oven and cool ten minutes. Mix powdered sugar
with remaining 1/4 cup lemon juice and one tablespoon zest.  Remove bread
from pan and pour glaze over top.
