                            Red Cabbage Relish

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :24:00
Categories    : Pickles & Relishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2             quarts  red cabbage -- shredded
  3              whole  red onions -- sliced
  3              whole  large bell peppers -- chopped
  1               pint  vinegar
  2               cups  sugar
  1         tablespoon  salt
  1         tablespoon  celery seed
  1         tablespoon  yellow mustard
     1/3    tablespoon  turmeric

Combine red cabbage, onions and bell peppers in a large ceramic bowl.
Bring vinegar to a boil, remove from heat, and stir in remaining
ingredients. Pour over cabbage mixture. Cover and chill overnight.
