                         Louisiana Grilled Shrimp

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :4:30
Categories    : Luncheon                        Main Dishes
                Seafood

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              pound  large shrimp
     1/2           cup  olive oil
     1/2           cup  fresh lemon juice
  1           teaspoon  Cajun seasoning
  1         tablespoon  Worcestershire sauce
     1/4           cup  butter -- melted
                  dash  Tabasco sauce
  2                     large bell peppers

     Place unpeeled shrimp in 2 quart casserole.  Mix together olive oil,
lemon juice, Cajun seasoning, and Worcestershire sauce.  Drizzle over
shrimp and marinate for at least 4 hours.  Remove from marinade and skewer
with chunks of pepper between shrimp.  Mix butter and Tabasco sauce and
brush over shrimp.  Grill over hot coals until browned.
