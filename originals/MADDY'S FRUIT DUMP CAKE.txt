                         MADDY'S FRUIT DUMP CAKE

Recipe By     :MADELINE M. HEALEY
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts
                Fruit                           Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              large  jar of cherry pie filling
  1              small  can/jar crushed pineapple with juice
  1                box  white or yellow cake mix
  1              stick  butter

in 9 x 13 ungreased baking dish, pour in cherry pie filling evenly over
bottom
place undrained pineapple over cherry filling
sprinkle cake mix over top
cut butter in little pieces and place evenly over top
bake in 350 degree oven for 30 to 40 minutes

use other fruits if desired, I.E., apple pie filling and applesauce,
blueberry pie filling, etc

sprinkle chopped nuts over top if desired
