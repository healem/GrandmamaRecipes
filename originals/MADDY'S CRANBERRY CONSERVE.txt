                        MADDY'S CRANBERRY CONSERVE

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Fruit                           Maddy's Sauces
                Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4               cups  CRANBERRIES
  1              TBLSP  ORANGE PEEL -- GRATED
  2              whole  ORANGES -- PEELED, CHOPPED
                        	
  1 1/4           cups  JUICE -- APPLE/CRANBERRY
  1                cup  PECANS -- CHOPPED
  1                cup  SUGAR
  2              whole  APPLES -- PEELED, SLICED

IN LARGE SAUCEPAN, COMBINE CRANBERRIES, ORANGE PEEL,
ORANGES,APPLES AND JUICE
COVER AND SIMMER OVER MEDIUM HEAT UNTIL CRANBERRIES ARE SOFT
ADD PECANS AND SUGAR
STIR WELL
SIMMER, UNCOVERED 10-15 MINUTES, STIRRING OFTEN
COOL
SPOON INTO COVERED CONTAINER
REFRIGERATE
SERVE AS A RELISH WITH POULTRY OR PORK, OR SPREAD ON BISCUITS OR ROLLS
YIELDS 3 PINTS
