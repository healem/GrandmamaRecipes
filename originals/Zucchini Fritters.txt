                            Zucchini Fritters

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Luncheon                        Vegetables
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4             medium  zucchini
  1         tablespoon  salt
     1/2           cup  flour
  1                     egg
  1           teaspoon  baking powder
     1/4           cup  milk
  1         tablespoon  onion -- grated

Wash and trim zucchini, but do not peel.  Grate and sprinkle with salt.
Drain in a colander for 30 minutes. Squeeze a handful at a time to remove
as much moisture as possible.  Mix remaining ingredients together.  When
batter is smooth stir in zucchini.  Drop by spoonfuls into oil heated to
350 degrees.

Serving Ideas : Serve with lemon wedges.
