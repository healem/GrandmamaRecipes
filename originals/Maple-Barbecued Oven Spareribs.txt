                      Maple-Barbecued Oven Spareribs

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:45
Categories    : Main Dishes                     Pork & Ham

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2           cups  maple syrup
  2        tablespoons  chili sauce
  2        tablespoons  cider vinegar
  1 1/2    tablespoons  onion -- chopped
  1         tablespoon  Worcestershire sauce
  1           teaspoon  salt
     1/2      teaspoon  dry mustard
     1/8      teaspoon  black pepper
  2             pounds  pork spareribs

     Combine all  ingredients except spare ribs and bring to a boil. Wipe
spareribs with damp paper towels. Brush sauce on both sides of ribs. Place
in a shallow, open roasting pan and bake for 1-1/2 hours, basting
frequently with remaining sauce and turning to glaze.
