                 Maddy's Cheese ane Rosemary Breadsticls

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 24    Preparation Time :0:35
Categories    : 

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/4           cup  parmesan cheese, grated
     1/3           cup  gruyere cheese, grated
  1                tsp  rosemary leaves, chopped
  1              11 oz  pkg refrigerated breadstick dough -- Pillsbury
                        finely ground sea salt, optional

Preheat oven to 350

Line 2 heavy large baking sheets with silicone baking sheets or parchment
paper
In a food processor, chop the parmesan, gruyere and rosemary together
until coarsely chopped.
Set the cheese mixture aside
Separate the dough strips
Using a piza cutter or a large sharp knife, cut each dough strip in half
lengthwise to form thin strips
Working with one dough strip at a time, coat each one with the cheese
misture, pressing very gently
Twist each cheese covered dough strip and place onto prepared baking
sheets
Sprinkle with salt, if you sish
Bake until the breadsticks are golden brown, about 10 to 15 minutes
Transfer the warm breadsticks to a basket and serve
