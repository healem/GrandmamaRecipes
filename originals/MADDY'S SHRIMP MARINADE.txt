                         MADDY'S SHRIMP MARINADE

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 1     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Salad Dressings

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2             ts  garam masala
  2                 ts  brown sugar
  2                 ts  tumeric
  1                 ts  honey
  2             dashes  soy sauce
     1/2            ts  red pepper paste
  2             cloves  garlic -- minced
     1/2          inch  ginger root -- peeled & grated
  2               tbls  curry oil

combine all ingredients in bowl
add 25 count shrimp (shelled and deveined) to bowl
turn to coat
marinate for a couple of hours
grill shrimp and vegetables
