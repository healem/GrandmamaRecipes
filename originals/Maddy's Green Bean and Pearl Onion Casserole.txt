               Maddy's Green Bean and Pearl Onion Casserole

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Casseroles                      Maddy's Main Meals
                Maddy's Vegetables              Main Dishes
                Vegetables                      Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 lb  fresh green beans
  2               cups  pearl onion
  2               tbls  olive oil
  2               tbls  butter
  2                     garlic cloves, chopped
  1                     shallot, chopped
  2                     bay leaves
  3               cups  assorted wild mushrooms, sliced
                        salt and pepper to taste
  2               tbls  fresh thyme leaves
  2               tbls  arrowroot
  5               tbls  sour cream
  1               tbls  chicken broth paste
  2               cups  milk
  1                can  fried onions

Blanch green beans and onions in large pot of boiling water
Cook for about 2 minutes until beans have brightened in color
Remove vegetables from water with a strainer
Plunge them into an ice bath to stop cooking process
Drain, and remove skins fron onions and set aside

Coat skillet with olive oil and butter over medium heat
Sweat the garlic and shallot down until they caramelize
Add bay leaves and mushrooms
Stir to combine
Season wit salt, pepper and thyme
Sprinkle the mixture with arrowroot to tighten up
Mix sour cream, chicken stock paste and milk together
Add to mushroom mix and boil for 3 minutes to thicken

Place the beans and onions in a 2 quart casserole dish
Pour the mushroom mixture over the vegetables
Toss to make sure they are well coated
Bake in preheated 350 degree oven for 30 minutes
Top with fried onions and continue to bake 10 minutes more
