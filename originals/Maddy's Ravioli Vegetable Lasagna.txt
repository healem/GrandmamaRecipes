                    Maddy's Ravioli Vegetable Lasagna

Recipe By     :Madeline M HEaley
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Main Meals              Maddy's Pasta Dishes
                Main Dishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                bag  spinach, steamed, chopped, wrung dry
     1/2            lb  asparagus, cut in 1/2 inch pieces
     1/4           cup  onion, chopped
  1              clove  garlic, minced
  1                cup  mushrooms, sliced
  1                can  artichokes, packed in water, quartered
  2        tablespoons  olive oil
                        	
  9                 oz  ravioli, any kind, fresh
                        	
  2        tablespoons  butter
  2        tablespoons  corn starch
  1                cup  stock, vegetable or chicken
     1/3           cup  cream or half-and-half
  2 1/2           cups  cheese, grated or shredded, any kind
     1/4      teaspoon  nutmeg
                        salt and pepper

bring a pan of water to a boil
salt and add ravioli
cook 4-5 minutes
ravioli should be less than al-dente, still a bit chewy
it will continue cooking when combined with sauce and vegetables

drain spinach well by wringing it dry in a kitchen towel
Heat a medium skillet to medium heat
add olive oil and garlic and onion
saute 1 minute
sprinkle spinach into the oil
add mushrooms and asparagus pieces and cook for 2-3 minutes
add artichokes and heat through
season vegetables
Remove from heat and place vegetables in a bowl

Place skillet back on the heat and melt butter
whisk corn starch into butter and cook for 1-2 minutes
Whisk stock into mixture and let it bbble
whisk in cream and 1/2 cup cheese
season with nutmeg, salt & pepper and thicken 1-2 minutes

Drizzle a touch of olive oil onto the bottom of a medium casserole or
baking dish and brush pan to coat evenly
arrange a layer of 1/2 the ravioli in the dish
layer 1/2 vegetables over the ravioli
add a few ladles of sauce
then the rest of the vegetables,
then top casserole with the last of the ravioli
dot top with any left over sauce
cover liberally with the rest of the cheese

brown cheese 8 inches from broiler for about 5 minutes
