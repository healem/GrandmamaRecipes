                      MADDY'S GARLIC MASHED POTATOES

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  5              heads  GARLIC, SEPARATED, UNPEELED
                        COOKING SPRAY
                        	
  18                OZ  BOILING POTATOES, PEELED, CUT INTO 1 INCH -- PIECES
  6               TBLS  NON FAT MILK
                        COOKING SPRAY
     3/4           cup  FAT FREE MOZZARELLA CHEESE (3 OZ) -- GRATED
     1/2           cup  FAT FREE SOUR CREAM
     1/4           cup  GRATED FAT FREE SMOKE MOZZARELLA CHEESE
  2               TBLS  CHOPPED FRESH ITALIAN PARSLEY
     1/8            TS  GROUND NUTMEG

PREHEAT OVEN TO 400
MIX GARLIC AND COOKING SPRAY IN LARGE BAKING DISH
BAKE UNTIL GARLIC IS TENDER, ABOUT 30 MINUTES
COOL
PRESS GARLIC BETWEEN FINGERTIPS TO RELEASE CLOVES
PUREE GARLIC IN PROCESSOR

COOK POTATOES IN LARGE POT OF BOILING SALTED WATER UNTIL JUST TENDER
DRAIN,  RETURN POTATOES TO POT
MASH
ADD MILK AND COOKING SPRAY AND CONTINUE MASHING UNTIL WELL BLENDED
MIX IN GARLIC PUREE AND ALL REMAINING INGREDIENTS
SEASON TO TASTE WITH SALT AND PEPPER
TRANSFER TO 6 CUP BAKING DISH

CAN BE PREPARED 1 DAY AHEAD
COVER AND REFRIGERATE
BRING TO ROOM TEMPERATURE BEFORE CONTINUING
PREHEAT OVEN TO 400
BAKE POTATOES UNTIL HEATED THROUGH, ABOUT 20 MINUTES
SPOON ONTO PLATES AND SERVE
