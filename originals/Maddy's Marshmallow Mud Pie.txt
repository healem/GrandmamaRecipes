                       Maddy's Marshmallow Mud Pie

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Cakes                           Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        CAKE
     1/2           cup  cocoa
  1                cup  butter, melted
  4                     eggs, beaten
  1 1/2           cups  flour
  2               cups  sugar
     1/8            ts  salt
  1                 ts  vanilla
  1 1/2           cups  pecans, chopped
                        	
                        FROSTING
  16                oz  light brown sugar
     1/3           cup  cocoa
     1/2           cup  butter, softened
     1/2           cup  milk, scalded
  1                 ts  vanilla
  10 1/2            oz  miniature marshmallows

Preheat oven to 350

Grease 13 x 9 x 2 baking dish

In a large bowl, stir together the cocoa and melted butter
Add eggs, flour, sugar, salt and vanilla
Beat well 
Add nuts, stirring with spatula
Pour battter into prepared pan
Bake for 35 minutes

While cake is baking, prepare frosting
Beat brown sugar and butter
Add cocoa and beat
Stir in scalded milk and vanilla 
Beat until smooth
Set aside

Remove cake from oven
Pour marshmalows over the cake while it's hot
Pour frosting over the marshmallows
Let cool slightly before cutting into pieces
