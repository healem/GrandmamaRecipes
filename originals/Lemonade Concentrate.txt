                           Lemonade Concentrate

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 24    Preparation Time :1:15
Categories    : Beverages

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2           cups  sugar
  2 1/2           cups  water
  9              whole  lemons
                        sprigs of mint

     Combine sugar and water in a small saucepan; bring to a boil  and
boil for 5 minutes. Remove from heat, cool slightly. 
     Reserve one lemon to slice for garnish.  Grate zest of two of the
remaining lemons.  Juice eight lemons.  Combine juice and zest with warm
syrup.  Steep one hour.  Strain.
     To serve: Mix 1 part mint-citrus mixture with 2 parts water. Serve
over ice. Garnish with lemon slices and mint.
