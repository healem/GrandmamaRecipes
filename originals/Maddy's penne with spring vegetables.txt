                   Maddy's penne with spring vegetables

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Pasta Dishes            Pasta
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 lb  asparagus, ends trimmed
  1                 lb  penne pasta
     1/4           cup  evoo
  1                     leek, (3 in green left on) rinsed -- & julienned
  1                 lb  mushrooms, quartered
  2                     garlic cloves,  minced
  1             splash  dry sherry
     1/2            lb  baby spinach leaves, rinsed
  1                     tomato, chopped
  10                    oil packed sun dried tomatoes -- julienned
  1                cup  fresh basil leaves, torn
                        salt & pepper

Cook the asparagus in large pot of boiling salted water until just tender,
3-4 mnutes
Remove and rinse under cold water
Cut on the diagonal into 1 1/2 in pieces and set aside
Add the pasta to the boiling water and cook al dente, 10-12 minutes

While the pasta is cooking, heat half of the olive oil in a large skillet
or wok over medium heat
Add the leek and cook until soft, stirring about 3 minutes
Add the mushrooms and cook until slightly browned, about 5 minutes
Add the garlic and cook 1 minute
Add the sherry and cook 1 minute
Add the asparagus, spinach, tomato and sun-dried tomatoes
Season with salt & pepper

Drain the pasta and add to the vegetables
Toss in the remaining olive oil and the basil
Adjust seasonings
