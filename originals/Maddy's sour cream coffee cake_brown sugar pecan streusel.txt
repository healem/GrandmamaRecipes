        Maddy's sour cream coffee cake_brown sugar pecan streusel

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        Cake
  1 1/2         sticks  butter, softened but cool -- cut in 1/2 " cubes
  4              large  eggs
  1 1/2           cups  sour cream
  1         tablespoon  vanilla
  2 1/4           cups  all purpose flour
  1 1/4           cups  granulated sugar
  1         tablespoon  baking powder
     3/4      teaspoon  baking soda
     3/4      teaspoon  salt
                        	
                        Streusel
     3/4           cup  all purpose flour
     3/4           cup  granulated sugar
     1/3           cup  dark brown sugar, packed
  2        tablespoons  cinnamon
  2        tablespoons  butter, cold, cut in 2 pieces
  1                cup  pecans, chopped

Streusel filling
In food processor, process flour, sugar, 1/4 cup brown sugar & cinnamon
until combined (15 seconds)
Transfer 1 1/4 cups of mixture to a small bowl, adding the remaining brown
sugar
Set aside for streusel filling

Streusel topping
Add butter and pecans to food processor
pulse until nuts and butter resembel small pebble pieces (10 seconds)
Set aside for streusel topping

Cake
Place oven rack on lowest position and heat to 350
Grease 10 inch tube pan with butter

Whisk eggs, 1 cup sour cream and vanilla in medium bowl until combined
Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt in bowl of
mixer, and mix on low speed for 30 seconds to blend
Add butter and remaining 1/2 cup sour cream, mix on low speed until dry
ingredients are moistened and mixture resembles wet sand with few large
butter pieces remainning (1 1/2 minutes)
Increase speed to medium and beat until butter comes together (10 seconds)
Scrape down sides of bowl with rubber spatula
lower speed to medium-low and gradually/slowly add egg mixture 
Increase speed to medium-high and beat until batter is light and fluffy (1
minute)

Spread 2 cups batter in bottom of pan, smoothing surface
Sprinkle evenly with 3/4 cup streusel filling
Repeat
Spread remaining batter on top
Sprinkle with streusel topping

Bake until cake feels firm to touch and long toothpick inserted into
center comes out clean (50-60 minutes)
Cool cake in pan on wire rack for 30 minutes
Remove from pan and cool to room tempurature (2 hours)
