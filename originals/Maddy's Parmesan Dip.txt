                           Maddy's Parmesan Dip

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Appetizers                      Cheese
                Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2            lb  parmesan, not too dry
     1/2            lb  asiago cheese, not too dry
  2                tsp  garlic, minced
  2                tsp  dried oregno
  1                tsp  red pepper flakes
  2               tbls  green onion, chopped
  1 1/2           cups  extra virgin olive oil
  1                tsp  black pepper

Remove any rind from the cheeses
Chop cheeses into rough 1 inch chunks
Place the cheese in a food processor with garlic, oregano, and red pepper
flakes
Pulse until reduced to the size of fine pea gravel
Add the green onion, olive oil and black pepper 
Pulse again
Cover and let stand at room temp for at least 4 hours before using
