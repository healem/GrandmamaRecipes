                             Maddy's Falafel

Recipe By     :madeline m healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:25
Categories    : Ethnic                          Healthy And Hearty

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2             cloves  garlic
     1/2           cup  fresh parsley
  15                oz  can garbanzo beans, drained
  1               tbls  evoo
  1                tsp  lemon juice
  1                tsp  ground cumin
     1/2           tsp  ground criander
     1/2           tsp  salt
                  dash  pepper
                 pinch  cayenne
  1                tsp  baking powder
     1/4           cup  flour
                        canola oil for frying
  4                     pitas
     1/2          head  green leaf lettuce, chopped
  1             medium  tomato, chopped
  1             medium  cucumber, chopped
                        	
                        YGURT TAHINI SAUCE
  6                 oz  plain fat free yogurt
  2               tbls  tahini
  2                tsp  lemon juice
     1/4           tsp  salt
                 pinch  ground cumin

place garlic cloves and parsley in food processor and process until they
are finely chopped
add drained beans and pulse a few times
garbanzo beans should be chopped to a coarse texture
transfer bean mixture to a mixing bowl and add evoo, lemon juice, cumin,
coriander, salt, pepper, cayenne, baking pwoder and flour
stir to combine well
form falafel dough into 8 balls and flatten into patties
heat 1/2" of oil in a large skillet over medium high heat
fry patties in hot oil until dark golden brown on both sides, 8 to 10
minutes
drain on paper towels before serving
serve falafel patties in pita bread with yogurt-tahini sauce, lettuce,
shopped tomatoes and cucmbers

YOGURT-TAHINI SAUCE
combine all ingredients in small mixing bowl and whisk until smooth
