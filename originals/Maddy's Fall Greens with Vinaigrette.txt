                   Maddy's Fall Greens with Vinaigrette

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Maddy's Salads                  Salad Dressings

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               tbls  champagne or white wine vinegar
  2               tbls  dijon mustard
  6               tbls  extra virgin olive oil
                        salt & pepper to taste
                        	
  5                 oz  mixed fall lettuces
  6                 oz  blue cheese
     1/2           cup  mixed nuts
     1/2           cup  dried cranberries or cherries

In a small bowl, combine vinegar, mustard and salt/pepper
Whisk to blend
Add oil in slow, steady stream, whisking constantly until smooth and fully
blended
Adjust seasonings
In large bowl, toss lettuces with 2/3s of the vinaigrette
Season and add more vinaigrette if necessary
Divide greens among 4 salad bowls and tp with equal amounts of cheese,
nuts and dried fruit
Serve immediately
