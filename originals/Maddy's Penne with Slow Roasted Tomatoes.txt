                 Maddy's Penne with Slow Roasted Tomatoes

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Maddy's Pasta Dishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  10              plum  tomatoes, cored and halved lengthwise
  1                 lb  penne
     1/2           cup  parmesan cheese
     1/4           cup  fresh basil, chopped
                        olive oil, for sprinkling
                        salt & pepper to taste

In 9 x 13 baking dish, arrange tomatoes cut sides up, overlapping if
necessary
Sprinkle with oil, salt and pepper
Cover with foil and roast in 300 degree oven for 1 hour
Remove foil and continue roastiing for 1 hour more
Set tomatoes aside to cool
With paring knife, peel off skins; saving the cooking juices 
In food processor, chop tomatoes coarsely
Tip mixture into a large saucepan

Cook penne in a large pot of boiling salted water for 8 to 10 minutes or
until tender

Reheat tomatoes
Drain pasta, but don't shake colander
add a ladle of tomatoes to 4 large shallow bowls 
Divide pasta among the bowls
Top with more tomatoes, parmesan and basil
Serve
