                          MADDY'S PESTO LASAGNA

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Maddy's Pasta Dishes
                Pasta

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  PACKED FRESH BASIL LEAVES
  1                cup  GRATED FAT FREE PARMESAN
     1/2           cup  CHICKEN BROTH
     1/4           cup  PINE NUTE
  4              small  GARLIC CLOVES, HALVED
                        	
     1/4           cup  FAT FREE BUTTER SPRAY
     1/3           cup  MINCED SHALLOTS
     1/2           cup  ALL PURPOSE FLOUR
  4               cups  SKIM MILK
  1                     BAY LEAF
     1/4            TS  GROUND NUTMEG
                        	
  16                    LASAGNA NOODLES
                        	
     1/3           cup  VEGETABLE BROTH
  1 1/3           cups  GRATED FAT FREE PARMESAN

FOR PESTO:  BLEND ALL INGREDIENTS IN PROCESSOR UNTIL COARSE PUREE FORMS
SEASON WITH SALT AND PEPPER

FOR BECHAMEL SAUCE:  MELT BUTTER SPRAY IN HEAVY LARGE SAUCEPAN OVER MEDIUM
HEAT
ADD SHALLOTS
SAUTE 2 MINUTES
ADD FLOUR
WHISK 1 MINUTE
GRADUALLY WHISK IN MILK
BRING TO BOIL, WHISKING CONSTANTLY
ADD BAY LEAF
REDUCE HAT
 SIMMER UNTIL SLIGHTLY THICKENED, STIRRING OFTEN, ABOUT 5 MINUTES
MIX IN NUTMEG
SEASON WITH SALT AND PEPPER
COOL SAUCE SLIGHTLY

COOK NOODLES IN POT OF BOILING WATER UNTIL JUST TENDER BUT STILL FIRM TO
BITE
DRAIN
RINSE UNDER COOL WATER, DRAIN

SPRAY 13X9X2 INCH BAKING DISH
MIX 1 CUP PESTO AND BROTH IN SMALL BOWL (RESERVE REMAINING PESTO)
SPREAD 1/2  CUP BECHAMEL SAUCE OVER BOTTOM OF PREPARED DISH
OVERLAP 4 NOODLES ATOP SAUCE TO COVER
SPREAD 1 CUP BECHAMEL SAUCE OVER
SPOON 1/3 OF PESTO OVER
SPRINKLE WITH 1/3 CUP PARMESAN

REPEAT LAYERING TWICE
COVER WITH 4 NOODLES
SPREAD REMAINING BECHAMEL OVER
SPRINKLE WITH 1/3 CUP PARMESAN

(can be made 1 day ahead.  cover.  chill)

PREHEAT OVEN TO 375
COVER LASAGNA LOOSELY WITH FOIL
BAKE 30 MINUTES   (if refrigerated, bake 45 minutes)
UNCOVER AND BAKE UNTIL BUBBLING, ABOUT 20 MINUTES
LET STAND 15 MINUTES
CUT INTO SQUARES 

                                    - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
