                          Apple Blackberry Crisp

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Desserts                        Fruit
                Maddy's Desserts                Pies & Pastry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                 FRUIT  	
  5             pounds  Golden delicious apples, cored -- peeled and sliced
  1 1/2           cups  fresh or frozen blackberries -- thawed and drained
  1                cup  sugar
     1/4           cup  flour
                        	
               TOPPING  	
  2               cups  flour
  1 1/2           cups  sugar
     1/4      teaspoon  salt
  1                cup  chilled, unsalted butter -- cut in pieces

TOPPING
Mix flour, sugar and salt in processor
Add butter and blend using on/off turns until mixture resembles coarse
crumbs
Cover and chill

FRUIT
Preheat ooven to 350
Butter 9 x 13 baking dish
Toss apples, blackberries, sugar and flour in a large bowl
Transfer to prepared dish, mounding slightly

Crumble topping over fruit
Bake until fruit bubbles and topping is golden brown
Cool at least 15 minutes
Scoop into bowls and serve warm
