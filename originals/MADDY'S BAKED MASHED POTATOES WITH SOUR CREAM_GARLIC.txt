           MADDY'S BAKED MASHED POTATOES WITH SOUR CREAM_GARLIC

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALE
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables              Potatoes
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  8                LBS  RUSSET POTATOES, PEELED -- 1/2 IN PIECES
  12               OZS  CREAM CHEESE, ROOM TEMP -- CUT IN PIECES
  1 1/3           cups  SOUR CREAM
  1              TBLES  CHOPPED GARLIC
  3              TBLES  MELTED BUTTER

COOK POTATOES IN LARGE POT OF BOILING SALTED WATER UNTIL TENDER, ABOUT 20
MINUTES
DRAIN POTATOES AND RETURN TO SAME POT
ADD 12 OZ CREAM CHESE AND MASH WELL
MIX IN SOUR CREAM AND CHOPPED GARLIC
SEASON POTATOES TO TASTE WITH SALT AND PEPPER
TRANSFER TO 15X10X2 INCH GLASS BAKING DISH
DRIZZLE BUTTER OVER TOP

(POTATOES CAN BE PREPARED 1 DAY AHEAD.  COVER AND REFRIGERATE)

PREHEAT OVEN TO 375
BAKE POTATOES UNCOVERED UNTIL HEATED THRU, ABOUT 30 MINUTES  (OR 45
MINUTES IF THEY HAVE BEEN REFRIGERATED)
