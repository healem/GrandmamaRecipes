                            Maddy's Tomato Pie

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4                     tomatoes, peeled and sliced
  10                    basil leaves, fresh, chopped
  1             9 inch  pre baked pie shell
  1                cup  mozzarella, grated
  1                cup  cheddar, grated
  1                cup  mayonnaise
                        salt & pepper
     1/2           cup  green onion, chopped

Preheat oven to 350
Remove the stem end from the tomatoes
Cut an X in the bottom of each
Put tomatoes in hot water for a few seconds until the skin starts to peel.
Remove and put in ice water for a few seconds
Peel and slice
Place the tomato slices in a colander in the sink in one layer
Sprinkle with salt and allow to drain for 10 minutes

Layer the tomato slices, basil and onion in a pie shell
Season with salt and pepper
Combine the grated cheeses and mayonnaise together
Spread mixture on top of the tomatoes
Bake for 30 minutes or until lightly browned

Serve warm
