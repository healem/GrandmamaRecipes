                           Parmesan Tuna Spread

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Appetizers                      Fish
                Luncheon

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1           7 oz can  solid white tuna
  4             ounces  Parmesan cheese -- grated
  1         tablespoon  mayonnaise

     Drain tuna, place in small bowl, and flake with a fork.  Then, by
rubbing with finger, flake very finely.  Blend parmesan cheese in blender
until powder-fine.  Mix cheese and mayonnaise into tuna with a fork. 
Mixture should be almost smooth, but fairly dry.

Per Serving (excluding unknown items): 211 Calories; 12g Fat (51.1%
calories from fat); 24g Protein; 1g Carbohydrate; 0g Dietary Fiber; 38mg
Cholesterol; 715mg Sodium.  Exchanges: 3 1/2 Lean Meat; 1 Fat.

Serving Ideas : Stuff tiny cream puffs or spread on crackers and broil.
