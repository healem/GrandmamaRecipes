                   maddy's potato salad with vinegrette

Recipe By     :madeline m healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Luncheon                        Maddy's Main Meals
                Maddy's Salad Dressings         Maddy's Salads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4              large  potatoes
  4               tbls  dry white wine
  2               tbls  wine vinegar
  1                 ts  mustard
  6              tebls  olive oil
  2               tbls  minced onion
  1               tbls  dill
                        salt & pepper
                        juice of 1/2 lemon

boil potatoes, peel and cut up   cool slightly
whisk other ingredients together
pour over potatoes
toss gently
serve warm or cold
