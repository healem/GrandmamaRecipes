             MADDY'S GREEN BEAN, ASPARAGUS, PEAPOD CASSEROLE

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Maddy's Vegetables
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 LB  GREEN BEANS, ENDS SNIPPED
     1/2            LB  SMALL STALK ASPARAGUS
     1/2            LB  PEAPODS
  2               cups  PEARL ONIONS
  2               TBLS  OLIVE OIL
  2               TBLS  BUTTER
  2              whole  GARLIC CLOVES, CHOPPED
  1              whole  CHOPPED SHALLOT
  2                     BAY LEAVES
  3               cups  Assorted Mushrooms, Sliced
                        salt & pepper to taste
  2               tbls  fresh thyme leaves
  3               tbls  flour
  3               cups  milk
  1              whole  can fried onion rings

Blanch vegetables one at a time in pot of boiling water for about 3
minutes
Remove and place in ice water bath
Drain and put aside

Coat skillet with olive oil, butter overmedium heat
Sweat garlic and shallot untill they carmelize
Add bay leaves and mushrooms.
Stir to combine
Season with salt, pepper and thyme
Sprinkle flour over top
Add milk slowly and boil for 3 minutes to thicken

Place vegetables in 2 quart casserole
Pour mushroom mixtre over top
Toss well to coat all vegetables
Bake at 350 for 30 minutes
Top with fried onion rings and bake for 10 minutes more
