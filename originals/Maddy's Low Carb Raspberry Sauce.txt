                     Maddy's Low Carb Raspberry Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Desserts                        Fruit
                Maddy's Desserts                Maddy's Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  10                oz  package frozen raspberries, tawed
     1/2           cup  current jelly (or any compatible jelly)
  1               tbls  cornstarch
  1               tbls  butter
     3/4           cup  fresh orange juice
                        vanilla ice cream

In a medium saucepan, mix 3/4 cup of raspberries with the jelly 
Bring to a boil over medium-high heat
Reduce to medium
In a medium bowl, combine the remaining raspberries with the cornstarch,
and add to the saucepan
Cook, stirring constantly, until thickened
Remove the pan from the heat and stir in the butter
Cool berry mixture, and then stir in the orange juice
Refrigerate until chilled
Serve over vanilla ice cream
