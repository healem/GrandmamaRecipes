                       Maddy's Macaroni and Cheese

Recipe By     :Madeline M healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Casseroles                      Luncheon
                Maddy's Pasta Dishes            Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 lb  elbow macaroni
  2               tbls  butter
  3               tbls  flour
  2               cups  milk
                        salt & pepper to taste
  2               cups  plus 1/2 cup shredded sharp cheddar

Cook macaroni for 10 minutes
Drain and set aside

In deep skillet, melt butter over medium heat
Sprinkle flour over top, stirring to make a roux
Pour in milk, whisking uuntil thick and smooth
Stir in 2 cups of cheddar and cook until melted
Add macaroni and stir

Transfer to a 2 quart casserole
Bake at 350 for 20 minutes
Remove from oven and add 1/2 cup cheddar over top
Bake for 5 minutes more
