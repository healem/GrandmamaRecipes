                           Ultimate Cheesecake

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :3:00
Categories    : Cakes                           Cheesecake
                Desserts                        Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                 CRUST  	
  1                cup  flour
     1/4           cup  sugar
  1           teaspoon  lemon peel -- grated
     1/2      teaspoon  vanilla
  1                     egg yolk
     1/4           cup  butter -- softened
                        	
               FILLING  	
  5           packages  soft cream cheese -- 8 oz size
  1 3/4           cups  sugar
  3        tablespoons  flour
  2          teaspoons  lemon peel -- grated
  5              whole  eggs
  1 1/2      teaspoons  orange peel -- grated
  2                     egg yolks
     1/4      teaspoon  vanilla
     1/4           cup  heavy cream

Preheat oven to 400
Grease inside of 9-inch springform pan, remove side

CRUST
Combine flour, sugar, lemon peel and vanilla
Make well in the center
Blend egg yolk and butter with fork
Pour in well, and mix with fingertips until smooth
Fiorm half of the dough into a ball
Place in bottom of pan and cover with waxed paper
Roll pastry to edge of pan.  Remove waxed paper
Bake 6 to 8 minutes or until golden.  Cool
Divide rest of dough into three parts
Cut six strips of waxed paper, 3 inches wide
On dmapened surface, between waxed paper, roll each piece of dough 2 1/4
by 9 inches
Assemble springform pan with crust on bottom
Line inside of pan with pastry strips, overlapping ends
Remove waxed paper strips
Preheat oven to 450

FILLING
Blend cheese, sugar, flour, peels and vanilla at high speed
Beat in eggs and yolks, one at a time
Beat in cream.  Pour into pan.  Bake 10 minutes
Lower oven to 250.  Bake 1 hour longer
Remove to rack to cool for 2 hours
