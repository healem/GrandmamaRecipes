                      MADDY'S ROOT VEGETABLE GRATIN

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :3:20
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables              Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2         tblsp  butter, melted
  2                lbs  russet potatoes, peeled, sliced thin
  8                 oz  carrots, peeled, sliced thin
  1 1/4           cups  grated extra sharp cheddar
  4                 oz  celery root, peeled, sliced thin
  2               cups  whipping cream
  2              tblsp  green onion, chopped

Preheat oven to 400
brush 13 x 9 x 2 inch glass dish with melted butter
arrange 1/4 of potatoes evenly over bottom of pan
arrange half of carrots over potatoes
arrange 1/4 of potatoes over carrots
sprinkle with salt and pepper
sprinkle 1/2 cup cheese over top
arrange celery root over cheese
arrange 1/4 of potatoes over cheese
arrange remaining carrots evenly over potaotes
arrange remaining potatoes over carrots
sprinkle with salt and pepper
pour cream over top
sprinkle with 3/4 cup cheese

cover vegetables with foil and bake 30 minutes
uncover and bake until vegetables are tender and top is golden, about 35
minutes longer
let cool for 15 minutes
sprinkle green onion over top
serve
