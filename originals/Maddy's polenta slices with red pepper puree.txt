               Maddy's polenta slices with red pepper puree

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3               cups  water
  1                cup  cornmeal
  1           teaspoon  salt
  1         tablespoon  basil, finely chopped
     1/4           cup  cheddar or parmesan cheese, freshly grated
     1/8      teaspoon  hot sauce (tabasco)
  2                     shallots, peeled, roughly chopped
     1/4      teaspoon  salt
     1/8      teaspoon  black pepper, freshly ground
                        	
                        Roasted bell pepper puree
  3                 lg  red bell peppers, seeded (1 1/2 lbs)
  1 1/2    tablespoons  olive oil
  1         tablespoon  balsamic vinegar
                        	
                        Garnish
  2        tablespoons  parsley, chopped
  1 1/2    tablespoons  additional parmesan cheese
  6                     basil sprigs, fresh

Polenta
Bring water to a boil in a saucepan
Slowly pour in a continuous stream of cornmeal, stirring constantly
Add the salt
Stir occasionally until the polenta pulls away from the sides of the pan,
about 20 minutes
shut off the heat and stir in the basil and cheese
Working quickly, grease a medium sized (9 x 7 inch) shallow casserole dish
or pan with the olive oil
Pour in the polenta, spreading it out over the whole surface
Let stand and cool for 1 hour

Preheat oven to 400

Puree
Cut the sweet red peppers into large pieces
Smear a little olive oil over the bottom of an ovenproof casserole dish
and lay the peppers inside
Cover and bake for 20 minutes until they become soft
Remove from oven (leave the oven on to bake the polenta) and let cool
Put the peppers in a food processr and process until smooth
Add the remaining ingredients, including all but a teaspoon of the
remaining olive oil, and process until thoroughly blended
Scrape the dip into a serving bowl

When the polenta has cooled completely, cut into 12 equal pieces
Smear some olive oil over a baking sheet and with a spatula remove each
piece and arrange them on the sheet, leaving space around each one so that
the edges can crisp
Bake on the top rack in oven for 15 minutes or until browned

To serve, pour 2 tablespoons of the puree on each of 6 serving plates
Arrange 2 polenta pieces alongside, sprinkle some chopped parsley and
parmesan oven them and place a basil sprig on the side
