                           Maddy's Tuscan Soup

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Soups

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  16                oz  sausages, hot, chicken, turkey
  2             quarts  chicken broth
     1/2          head  cauliflower, sliced 1/4" thick
  1                 lb  carrots, sliced
  4                     celery stalks, sliced
  1                     onion, sliced
  6               cups  kale, chopped
     1/2            ts  red pepper flakes
  2             cloves  garlic, crushed

Saute sausages until cooked through, set aside
In same pan, cook carrots, celery, and onion until done

Heat chicken broth in crock pot or soup pan over medium heat
Add cauliflower, kale and cooked vegetables to broth

Slice sausages on the diagonal, abot 1/2" thick.  Cut each one in half
again
Add to soup
Stir in red pepper flakes and garlic

Turn burner to lowest heat and simmer for an hour, stirring now and then
