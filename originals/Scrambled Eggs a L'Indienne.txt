                       Scrambled Eggs a L'Indienne

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 2     Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Breakfast                       Eggs
                Luncheon

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              whole  onion -- chopped
  3        tablespoons  butter
     1/2      teaspoon  curry powder
  4                     eggs
     1/4           cup  tomato sauce

Saute onions in butter until soft, about 10 minutes. Add curry powder and
stir well.  Beat eggs slightly, add the tomato sauce and blend.  Stir into
the onion mixture and cook over low heat, stirring occasionally until eggs
are set.
