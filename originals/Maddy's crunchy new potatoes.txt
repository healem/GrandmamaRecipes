                       Maddy's crunchy new potatoes

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables              Potatoes
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  12             small  new potatoes
  1                pkg  ranch dressing mix (0.4 oz)
  2               cups  crushed corn flakes
  8               tbls  melted butter

Preheat oven to 400
Cook potatoes in boiling water until tender, about 20 - 25 minutes
Drain potatoes, let cool slightly, and then peel
Mix dressing and cornflakes in a bowl
Dip potatoes in melted butter, and then roll them in the cornflake mixture
Place potatoes in a greased baking dish and bake for 20 to 25 minutes, or
until golden brown
