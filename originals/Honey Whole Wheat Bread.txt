                         Honey Whole Wheat Bread

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :3:00
Categories    : Breads                          Healthy And Hearty

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  9               cups  whole-wheat flour
  4          teaspoons  salt
  2                pkg  active dry yeast
  1 1/2           cups  milk
  1 1/2           cups  water
  6        tablespoons  butter
     1/2           cup  honey

     Sift together 3 cups flour, salt, and yeast.  Combine milk, water,
butter, and honey in a saucepan and heat over low heat until liquids are
warm (butter need not melt completely).  Gradually add to dry ingredietns
and beat 2 minutes.  Add remaining flour a cup full at a time until a soft
dough forms.  Turn out onto lightly floured surface and allow to rest 10
minutes.  Knead until smooth and elastic, about 10 minutes.  Place in
large grease bowl, turn to grease all sides of dough.  Cover and allow to
rise in a warm place until doubled in bluk, about one hour.
     Punch dough down and turn out onto lightly floured surface.  Divide
dough in half and shape each half into a loaf.  Place into greased loaf
pans.  Coverr and let rise in warm place until double in bulk, about one
hour.  Bake at 375 degrees for 35-40 minutes.  Remove from pans and cool
on racks.
