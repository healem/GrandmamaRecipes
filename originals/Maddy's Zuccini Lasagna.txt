                         Maddy's Zuccini Lasagna

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:40
Categories    : Maddy's Low Carb                Maddy's Main Meals

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 lb  ground beef
  1                     garlic clove
  15                oz  tomato sauce
  1                 ts  salt
  1                 ts  dried basil
  1                 ts  dried oregano
     1/2            ts  dried thyme
     1/2            ts  dried majoram
  14                oz  whole milk ricotta chese
     1/2           cup  grated parmesan cheese
  1                     egg
  4             medium  zuccini
  2               tbls  low carb bake mix
  1                cup  mozzarella cheese

In large skillet, brown beef with garlic, breaking up beef
Drain off fat
Stir in tomato sauce, salt, basil, oregano, thyme and marjoram
Simmer uncovered for 10 minutes

In bowl, combine ricotta cheese, 1/2 parmesdan and egg

Scrub zuccini, slice lengtwise in 1/8" slices

Prehat oven to 350
Lightly oil baking dish 13 x 9 x 2

Layer 1/2 of zuccini in baking dish
Sprinkle with 1/2 of bak mix
Top with 1/2 ricotta cheese mix and 1/2 of meat
Then with 1/2 mozzarella
Top with remaining zucchini
Sprinkle withremaining bake mix
Top with remaining ricotta cheese, meat and mozzarella cheesse
Sprinkle with remaining parmesan cheese over top

Bake uncovered 1 hour
Let stand 15 minutes before serving
Top
