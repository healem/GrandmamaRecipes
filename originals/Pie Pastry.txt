                                Pie Pastry

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:45
Categories    : Pies & Pastry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  flour
  1           teaspoon  salt
     1/2           cup  unsalted butter
  2        tablespoons  lard
  5        tablespoons  ice water

     Mix flour and salt.  Cut in butter and lard and until mixture
resembles coarse cornmeal.  Sprinkle the cold water over flour mixture and
quickly beat in, using a fork.  Gather dough into a ball, wrap in wax
paper and chill for 30 minutes.

Per Serving (excluding unknown items): 244 Calories; 15g Fat (55.4%
calories from fat); 3g Protein; 24g Carbohydrate; 1g Dietary Fiber; 34mg
Cholesterol; 269mg Sodium.  Exchanges: 1 1/2 Grain(Starch); 3 Fat.

NOTES : Makes one batch -- enough for a two crust, 9" pie.
