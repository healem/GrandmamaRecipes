                          Nonie's Chocolate Cake

Recipe By     :Madeline
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  sugar
  1 1/2           cups  flour
     1/4           cup  cocoa
  1           teaspoon  baking soda
     1/2           cup  oil
  1           teaspoon  vanilla
  1         tablespoon  vinegar
  1                cup  cold water

Mix dry ingredients together
Make three dents in mixture
Add oil, vanilla, vinegar and cold water
Mix well
Bake at 350 for 35 to 40 minutes
Serve
