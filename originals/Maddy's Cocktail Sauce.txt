                          Maddy's Cocktail Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 32    Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Maddy's Sauces                  Sauces
                Seafood

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  chili sauce
     1/2           cup  ketchup
  1         tablespoon  Worcestershire sauce
  8        tablespoons  prepared horseradish (heaping)
  16             drops  Tabasco sauce
     1/2      teaspoon  dry mustard
  1               dash  paprika
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
  1           teaspoon  fresh ground black pepper
  2              tblsp  chopped cilantro

Blend the chili sauce, ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, horseradish, Tabasco
sauce, and dry ingredients. Chill before serving. Makes about 2 cups, or
32 one-tablespoon servings.
