                            Potatoes Romanoff

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:30
Categories    : Potatoes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4                     potatoes -- quartered
  2        tablespoons  butter
  1                cup  sour cream
  4              whole  green onions -- sliced
  1 1/4           cups  cheddar cheese -- shredded
  1           teaspoon  salt
     1/8      teaspoon  black pepper
                        paprika -- to taste

Boil potatoes until tender.  Mash with butter, sour cream, onions, 3/4 cup
cheese, salt and pepper.  Turn mixture into buttered 1-1/2 quart
casserole.  Sprinkle with remaining cheese and paprika.  Bake uncovered at
350 degrees for 40 minutes.
