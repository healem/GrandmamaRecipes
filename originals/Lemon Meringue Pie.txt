                            Lemon Meringue Pie

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Desserts                        Pies & Pastry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2                     eggs -- separated
     1/4           cup  fresh lemon juice
  1         tablespoon  lemon zest -- grated
  3        tablespoons  all-purpose flour
  1                cup  sugar
  2        tablespoons  butter
  1 1/4           cups  boiling water
  1                     9 inch pie crust
  3        tablespoons  sugar

     Beat egg yolks with lemon juice and zest.  Pour into heavy saucepan
and heat on low.  Beat in flour, sugar and  butter.  Add boiling water,
cook and stir until mixture thickens.  Pour into prepared pie crust.  Beat
egg whites until stiff, but not dry.  Gradually add sugar. Spread over
filling.  Bake at 450 degrees until brown.
