                          Spicy Stir Fried Fish

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Chinese                         Fish
                Main Dishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4                     dried mushrooms
  2               cups  peanut oil
  1              pound  whitefish fillets
  2        tablespoons  tomato sauce
  2        tablespoons  red pepper flakes
     1/4           cup  bamboo shoots -- drained and chopped
  1         tablespoon  rice wine
  1         tablespoon  sugar
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
  1         tablespoon  soy sauce
  2          teaspoons  cornstarch
  2        tablespoons  green onions -- chopped

    Soak mushrooms for 10 minutes in warm water, then chop.  Heat oil in a
wok. When it is very hot, add fish and fry until lightly browned.  Remove
to warm dish and pour off all but 2 Tbsp. of oil.  Add tomato sauce,
pepper, bamboo shoots, and mushrooms.  Cook, stirring for 3-4 minutes,
until mushrooms soften.  Add wine, sugar, salt, and soy sauce and stir
mixture for 30 seconds.  Return fish to pan and turn it over in the sauce.
Dissolve conrstarch in 1 Tbsp. water and add to wok.  Stir gently just
until sauce  thickens.  Pour onto serving dish and garnish with onions.
