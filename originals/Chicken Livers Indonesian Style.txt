                     Chicken Livers Indonesian Style

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:50
Categories    : Ethnic                          Main Dishes
                Poultry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              pound  chicken livers
  1                cup  coconut milk
  3        tablespoons  butter
  1                     onion -- chopped
  1              clove  garlic -- minced
     1/2      teaspoon  chili powder
     1/4           cup  almonds -- ground
  2        tablespoons  lemon rind -- grated
  2        tablespoons  fresh lemon juice
     1/2      teaspoon  salt

      Melt butter, add onion, garlic and chili powder, and saute 5
minutes, stirring frequently. Add livers and saute for  five minutes,
until livers are cooked through, but still pink inside. Add remaining
ingredients and simmer over low heat for 10 minutes, stirring
occasionally.
