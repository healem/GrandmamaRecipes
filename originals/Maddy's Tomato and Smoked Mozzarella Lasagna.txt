               Maddy's Tomato and Smoked Mozzarella Lasagna

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Italian                         Maddy's Main Meals
                Maddy's Pasta Dishes            Main Dishes
                Pasta                           Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        olive oil, for drizzling
  1                cup  ricotta cheese, part skim milk
  3               tbls  parsley, fresh, chopped
  1              clove  garlic, finely chopped
     1/4           cup  parmesan cheese, grated
                        salt & pepper to taste
     1/2            lb  lasagna sheets, cooked and drained
  6                     plum tomatoes, cored and thinly sliced
     1/3            lb  mozzarrella cheese, thinly sliced

set the oven at 400
oil an 8 inch baking dish
in bowl, combine ricotta, parsley, garlic, parmesan, salt & pepper
place 1/3 of pasta sheets in the baking dish
add half of the ricotta mixture, half the plum tomatoes, half the smoked
mozzarella
Continue layering until you have 3 layers of pasta sandwiching 2 layers of
tomatos and cheese
drizzle the pasta with a little olive oil
spread the oil all over the top of the pasta
transfer the dish to the hot oven and bake for 35 to 40 minutes or until
the filling is bubbling at the edges
serve at once
