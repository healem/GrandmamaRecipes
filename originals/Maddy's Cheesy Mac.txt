                            Maddy's Cheesy Mac

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Pasta Dishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 lb  macaroni, cooked anddrained
  1                 lb  grated sharp cheese
  3                     beaten eggs
  1                cup  sour cream
  6               tbls  butter, cold, cut into pieces
     1/2           tsp  salt
  1 1/2           cups  milk

Peheat oven to 350

place hot cooked macaroni in large bowl
Add 1/2 of the sharp cheese
Mix together
In a separate bowl, combine remaining ingredients
stir together to blend

Add mixture to macaroni and mix well

Place in a greased casserole dish
Cover with remaining sharp cheese

Bake for 40-50 minutes until crusty on top
Top with additional cheese if desired
