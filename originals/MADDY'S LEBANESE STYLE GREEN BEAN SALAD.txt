                 MADDY'S LEBANESE STYLE GREEN BEAN SALAD

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Salads                  Salads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 LB  GREEN BEANS, CUT INTO THIRDS
  1                 LB  CHICK PEAS (GARBANZO BEANS) COOKED
  1              large  TOMATO, CHOPPED
  1              small  RED ONION, SLICED
  1              clove  ELEPHANT GARLIC
     1/8            TS  KOSHER SALT
  1              whole  LEMON, JUICED
  1               TBLS  RED WINE VINEGAR
  3               TBLS  OLIVE OIL
  1               TBLS  SESAME SEEDS, TOASTED
  1                 TS  TARRAGON

BRING POT OF WATER TO BOIL
ADD GREEN BEANS
COOK FOR 10 MINUTES (TENDER, BUT WITH A BITE)
SPREAD BEANS OUT ON TOWEL TO COOL
TRANSFER TO BOWL
ADD CHICK PEAS, TOMATOES AND ONION
SPRINKLE SALT ON CUTTING BOARD.  SET GARLIC ON SALT. WITH THE FLAT BLADE
OF A KNIFE, CRUSH THE GARLIC INTO THE SALT UNTIL PASTY
RUB GARLIC THROUGH THE GREEN BEAN MIXTURE
MIX LEMON, VINEGAR, OIL, TARRAGON, PEPPER AND POUR OVER SALAD INGREDIENTS
ADD SALT & PEPPER TO TASTE
TOSS GENTLY
