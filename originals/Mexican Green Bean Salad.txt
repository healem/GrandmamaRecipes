                         Mexican Green Bean Salad

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Mexican                         Salads
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              pound  green beans
     1/2           cup  olive oil
  2                     jalapeno peppers, canned -- chopped
  1         tablespoon  vinegar
  2        tablespoons  lemon juice
  1         tablespoon  onion -- minced
  1         tablespoon  parsley -- minced
  1         tablespoon  fresh cilantro -- minced

     Steam green beans until tender-crisp, about 15 minutes.  Drain. 
Whisk together other ingredients and pour over warm green beans.  Allow to
marinate at least 1/2 hour.  Serve at room temperature or chilled.
