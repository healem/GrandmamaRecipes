                          Maddy's Vegetable Tian

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables              Main Dishes
                Vegetables                      Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        olive oil
  2                 lg  onions, cut in half and sliced 1/4 inch
  2                     garlic cloves, minced
  1                 lb  medium potatoes, peeled, sliced 1/4 inch
     3/4            lb  zuccini, sliced
  1 1/4            lbs  medium tomatoes, sliced 1/4 inch
  1                 ts  kosher salt
     1/2            ts  ground black pepper
  1               tbls  fresh thyme leaes, plus extra sprigs
  2                 oz  gruyere cheese, grated

Preheat oven to 375

Brush 9 x 13 baking dish with olive oil
In medium saute pan, heat 2 tbls olive oil and cook the onions over
medium-low heat for 8 to 10 minutes, until translucent
Add the garlic and cook for another minute
Spread the onion mixture on the bottom of baking dish

Layer sliced potatoes, zuccini and tomatoes alternately in the dish on top
of the onions, fitting tem tightly, making only 1 layer
Sprinkle with salt, pepper, thyme leaves and thyme sprigs
Drizzle with 1 tbls olive oil
Cover dish with foil and bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until potatoes are
tender
Uncover the dish, remove thyme sprigs, sprinkle the top with cheese 
Bake another 30 minutes until browned.
Serve warm
