                           Maddy's Quinoa Pilaf

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 2     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Maddy's Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  quinoa, dry
  1                     onion, chopped
  1              clove  garlic, chopped
     1/2           cup  dried fruit
     1/2           cup  nuts
  1                tsp  olive oil
  1                cup  water or broth

Saute onion and garlic in oil until done

Rinse quinoa until water runs clear
Place in dry saute and toast for 5 minutes

Place water in saute pan.  
Add toasted quinoa 
Bring to boil
Cover and lower heat to simmer
Simer for about 15 minutes, until germ has spiraled out from each grain

Add onion and garlic, and dried fruit and nuts
Serve
