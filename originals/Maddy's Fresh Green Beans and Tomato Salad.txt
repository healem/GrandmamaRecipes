                Maddy's Fresh Green Beans and Tomato Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Maddy's Salads                  Salads
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2            lb  green beans, fresh, trimmed
     1/2           cup  snow peas, fresh or frozen
  2                     tomatoes, seeded and cut into quarters
  2                     eggs, hard-boiled, cut into quarters
  1                can  water chestnuts, drained, sliced
  3                     green onions, sliced
                        salt & pepper
     1/3           cup  olive oil
  1               tbls  lemon juice
  1              large  romaine or red leaf lettuce, washed, -- dried

Cook eggs until hard, peel and cut in quarters
Place in large bowl

Seed tomatoes and cut into quarters
Add to bowl

Drain water chestnuts and slice
Add to bowl

Slice green onions
Add to bowl

Blanch beans in large pot of boiling salted water 5 minutes
Remove to an ice bath to stop cooking
Drain and add to bowl

Blanch snow peas in same pot 
Remove to an ice bath 
Drain and add to bowl

Season with salt & pepper
Mix lemon juice ad olive oil
Pour over vegetables and combine

Serve over lettuce leaves
