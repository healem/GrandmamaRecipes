                          Maddy's Tabouli Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Maddy's Salads
                Maddy's Vegetables              Vegetables
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  bulgar wheat
  1 1/2           cups  boiling water
  1                cup  fresh parsley, chopped
  2                     fresh tomatoes, chopped
  1                     european cucumber, seeded, chopped
  1                cup  minced scalliona (1 bunch)
     1/4           cup  olive oil
     1/4           cup  lemon juice (2 lemons)
                        salt & pepper to taste

Place bulgar wheat in bowl
Pour boiling water over top
mix, cover and let sit until all water is absorbed

Add lemon juice, olive oil, salt and pepper

When cooled, add all the rest of the ingredients

Refrigerate to blend flavors
