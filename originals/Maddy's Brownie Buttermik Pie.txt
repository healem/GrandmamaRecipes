                      Maddy's Brownie Buttermik Pie

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Desserts                        Maddy's Desserts
                Pies & Pastry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  12                oz  chocolate chips
  1                cup  buttermilk
  7                     eggs
                  dash  vanilla extract
  1                cup  sugar
  1                     pre-made pie shell

Prepare pie shell
Put all other ingredients in mixing bowl
Beat together until well blended
Pour into pie shell
Bake 1 hour at 350.
Let cool before serving
