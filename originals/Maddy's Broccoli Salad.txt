                          Maddy's Broccoli Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Maddy's Salads                  Salads
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4              heads  BROCCOLI CROWNS, BITE SIZE PIECES
     1/4        medium  RED ONION, CHOPPED
  1                cup  MIXED NUTS, WHOLE
  1                cup  GOLDEN RAISINS
     1/4           cup  CELERY
                        	
                        	
  1                cup  MAYONNAISE
  2               TBLS  CIDER VINEGAR
  1               TBLS  CURRY POWDER
     1/4           cup  SUGAR

MIX MAYONNAISE, VINEGAR, CURRY POWDER AND SUGAR TOGETHER

PLACE ALL OTHER INGREDIENTS IN BOWL
COVER WITH MAYONNAISE MIXTURE

TOSS UNTIL WELL MIXED
