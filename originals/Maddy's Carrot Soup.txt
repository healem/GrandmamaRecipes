                           Maddy's Carrot Soup

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Soups                   Maddy's Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2                lbs  carrots, shredded or chopped
  56                oz  chicken broth
  4             stalks  celery, chopped
  1                     onion, chopped
     1/4           cup  butter
                        fresh dill

Saute onion and celery in butter until tender
Add carrots and broth
Bring to boil

Reduce heat and simmer, covered for about 1/2 hour

Let cool slightly
Puree until smooth
Add dill and serve
