                               Pumpkin Pie

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:15
Categories    : Desserts                        Pies & Pastry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  mashed pumpkin
     2/3           cup  brown sugar
  1           teaspoon  ground ginger
     1/2      teaspoon  nutmeg
  1           teaspoon  cinnamon
  2                     eggs -- beaten
     3/4           cup  light cream
     1/4           cup  dark rum -- (optional)
     1/2         batch  pie pastry -- enough for a 9"pie

     Roll out pastry and line a 9" pie pan with it. Bake at 450 degrees
for 10 minutes.  Cool shell.  Combine pumpkin, sugar, and spices.  Beat in
eggs and cream.  If desired, stir in rum.  Bake at 325 degrees for 1 hour
or until a  knife inserted into pie comes clean.

Serving Ideas : Serve with fresh whipped cream or vanilla ice cream.
