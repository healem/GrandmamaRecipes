                        Maddy's Gingerbread Scones

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Breakfast
                Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  flour
     1/3           cup  dark brown sugar
  2                tsp  baking powder
     1/8      teaspoon  baking soda
     1/2      teaspoon  ground ginger
     1/2      teaspoon  ground cinnamon
     1/8      teaspoon  ground cloves
     1/8      teaspoon  ground nutmeg
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/3           cup  cold butter
  1              large  egg
  3        tablespoons  molasses
  3        tablespoons  milk
  1           teaspoon  vanilla
     1/2           cup  golden raisins

Preheat oven to 375
Lightly butter the center of a baking pan 

In large bowl, stir together all dry ingredients
Cut the butter into 1/2 inch pieces and distribute them over the flour
mixture.  With a pastry blender, cut in the butter until the mixture
resembles coarse crumbs.
Add the raisins

In a small bowl, stir together the wet ingredients.
Add the wet mixture to the dry mixture and stir to combine.  The dough
will be sticky.

With lightly floured hands, pat the dough into an 8 inch diameter circle
in the center of the baking sheet.  Cut into 8 wedges.

Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes
out clean.

Move the baking sheet to a wire rack and cool for 5 minutes.
Using a spatula, transfer the scones to the wire rack to cool.
Recutinto wedges.  Serve warm.
