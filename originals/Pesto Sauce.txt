                               Pesto Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Appetizers                      Food Processor Cooking
                Italian                         Maddy's Sauces
                Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  5               cups  fresh basil
     1/4           cup  chopped parsley
     3/4           cup  olive oil
  2        tablespoons  minced garlic
     1/2           cup  pignoli nuts
  1           teaspoon  salt
     1/2           cup  peccorino or parmesan

Place all ingredients in food processor until well blended.

If not used immediately, put in jar & cover with 3/4 cup olive oil.  Cover
tightly in refrigerator.
