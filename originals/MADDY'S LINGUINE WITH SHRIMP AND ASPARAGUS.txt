                MADDY'S LINGUINE WITH SHRIMP AND ASPARAGUS

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Casseroles                      Healthy And Hearty
                Pasta

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        COOKING SPRAY
  1                 LB  UNCOOKED SHRIMP -- PEELED & DEVEINED
  1                cup  SHALLOTS -- SLICED
  16            SPEARS  ASPARAGUS -- CUT IN 1 INCH PIECES
  1                 LB  PLUM TOMATOES PEELED -- SEEDED, CHOP
  1                cup  FAT FREE YOGURT
     1/2           cup  VODKA
     1/2           cup  SLICED DRAINED SUN-DRIED TOMATOES -- 2 OZ
  12                OZ  LINGUINE
     1/4           cup  THINLY SLICED FRESH BASIL

SPRAY COOKING SPRAY IN SKILLET OVER MEDIUM HEAT
ADD SHRIMP, SAUTE UNTIL OPAQUE IN CENTER, ABOUT 2 MIN
USING SLOTTED SPOON, TRANSFER SHRIMP TO BOWL
ADD SHALLOTS AND ASPARAGUS TO SKILLET
SAUTE UNTIL SHALLOTS ARE TENDER AND SLIGHTLY BROWN,
ABOUT 3 MINUTES
ADD PLUM TOMATOES, YOGURT, VODKA AND SUN-DRIED TOMATOES
SIMMER UNTIL TOMATOES ARE TENDER, ABOUT 4 MINUTES
ADD SHRIMP, TOSS TO COAT

MEANWHILE, COOK LINGUINE IN LARGE POT OF BOILING SALTED WATER UNDER TENDER
BUT STILL FIRM TO BITE
DRAIN, RESERVING 1/2 CUP COOKING LIQUID
RETURN PASTA AND RESERVED COOKING LIQUID TO POT
ADD SAUCE, TOSS TO COAT
SEASON WITH SALT AND PEPPER
TRANSFER TO SERVING DISH
SPRINKLE WITH BASIL AND SERVE
