                         Maddy's Asparagus Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Salads                  Salads
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/4           cup  white wine vinegar
  2               Tbls  fresh basil, chopped  (or 2 tsp dried)
  1               Tbls  olive oil
  1               Tbls  dijon mustard
  1                     clove garlic, chopped
     1/2           tsp  bottled hot pepper sauce
  3               cups  thinly diagonal sliced asparagus
  1                cup  julienned jicama
  1                cup  julienned sweet red pepper
  2               tbls  minced onions

Dressing:  mix first 6 ingredients together and set aside
Salad:  combine next 4 ingredients together, and pour dressing over the
top.
Toss together
