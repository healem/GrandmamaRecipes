                                Onion Pie

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Pies & Pastry                   Vegetables
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3               cups  onions -- sliced
  2        tablespoons  butter
  1              pound  cottage cheese
     1/4           cup  heavy cream
  1                     9 inch pie crust
                        salt
                        pepper
                        cayenne

     Fry onions in butter until soft.  Blend cheese with cream and season
to taste.  Pour into pie shell.  Spread onion onions over cheese.  Bake at
400 degrees for 15 minutes.
