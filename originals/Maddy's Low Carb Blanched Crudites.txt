                    Maddy's Low Carb Blanched Crudites

Recipe By     :Madelin M Healey
Serving Size  : 1     Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers
                Maddy's Low Carb

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 lb  asparagus
  1                 lb  carrots, sliced diagonally in 1-inch chunk
  1               pint  cherry tomatoes
  2              heads  radicchio
                        	
                        Herb Dip
  8                 oz  cream cheese, room temperature
     1/2           cup  sour cream
     1/2           cup  mayonnaise
  4                     scallions, white and green parts, minced
  2               tbls  parsley leaves, minced
  1               tbls  dill, minced
  1                tsp  kosher salt
     3/4           tsp  black pepper

Prepare large pot of boiling salted water
Fill large bowl with ice water

Remove tough bottoms of the asparagus
If they are thick, peel the stems half-way up the stalk

Add the carrots to the pot of boiling water and blanch for 1 minute
Immediately remove carrots with a slotted spoon and plunge them into the
bowl of ice water
Drain when fully cooled

Add the asparagus to the boiling water and blanch for 1 minute
Immediately remove the asparagus with a slotted spoon and plunge them into
he bowl of ice water
Drain the asparagus when fully cooled

Slice the rdicchio into wedges

Arrange the carrots, asparagus, cherry tomatoes and radicchio on a platter
and serve with the Herb Dip

Herb Dip
Place the cream cheese, sour cream, mayonnaise, scallions, parsley, dill,
salt and pepper in the bowl of an electric mixer fitted with the paddle
attachment and blend
Serve at room temperature
2 cups
