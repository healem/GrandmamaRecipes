                              Dilly Carrots

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Canning, Preserves, Etc.        Healthy And Hearty
                Pickles & Relishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2                small onion
  2             cloves  garlic
  2        tablespoons  salt
     1/8      teaspoon  cayenne pepper
  1         tablespoon  fresh dill weed
  1         tablespoon  dill seed
  1              pound  carrots
  1 1/4           cups  vinegar
  1 1/4           cups  water

     Cut carrots into lengthwise slices that will fit a pint size jar. 
Pack into two pint sized jars.  In each jar, also place a piece of onion,
garlic clove, 1 tablespoon each of salt,  dill weed, and dill dillseed,
and a pinch of cayenne pepper.  
     Bring vinegar and water to a boil.  Pour over carrots.  Stor in
refrigerator, or process jars according to manufacturers' instructions.
