                             Monster Cookies

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 1     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Cookies                         Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  12             whole  eggs
  1              pound  butter
  2             pounds  brown sugar
  3             pounds  peanut butter
  4               cups  white sugar
  18              cups  oatmeal
  1         tablespoon  vanilla
  1              pound  chocolate chips
  1         tablespoon  maple syrup
  1              pound  plain M&Ms
  8          teaspoons  baking soda
     1/2         pound  raisins

Preheat oven to 350
Mix in order given
Drop by large tablespoonsfuls or ice cream scoop on cookie sheet
Flatten.  Bake for 12 minutes.  DO NOT OVERCOOK
