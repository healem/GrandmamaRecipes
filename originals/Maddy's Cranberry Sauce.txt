                         Maddy's Cranberry Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Fruit                           Maddy's Sauces
                Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                bag  cranberries
  2                     apples
  1                     orange
  2                     cinnamon sticks
  8                 oz  honey
  1                cup  apple cider
  1                cup  orange juice

Place cranberries in medium saucepan
Peel, core and dice apples and add to saucepan
Zest orange and add to saucepan
Peel, remove pith and dice orange, add to saucepan
Pour apple cider, orange juice and honey into pan
Add cinnamon sticks
Cook on high heat until boiling and cranberries burst
Reduce to simmer and cook until thick (about 3/4 hour)
Remove cinnamon sticks
Cool in refrigerator until ready to use
