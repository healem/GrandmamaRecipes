                MADDY'S NEW POTATOES WITH HERBS AND GARLIC

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables              Potatoes
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               TBLS  OLIVE OIL
  1                 TS  SAGE
  2                 TS  CHOPPED GARLIC
  1                 TS  ROSEMARY
  1                 LB  SMALL RED-SKINNED POTATOES
     1/4           cup  WATER (MAY BE MORE)
     1/4           cup  MINCED PARSLEY

HEAT 1 TBLS OLIVE OIL IN HEAVY MEDIUM SAUCEPAN OVER MEDIUM HIGH HEAT
ADD SAGE, GARLIC AND ROSEMARY
SAUTE UNTIL GARLIC IS TENDER, ABOUT 2 MINUTES
ADD POTATOES AND 1/4 CUP WATER
REDUCE HEAT TO MEDIUM
COVER PAN
COOK POTATOES UNTIL JUST TENDER, STIRRING OCCASIONALLY AND ADDING
ADDITIONAL WATER BY TABLESPOONFULS IF POTATOES ARE DRY, ABOUT 30 MINUTES
ADD PARSLEY AND 1 TBLS OIL AND TOSS TO COAT
SEASON TO TASTE WITH SALT AND PEPPER
TRANSFER TO BOWL AND SERVE
