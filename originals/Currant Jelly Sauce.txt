                           Currant Jelly Sauce

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:10
Categories    : Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2        tablespoons  butter
     1/4           cup  scallions -- chopped
  1         tablespoon  all-purpose flour
  1                cup  chicken stock
  3        tablespoons  currant jelly
  1         tablespoon  creme de cassis

     Melt butter and saute scallions for two minutes.  Stie in flour and
cook, stirring, one minute more.  Gradually add stock, stirring
constantly, and bring to a boil.  Turn off heat.  Add currant jelly and
creme de cassis, and stir until smooth.
