                        Chocolate Chip Cheese Bar

Recipe By     :Madeline
Serving Size  : 25    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cookies                         Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2           packages  chocolate chip cookie dough -- regular size
     3/4           cup  sugar
  16            ounces  cream cheese
  2              whole  eggs
  1           teaspoon  vanilla
     1/3           cup  nuts -- chopped

Lightly grease 9 x 13 baking dish
While cold, cut one cookie roll into slices 1/4 inch thick
Spread on bottom of pan close together - to form a crust

Whip cream cheese, sugar, eggs and vanilla
Spread over crust

Also, while cold, cut second cookie roll into slices 1/4 inch thick
Spread over cream cheese layer
Sprinkle top with nuts

Bake at 350 for 25 minutes
