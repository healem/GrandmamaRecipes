                            Chicken Cacciatore

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Ethnic                          Poultry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1            3-pound  whole chicken
     1/2           cup  all-purpose flour
                        salt
                        black pepper
                        cayenne pepper
     1/4           cup  olive oil
  1             medium  onion -- sliced thinly
  1              clove  garlic -- minced
  1                     large bell pepper -- sliced thinly
  1          1 lb. can  Italian plum tomatoes -- coarsely chopped
     1/4      teaspoon  dried basil
     1/4      teaspoon  oregano
     1/4      teaspoon  rosemary
  4             ounces  mushrooms -- sliced

     Cut chicken into serving-size pieces.  Season flour with salt,
pepper, and cayenne.  Roll chicken in seasoned flour to coat.
       In a Dutch oven or similar heavy kettle, saute onions, garlic, and
bell pepper in olive oil until limp.  Remove from pan .  Brown chicken in
same olive oil.  Add cooked onion mixture and remaining ingredients to
kettle.  Simmer over medium low heat until chicken is cooked through,
30-40 minutes.
