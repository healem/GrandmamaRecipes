                        MADDY'S BROCCOLI CASSEROLE

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALRY
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Casseroles                      Maddy's Vegetables
                Vegetables                      Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               PKGS  FROZEN CHOPPED BROCCOLI
  8                 OZ  CREAM CHEESE
  1              small  ONION
  2               cups  CRUSHED RITZ CRACKERS
  1                can  CREAM MUSHROOM SOUP
  2             sticks  BUTTER

COOK BROCCOLI - RESERVE
MELT 1 STICK BUTTER AND SAUTE ONION IN BUTTER
RESERVE BUTTER FOR CRACKERS LATER
REMOVE ONION FROM PAN AND MIX WITH COOKED BROCCOLI

WARM UNDILUTED SOUP

MELT 2ND STICK OF BUTTER AND ADD CREAM CHEESE

MIX COOKED BROCCOLI, ONION, SOUP AND CREAM CHEESE TOGETHER - PLACE IN
CASSEROLE DISH

SAUTE CRACKERS IN RESERVED BUTTER UNTIL BROWN
TOP CASSEROLE WITH CRACKERS
BAKE AT 350 FOR 45 MINUTES UNCOVERED
