             Maddy's Pesto Sauce (Kathy Kiesel via Boo Leach)

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Appetizers                      Italian
                Maddy's Sauces                  Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  fresh basil, packed
     1/2           cup  olive oil
  3        tablespoons  pignoli nuts
  3              whole  garlic cloves
  1           teaspoon  salt
                        	
     1/2           cup  parmesan cheese
  2        tablespoons  romano or peccorino, shredded
  3        tablespoons  riccota
  2        tablespoons  olive oil

Place first 5 ingredients in a food processor
Process until well blended

(This much may be frozen)

Before serving, thaw and add
last 4 ingredients and process until fully blended
