                            Maddy's Pot Roast

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :8:00
Categories    : Beef                            Maddy's Main Meals

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3                 lb  boneless chick roast
  1 1/2            tsp  house dressing
     1/4           cup  olive oil
  1                     onion, thinly sliced
  3                     bay leaves
  4                     beef bouillon cubes, crushed
  2             cloves  garlic , crushed
  10 3/4            oz  can condensed cream of mushroom soup
     1/2           cup  chardonnay
                        	
                        HOUSE DRESSING
  1                cup  salt
     1/4           cup  black pepper
     1/4           cup  garlic powder

Sprinkle roast on all sides with house seasoning.
Using a skillet over high heat, sear roast in oil until brown 
Place roast in a crock pot
Layer onions, bay leaves, crushed bouillon cubes, garlic and soup
Add chardonnay 
Add enough water to cover meat
Cook on low setting for 8 hours

HOUSE DRESSING
Mix ingredients together and store in an airtight container for up to 6
months
