                          maddy's shrimp scampi

Recipe By     :madeline m healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Main Meals              Maddy's Pasta Dishes
                Seafood

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2                lbs  shrimp, peeled, deveined - tails left on
                        salt & fresh pepper to taste
  2        tablespoons  olive oil
  1              small  onion, sliced thinly
     1/3            lb  mushrooms, fresh, sliced thinly
  2        tablespoons  garlic, chopped
  1                     lemon, juiced
     1/2           cup  white wine
     1/2           cup  stock, seafood, fish or chicken
  2        tablespoons  butter
  2        tablespoons  parsley, fresh, chopped
                        lemon slices for garnish
                        lingine or rice

season the shrimp with salt & pepper
heat a large saute pan over medium-high heat
when the pan is hot, add oil to lightly coat the pan
add the shrimp and quickly saute until just starting to turn pink, but not
cooked thru
remove from the pan and set aside
add the onions and saute just until they begin to soften, about 3 minutes
add the garlicand mushrooms and cook another 30 seconds
add the lemon juice, white wine, and stock, and reduce by 2/3, about 5
minutes
add the shrimp back to the pan and swirl in the butter
finish with the parsley and check for seasoning
garnish with lemon slices and serve over rice or linguine
