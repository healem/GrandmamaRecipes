                       Maddy's Peanut Butter Balls

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 18    Preparation Time :0:40
Categories    : Desserts                        Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  peanut butter
  1                cup  honey
  2               cups  powdered milk
  1 1/2           cups  crushed corn flakes or
  1 1/2           cups  finely chopped nuts of your choice or
  1                cup  confection sugar

Mix peanut butter, honey and milk together in a large bowl to form a very
thick mixture
Roll mixture in small balls, about the size of a walnut
Roll the balls in either crushed cornflakes, chopped nuts, or confection
sugar
Place on waxed paper and refrigerate for 20 minutes
