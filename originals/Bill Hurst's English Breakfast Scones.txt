                  Bill Hurst's English Breakfast Scones

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Breakfast
                Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2 3/4           cups  all purpose flour
  6        tablespoons  sugar
  1 1/2    tablespoons  baking powder
  1           teaspoon  salt
     1/2           cup  (1 stick) chilled unsalted butter
     3/4           cup  raisins
  1                cup  chilled whipping cream
  2              large  eggs
                        additional sugar

Preheat oven to 400
Lightly dust baking sheet with flour
Sift first 4 ingredients into a large bowl
Add butter and blend until mixture resembles coarse meal
Mix in raisins
Whisk cream and eggs in a small bowl to blend
Pour over crumb mixture, stirring just until combined

Transfer dough to lightly floured work surface
Press dough into 1-inch thickness
Cut out rounds using 3-inch round cookie cutter
Transfer scones to prepared baking sheet
Sprinkle with additional sugar
Bake 10 minutes at 400
Reduce to 350 and continue baking until light brown, about 18 minutes
Cool slightly
Serve warm
