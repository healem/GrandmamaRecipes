                     Karin King's Zucchini Casserole

Recipe By     :Karin King
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Casseroles                      Luncheon
                Vegetables                      Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  6                     zucchinis
  2                     onions
  1 1/2            lbs  cheeses
                        i.e.,monterey jack, swiss, cheddar
  1              stick  butter
     1/2           cup  cream

Grate zucchini
Dice Onion
Grate cheese

Place 1/2 butter in skillet
Add onion
Saute until translucent
Add second 1/2 of butter
Add zucchini
Saute until clear

Drain all the moisture from the mixture

Return to skillet
Add cream and 3/4 cheese
saute until incorporated

Place one layer of mixture in 9x13 dish
Add 1/2 of cheese
Add the rest of the mixture
Cover with the remaining cheese

Bake 45 minutes at 350 until cheese is golden
