                     Maddy's Broccoli and Green Beans

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Casseroles                      Maddy's Vegetables
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  8               cups  broccoli florets (about 1 1/2 lbs)
     1/2            lb  green beans
  2               tbls  olive oil
  2                     cloves garlic, sliced thin
     1/2           tsp  crushed red pepper flakes
                        sea salt and pepper

In large pot, bring 6 quarts of saltedwater to a boil
Have ready a large bowl of ice water
Place green beans into pot and cook for 2 minutes
Add broccoli florets and cook 2 minutes more
Remove vegetables from pot and shock them in the ice water
When completely cook, place in colander and allow to drain

In large saute pan, heat olive oil
When almost smoking, add the garlic and saute for about 45 seconds
Remove immediately and discard
Add the red pepper flakes, broccoli and green beans to the pan and cook
for 5 minutes
Season with salt and pepper
Serve immediately
