                       Maddy's Baked Cheese Spread

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  mayonnaise
  2               cups  colby cheese, grated
  2               cups  onion, chopped
                  dash  hot red pepper sauce
                  dash  worcestershire sauce

Preheat oven to 350
Mix all ingredients together and place in a pie plate or any baking dish
Bake until golden brown on top, approximately 30 minutes
Serve with crackers or bagel chips
