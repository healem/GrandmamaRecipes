                   MADDY'S HUMMUS WITH YOGURT AND LEMON

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers
                Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2                 LG  GARLIC CLOVES, MINCED
  15                OZ  CAN GARBANZO BEANS, DRAINED
  2              TBLSP  PLAIN NON-FAT YOGURT
  2              TBLSP  TAHINI PASTE (SESAME SEED PASTE)
  2              TBLSP  LEMON JUICE, FRESH
  1                TSP  GROUND CUMIN

PLACE ALL INGREDIENTS IN FOOD PROCESSOR
BLEND UNTIL COARSE PUREE FORMS, OCCASIONALLY SCRAPING DOWN SIDES OF
PROCESSOR
SEASON HUMMUS TO TASTE WITH SALT AND PEPPER
TRANSFER TO SMALL BOWL
MAKES 1 1/2 CUPS

(CAN BE PREPARED 3 DAYS AHEAD
COVER AND REFRIGERATE
BRING TO ROOM TEMPERATURE BEFORE SERVING)
