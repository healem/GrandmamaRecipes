                        Maddy's Tomato Bruschetta

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Appetizers                      Breads
                Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4             slices  rustic country-style thickly sliced bread
  2             cloves  garlic, halved
     1/4           cup  extra virgin olive oil (the best)
     1/4           cup  fresh basil, stem removed, chopped
  3                     tomatoes (thinly sliced)
                        salt & pepper

Toast bread in toaster or oven until very brown

Immediately after removing bread from oven, rub the split side of a garlic
clove over the entire surface of 1 side of the bread. (each slice should
use up between 1/4 to 1/3 of a clove)  
BE CAREFUL - WHEN THE GARLIC MEETS THE HOT BREAD IT WILL CREATE FUMES THAT
STING YOUR EYES, JUST LIKE AN ONION

Generously drizzle olive oil over each slice
Sprinkle basil over top
Then top with tomato slices
Sprinkle generously with salt & pepper

Slice into 1 inch strips or quarters, and serve immediately
