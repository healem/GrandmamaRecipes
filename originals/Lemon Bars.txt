                                Lemon Bars

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 18    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cookies

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  all-purpose flour
     1/4           cup  sugar
  1                cup  butter
  4                     eggs
  2               cups  brown sugar
     1/4           cup  all-purpose flour
  1           teaspoon  double-acting baking powder
  1                cup  fresh coconut
  1                cup  walnuts
     1/4           cup  fresh lemon juice
  1         tablespoon  lemon zest -- grated
  1                cup  powdered sugar

     Mix 2 cups flour with 1/4 cup sugar.  Blend in butter until finely
mixed.  Press  into a jelly roll pan.  Bake 15 mins at 350 degrees.
     Beat together eggs, brown sugar, flour and baking powder.  Stir in
coconut and nuts. Spread filling over crust and return pan to oven.  Bake
25 mins more.
     Blend lemon juice, zest, and powdered sugar until smooth.  Spread on
hot cookies  Cool and cut into bars.
