                         Maddy's Cranberry Salsa

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Sauces
                Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2           cups  cranberries, fresh
  1                     green onion
  1                     jalapeno pepper
     1/3           cup  sugar
     1/4           cup  cilantro
  1             1 inch  ginger root
  1                     lime, juice and zest

Combine all ingredients in food processor
Chop to desired consistency
Do not overprocess
Colors should be visable
Refrigerate several ours to allow flavors to blend
