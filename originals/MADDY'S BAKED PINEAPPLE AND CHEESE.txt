                    MADDY'S BAKED PINEAPPLE AND CHEESE

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Canning, Preserves, Etc.        Cheese
                Desserts                        Fruit
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3               cans  20 oz, pineapple chunks, or 2 fresh -- reserve juice
  3               cups  shredded cheddar cheese
  6               tbls  flour
  1                cup  sugar
                        	
                        	
               TOPPING  	
     1/2         stick  butter, melted
  1 1/2           cups  ritz cracker crumbs

Mix drained pineapple and cheese in bowl
Mix sugar and flour together
Pour sugar, flour mixture over pineapple and cheese
Mix it all together
Place in greased casserole

TOPPING
Sprinkle with cracker crumbs
Spoon melted butter over top
Bake 45 minutes at 350
