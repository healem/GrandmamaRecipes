                             Maddy's Crostini

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1               loaf  crusty bread, sliced into 3/4 inch pieces
                        olive oil
                        salt & pepper

Preheat oven to 400
Place te bread in a single layer on a cookie sheet
Drizzle with olive oil 
Sprinkle with salt and pepper
Bake until bread is golden brown and crisp, about 8-10 minutes
