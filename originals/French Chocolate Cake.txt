                          French Chocolate Cake

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:30
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  20            ounces  semisweet chocolate -- coarsely chopped
  1                cup  butter -- 2 sticks
     1/4           cup  brandy
  6              whole  eggs -- separated, room temp
  1                cup  sugar
     1/4           cup  flour -- sifted
     1/4           cup  walnuts -- coarsely chopped
  1                cup  whipping cream
  1           teaspoon  vanilla


Preheat oven to 350
Butter and flour two 9-inch cake pans
Melt 12 oz chocolate with butter and brandy in double boiler over
simmering water
Stir until smooth
Remove chocolate mixture from over water and cool

Using electric mixer, beat egg yolks until pale yellow and slowly
dissolving ribbon forms when beaters are lifted
Gradually add sugar, flour, and vanilla
Continue beating until thick

Using clean dry beaters, beat egg whites in another bowl untill stiff
peaks form
Fold melted chocolate into yolk mixture
Gently fold in 1/4 of whites
Fold in remaining whites and nuts

Bour batter into prepared pans
Bake until tester inserted in centers comes out clean, about 30 minutes
Cool in pans on racks for 10 minutes
Invert onto racks.  COOL CAKE LAYERS COMPLETELY

Melt remaining 8 oz chocolate with cream in double boiler over simmering
water
Stir until smooth
Transfer to bowl set over larger bowl filled with ice
Using electric mixer, beat until cool
Set 1 cake layer on plate.  Spread with 1/3 of icing
Top with second cake layer.  Cover top and sides of cake with remaining
icing
Chill at least 1 hour before serving.
