                            Farmer's Breakfast

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : American                        Breakfast
                Eggs

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2         pound  bacon -- chopped
  2                     boiled potatoes -- peeled and cubed
  1                     onion -- sliced thinly
  8                     eggs -- beaten slightly
                        salt
                        black pepper

     Saute bacon until crisp.  Remove from pan, drain on paper towel. 
Pour off all but 3 tablespoons bacon fat.  Saute onions in bacon fat until
soft.  Add potatoes and cook, stirring often, until potatoes start to
brown.  If potatoes stick to pan, add more bacon fat.  When potatoes begin
to brown, add bacon and eggs.  Cook, stirring constantly, until eggs set. 
Season with salt and pepper to taste.
