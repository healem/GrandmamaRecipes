                           Maddy's Barley Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Maddy's Salads
                Salads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  3               cups  vegetable stock
  1                cup  pearl barley
                        salt to taste
     1/2           cup  parsley, fresh, chopped
  1              bunch  scallions, sliced thin
  1              bunch  radishes, sliced
  1                     cucumber, peeled, seeded and diced
  1                     red bell pepper, seeded and sliced
     1/2           cup  mint, fresh, chopped (or 1/4 cup dried)
                        	
                        DRESSING
  3        tablespoons  olive oil
  3        tablespoons  lemon juice, fresh
  4             cloves  garlic, mashed
                        salt to taste

Boil the vegetable stock
Add barley and salt
Cover, reduce heat and simmer until barley is tender and liquid is
absorbed, about 45 minutes
Drain well and place in a medium bowl

Mix dressing ingredients together and pour over barley

Allow barley to cool
Add parsley, scallions, radishes, cucumber, red pepper and mint

Mix well and chill for several hours
