                          Broccoli Sesame Salad

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:20
Categories    : Ethnic                          Salads
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1               head  broccoli
     1/4           cup  rice wine vinegar
     1/4           cup  soy sauce
  2        tablespoons  sesame oil
  2        tablespoons  olive oil
  4        tablespoons  sesame seeds -- toasted

     Wash broccoli, discarding leaves and toughest part of stem.  Blanch
entire head in boiling water for one minute.  Rinse under cold water. 
Break off florets and cut remaining stem (peeled, if desired) into 2"
pieces.
      Preheat oven to 450 degrees.  Spread broccoli pieces in one layer on
a greased baking sheet.  Roast at 450 degrees for 5 minutes, turn broccoli
pieces over, and continue roasting until broccoli begins to brown, about 5
minute more.
      Whisk together soy sauce, vinegar, and oils.  Stir in 3 tablespoons
sesame seeds.  When broccoli is done, transfer to a bowl and pour dressing
over it, stirring gently to coat.  Sprinkle with remaining tablespoon
sesame seeds.  Serve warm or at room temperature.
