                          Creamy Chocolate Sauce

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:15
Categories    : Desserts                        Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2           cups  milk
  2             ounces  baking chocolate
     1/2           cup  sugar
  1         tablespoon  all-purpose flour
  2        tablespoons  butter
  2        tablespoons  vanilla extract
  1               dash  salt

     Heat milk and chocolate in sucepan until chocolate melts.  Beat with
whisk until smooth.  Mix sugar, flour, and salt and stir slowly into
chocolate mixture.  Cook and stir 5 minutes.  Add butter and vanilla.
