                          Baked Shrimp Indienne

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Main Dishes                     Seafood

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  butter
  1              clove  garlic -- minced
  1         tablespoon  onion -- grated
  1           teaspoon  fresh parsley -- chopped
  1           teaspoon  fresh chervil -- chopped
  1           teaspoon  fresh tarragon -- chopped
     1/8      teaspoon  turmeric
     1/8      teaspoon  ground mace
     1/8      teaspoon  coriander
  1           teaspoon  salt
     1/2           cup  sherry
  1 1/2         pounds  shrimp -- cooked and peeled
  1 1/2           cups  bread crumbs
     1/4           cup  butter -- melted

Cream butter with herbs, spices, and salt. Beat in sherry. Cream the
mixture until soft and blend in shrimp.

Generously butter a baking dish. Make alternate layers of shrimp mixture
and bread crumbs. Finish with bread crumbs and pour the melted butter over
top.

Bake at 450 degrees for 15 minutes.
