                               Spanish Rice

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:40
Categories    : Ethnic                          Healthy And Hearty
                Rice

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2        tablespoons  olive oil
     1/2                large bell peppers -- chopped
  1                     medium onions -- sliced thinly
  1              clove  garlic -- minced
     1/2      teaspoon  dried basil
     1/2      teaspoon  rosemary
  1                cup  long-grain rice
  1                cup  tomatoes -- peeled and chopped
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/8      teaspoon  cayenne pepper
  2               cups  water

In a skillet saute green pepper, onion,and garlic in olive oil until
tender.  Stir in herbs,  rice, chopped tomatoes, salt, pepper, and water. 
 Bring to a boil over moderately high heat.  Cover, turn heat to low, and
cook  for about 20 minutes or until rice is done.
