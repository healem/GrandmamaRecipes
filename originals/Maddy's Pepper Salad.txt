                           Maddy's Pepper Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Maddy's Salads
                Salads                          Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                     green pepper, slivered
  1                     red pepper, slivered
  1                     yellow or orange pepper, slivered
  1                     tomato, seeded and diced
  1                     european cucumber, peeled, seeded and dice
     1/2                red onion, diced
     1/4           cup  capers
  3               tbls  olive oil
                        juice of 1 lemon

Mix peppers, tomato, cucumber and onion in bowl.
Separately mix capers, oil and lemon
Pour over ingredients and mix well
Place in refrigerator for at least an hour to blend flavors
