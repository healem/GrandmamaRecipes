                    MADDY'S DARK CHOCOLATE FUDGE CAKE

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:50
Categories    : Cakes                           Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2              BOXES  chocolate cake mix
  2 2/3           cups  water
  6             medium  eggs
     1/2           cup  oil
                        	
                        topping
  1               8 oz  pkg chocolate bits
  1                cup  all purpose cream

mix all cake ingredients in large bowl until blended well
run mixer for several minutes longer
grease angel food cake pan
pour mix into pan
bake at 325 until done, (probably 1 hour and 15-20 minutes) middle is dry
when pierced with probe

place chocolate bits and cream in double boiler.
mix constantly until blended

while cake is still hot, poke holes in it and pour the chocolate in the
holes all around cake
pour remaining chocolate on top as a glaze
