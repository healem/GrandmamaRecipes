                              Indian Pudding

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :3:30
Categories    : American                        Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2                     eggs
  6               cups  milk
     1/2           cup  molasses
     1/4           cup  brown sugar
  1           teaspoon  salt
  1           teaspoon  cinnamon
     1/2      teaspoon  ground ginger
  1                cup  yellow cornmeal
     1/4           cup  butter

     In top of double boiler set over hot water, beat together eggs and
four cups milk.  Beat in molasses, sugar, salt, cinnamon, and ginger and
cook until sugar dissolves.  Add cornmeal and butter and cook 20 minutes,
stirring occasionally.  Remove from heat, stir in remaining milk and pour
into greased baking dish.  Bake at 325 degrees for 3 hours, or until
pudding is firm.
