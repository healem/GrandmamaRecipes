                            Maddy's Baked Brie

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           pkg  Pepperidge Farm Frozen Puff Pastry Sheets -- (use 1 sheet)
  1                     egg
  1                     tbls water
  1                cup  apricot preserves
  1                 lb  Brie round

Remove 1 Pepperidge Farm sheet from package
Cover with plastic
Let stand at room temperature for 1/2 hour
Preheat oven to 400
Mix egg and water
Unfold pastry sheet on lightly floured board
Roll out until big enough to easily cover brie round
Cut off corners of pastry
Cover pastry to within 1 inch of edges with 1/2 apricot preserves
Put rest of apricot in middle of pastry round
Place brie on top
Brush edges of pastry with egg mixture
Fold two corners of dough up and over the brie
Fold in the other two sides, cutting off any excess
Make sure there are no holes (to fix, cut a piece of pastry, brush with
egg and place over the hole, pressing to make sure it adheres)
Turn upside down (folded dough on bottom) on ungreased cookie sheet
Brush egg mixture over top and sides
Bake about 20 minutes or until golden
Let stand for 1 hour
