                        Maddy's Horseradish Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Maddy's Sauces                  Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/4           cup  bottled horseradish sauce, drained
                        (or 1/4 cup grated fresh horseradish)
  1         tablespoon  lemon juice
  1           teaspoon  sugar
     1/4           cup  dry English mustard
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/2      teaspoon  white pepper
     1/2           cup  whipping cream, chilled

In a small bowl. stir the horseradish, lemon, sugar, mustard, salt and
pepper together until well blended
Beat the cream until stiff enough to form unwavering peaks
Pour the horseradish mixture over the cream and, with a spatula, fold
together lightly but thoroughly
Serve with roast beef or fish

Makes about 1 cup
