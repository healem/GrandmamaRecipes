                    Maddy's Pineapple Upside Biscuits

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Breakfast                       Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  10                oz  crushed pineapple
     1/2           cup  light brown sugar
     1/4           cup  butter
  10                    maraschino  cherries
  12                oz  pkg refrigerated buttermilk biscuits (10)

Preheat oven to 350

Grease 10 cups of a muffin tin
Strain can of crushed pineapple, saving juice for later
Combine the pineapple, sugar and butter and mix well
Divide the pineapple mixture among the muffin cups
Place a cherry in the center of each muffin cup, making sure cherry hits
bottom of cup
Place 1 biscuit in each cup on top of sugar and pineapple mixture
Spoon 1 teaspoon reserved pineapple juice over each biscuit
Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, or until golden brown
Cool for 2 minutes
Invert the pan onto a plate to release the biscuits
Serve warm
