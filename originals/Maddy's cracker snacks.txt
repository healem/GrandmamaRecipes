                          Maddy's cracker snacks

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :0:10
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Snacks

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              11 oz  package of oyster crackers
  1               1 oz  package hidden valley ranch mix
     1/2      teaspoon  dill weed
     3/4           cup  olive oil
     1/4    tablespoon  lemon pepper
     1/2    tablespoon  garlic powder

combine hidden valley ranch mix, dill weed, oil, lemon pepper and garlic
powder
pour over crackers
stir to coat
place in warm oven (250) for 10 minutes
stir gently 1/2 way through baking
pour onto paper towel to cool
put in zipper bag to keep fresh
