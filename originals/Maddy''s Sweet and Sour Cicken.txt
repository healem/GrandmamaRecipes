                      Maddy''s Sweet and Sour Cicken

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Chicken                         Maddy's Main Meals

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 lb  cheicken tenders (or chicken breasts)
  1         tablespoon  onion flakes
     1/2           cup  french dressing
     1/4           cup  spreadable fruit (grape)
  1           teaspoon  parsley flakes
                        salt & pepper to taste

Preheat oven to 325
Spray baking dish
Place chicken in baking dish
Combine all other ingredients
Spoon oven chicken
Cover and bake 30 minutes
Uncover and bake an additional 20 minutes until cooked through
