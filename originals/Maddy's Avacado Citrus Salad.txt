                       Maddy's Avacado Citrus Salad

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Maddy's Salads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  6              heads  curly endive, white only, wash & spin dry
  1              small  onion, chopped (or shallot)
  2              tblsp  white wine or champagne vinegar
  1                     lemon
  1                     orange
  2                     grapefruits
     3/4           cup  olive oil
  3               ripe  avacados
                        pinch salt

Scrape 1/4 tsp lemon and orange zest from fruit and set aside

Squeeze juice from lemon and orange.  Set aside

Peel shallot or onion and dice
Place in bowl and add vinegar, 1 tblsp each lemon and orange juice, and a
pinch of salt  Set aside

Remove grapefruit peel, white pith and membranes surrounding each segment.
 Cut the sections free.  Place in bowl and Set aside

Whisk the olive oil into the shallot mixture.  Add lemon and orange zest
and remaining juice.  Taste, adding more olive oil or lemon juice if
necessary

Cut avacados in half lengthwise and remove pits.  Scoop out meat of
avacado and slice about the same size as the grapefruit sections.

Add curly endive to bowl with grapefruit
Add about 2/3rds of the dressing'Arrange on platter or individual dishes

Artfully place avacad slices alongside the endive and grapefruit 
Season with salt and drizzle the rest of the dressing over them.
