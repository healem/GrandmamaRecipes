                    Maddy's Tomato Vegetable Casserole

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1             medium  potato, peeled, cut into 1/2 in pieces
  1             medium  yam, peeled and cut into 1/2 in pieces
  1             medium  red bell pepper, seeded and cut into 1/2 -- in pieces
  2                     carrots, peeled and cut into 1/2 in pieces
  5               tbls  olive oil
  1                     red onion, thinly sliced into rings
  2              small  zucchini, cut crosswise into 1/4 in pieces
                        salt and pepper
  2                 lg  tomatoes, cut crosswise into 1/4 in slices
     1/2           cup  grated cheese
  2               tbls  dried italian style bread crumbs
                        fresh basil for garnish

Preheat oven to 400
Toss potato, yam, bell pepper, carrots and 2 tablespoons olive oil in a 13
by 9 by 2 inch baking dish to coat
Sprinkle with salt and pepper and toss until coated
Spread vegetablres evenly over the bottom of the pan

Arrange onion slices evenly over the vegetable mixture
Arrange the zucchini over the onion
Drizzle with 2 tablespoons olive oiil
Sprinkle with salt and pepper
Arrnage tomato slices over the zucchini

Stir the parmesan and bread crumbs in a small bowl to blend
Sprinkle parmesan mixture over vegetables 
Drizzle with the last tablespoon of olive oil

Bake uncovered until the vegetables are tender and the topping is golden
brown (abot 40 minutes)
Garnsh with fresh basil springs
