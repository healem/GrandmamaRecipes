                        Maddy's Low Carb Meatloaf

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:30
Categories    : Maddy's Low Carb                Maddy's Main Meals
                Main Dishes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2                lbs  ground meat (pork, veal, turkey or beef)
  2                     eggs
     1/2           cup  grated parmesan cheese
     1/4           cup  red onion, diced
     1/4           cup  roasted or fresh red bell peppers, diced
  2               tbls  chopped fresh parsley
  2             cloves  garlic, minced
     1/2           tsp  dried oregano
     1/2           tsp  dried basic
  1                tsp  kosher salt
     1/2           tsp  black pepper
     1/4            lb  prosciutto, or any ham, thinly sliced
     1/4            lb  provolone cheese, sliced

preheat oven to 350
In a large bowl, mix meat, eggs, parmesan, vegetables, herbs and
seasonings
Working on waxed paper lined sheet pan or counter, form meatloaf mix into
a 10x8 inch flat rectangle on the waxed paper
Place a layer of prosciutto slices on top, followed by a layer of
provolone
Roll up the stuffed meatloaf mix like a burrito and seal the edges all
around by pinching the meat

Place the roll, seam side down, into a 5x9 inch loaf pan
Place in oven and bake for about 1 hour and 15 minutes, or until the
temperature registers 165
Drain fat and let rest at least 10 minutes before slicing.
