                         MADDY'S HOT FUDGE SAUCE

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:20
Categories    : Fillings & Frostings            Maddy's Desserts
                Maddy's Sauces                  Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4             ounces  unsweetened baking chocolate
  2        tablespoons  butter
     1/3           cup  black coffee
     1/3           cup  water -- boiling
  2               cups  sugar
     1/4           cup  corn syrup
  1         tablespoon  rum

Melt chocolate in top of double boiler set over gently simmering water
Add butter and stir until melted
Pour in coffee and water, stirring constantly until well combined
Blend in sugar and corn syrup
Transfer mixture to small saucepan
Cover and boil, without stirring, 3 minutes
Reduce heat and cook, uncovered, without stirring, 2 minutes
Remove from heat and stir in rum
Serve immediately
