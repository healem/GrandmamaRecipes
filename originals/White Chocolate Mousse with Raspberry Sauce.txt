               White Chocolate Mousse with Raspberry Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Desserts                        Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                MOUSSE  	
  1         tablespoon  unflavored gelatin
     1/4           cup  cold milk
                        	
  2               cups  milk
  8             ounces  white chocolate -- grated
     1/2           cup  sugar
  2                     egg whites -- room temperature
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
  8        tablespoons  confectioner's sugar -- 1/2 cup
  6        tablespoons  creme de cacao
  1                cup  whipping cream
                        	
                 SAUCE  	
  1            package  frozen sweet raspberries, (10 oz) -- thawed and drained
  6        tablespoons  raspberry jam
  1         tablespoon  raspberry liqueur

Sprinkle gelatin over milk.  Let stand until softened

Scald 2 cups milk in saucepan.  Remove from heat
Add gelatin, chocolate and sugar
Whisk until mixture isssmooth and gelatin is dissolved
Refrigerate until it is the consistency of egg whites, stirring
occasionally

Beat cream to soft peaks

Using clean, dry beaters, beat whites and salt until soft peaks form
Add powdered sugar, 1 tablespoon at a time
Beat until stiff, but not dry

Stir creme de cacao into chocolate mixture.  Fold in cream
Gently fold in egg whites
Pour into stemmed glasses or bowls
Chill well.  Drizzle sauce over top and serve
