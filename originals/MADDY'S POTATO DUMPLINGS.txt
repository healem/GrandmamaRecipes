                         MADDY'S POTATO DUMPLINGS

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 16    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Maddy's Vegetables              Potatoes
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4               cups  SHREDDED RAW POTATOES
  2               cups  FLOUR
  1               TBLS  SALT
                 quart  BEEF, CHICKEN OR HAM BROTH
  16            1/2 IN  CUBES HAM
                        MELTED BUTTER
                        PARSLEY GARNISH

PLACE POTATOES IN STRAINER
RINSE WITH COLD WATER TO PREVENT BROWNING
DRAIN WELL

IN A BOWL, COMBINE FLOUR AND SALT
ADD POTATOES
STIR UNTIL COATED

IN A MEDIUM SAUCEPAN, BRING BROTH TO BOIL OVER MEDIUM HEAT
USING A SERVING SPOON, SCOOP A ROUNDED SPOONFUL OF POTATO MIXTURE, ABOUT
THE SIZE OF AN EGG
PRESS ONE HAM CUBE INTO CENTER, COVERING COMPLETELY

USING SLOTTED SPOON, CAREFULLY LOWER DUMPLING INTO HOT BROTH
QUICKLY SHAPE REMAINING DUMPLINGS AND LOWER INTO BROTH

ADJUST HEAT TO MAINTAIN SIMMER, BUT NOT HARD BOIL
SIMMER AT LEAST 45 MINUTES, TURNING DUMPLINGS AFTER 25 MINUTES

USE SLOTTED SPOON TO LIFT DUMPLINGS FROM BROTH
SERVE HOT WITH MELTED BUTTER AND PARSLEY GARNISH
