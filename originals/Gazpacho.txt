                                 Gazpacho

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Healthy And Hearty              Soups & Stews
                Vegetables                      Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4               cups  red ripe tomatoes -- chopped
  1                cup  cucumber -- chopped
  1                cup  large bell peppers -- chopped
     1/2           cup  onion -- chopped
  1              clove  garlic
  1                cup  bread crumbs
     1/4           cup  olive oil
  1         tablespoon  balsamic vinegar
     1/2      teaspoon  salt
     1/4      teaspoon  white pepper

     Puree vegetables in blender or food processor.  Press through sieve
to remove seeds.  Puree bread crumbs with olive oil and vinegar.  Blend
into vegetable puree and season with salt and pepper.  Chill well.
