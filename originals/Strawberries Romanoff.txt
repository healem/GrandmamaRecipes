                          Strawberries Romanoff

Recipe By     :Madeline M. Healey
Serving Size  : 4     Preparation Time :0:20
Categories    : Desserts                        Fruit
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2             quarts  fresh strawberries -- cleaned and sliced
     1/8           cup  Cointreau
     1/8           cup  grand Marnier
  1                cup  heavy cream -- whipped
  1               pint  vanilla ice cream -- softened
     1/8           cup  lemon juice

Soak strawberries with liqour for 1/2 hour.
 Allow the ice cream to defrost slightly.

Whip the cream until stiff.
In another bowl, beat the vanilla ice cream with a wooden spoon. Fold in
the whipped cream, lemon juice, and remaining liquor and blend until
smooth.
Pour the whipped ice cream over the strawberries.
Serve at once in stemmed wine glass.
