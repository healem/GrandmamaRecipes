                            Boston Brown Bread

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :3:00
Categories    : American                        Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                cup  whole-wheat flour
  1                cup  rye flour
  1                cup  yellow cornmeal, degermed
  2          teaspoons  baking soda
  1           teaspoon  double-acting baking powder
  1           teaspoon  salt
  2               cups  buttermilk
     2/3           cup  molasses
     3/4           cup  seedless raisins

     Sift together flours, cornmeal, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
 Stir in raisins.  Beat together milk and molasses and stir this mixture
into dry ingredients.  Heavily butter two 1 lb coffee cans and divide
batter equally between them.  Cover with butter round of aluminum foil,
securing it in place with kitchen twine.  Place cans on a rack in a large
soup kettle.  Add boiling water to reach halway up sides of can.  Bring
water to a boil, cover kettle tightly, and simmer bread for two hours. 
Remove cans from kettle and allow bread to cool for ten minutes.  Turn
bread out of cans and serve at once.
