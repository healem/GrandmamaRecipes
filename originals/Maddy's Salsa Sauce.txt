                           Maddy's Salsa Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Maddy's Sauces                  Sauces

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2            lg cans  crushed tomatoes
  4             cloves  garlic
     1/2                red onion
  1                     red pepper
  1              small  yellow onion
  1               tbsp  dried cilantro (or 2 tbls fresh)
  2         small cans  green chilies (ortega)
  1               tbsp  sugar
  2                tsp  salt
  1               tbls  red wine vinegar
  2                tsp  dry basil (or 4 tsp fresh)
  3               tbsp  olive oil
  1                tsp  chili powder
     1/4           tsp  cumin
     1/4           tsp  paprika
     1/4           tsp  coriander
     1/4           tsp  cayenne pepper
                        red pepper sauce to taste
                        a couple of jalapenos for more kick

Place garlic, onions, peppers (and jalapenos if using them) in food
processor
Pulse until chopped
Add all other ingredients except tomatoes
Pulse again
Add tomatoes and pulse again a few times, leaving some chunks
