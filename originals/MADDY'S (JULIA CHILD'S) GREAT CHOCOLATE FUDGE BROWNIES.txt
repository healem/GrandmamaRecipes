          MADDY'S (JULIA CHILD'S) GREAT CHOCOLATE FUDGE BROWNIES

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Cookies                         Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4                 OZ  UNSALTED BUTTER
  4                 OZ  UNSWEETENED CHOCOLATE
  4                 OZ  MORE UNSALTED BUTTER CUT INTO 8 PIECES
  2               cups  SUGAR
  3              whole  LARGE EGGS
  1                TSP  VANILLA
     1/2           TSP  SALT
  1                cup  ALL PURPOSE FLOUR

PREHEAT OVEN TO 350
BUTTER A PAN 11 X 17
CUT A PIECE OF WAX PAPER TO FIT WITH OVERHANG AT EACH END AND PRESS INTO
PAN
BUTTER AND FLOUR THE PAPER, KNOCKING OUT EXCESS FLOUR

SET THE FIRST 4 OZ OF BUTTER AND THE CHOCOLATE IN A MELTING PAN AND PLACE
IN DOUBLE BOILER WITH 2 OR 3 INCHES OF WATER
BRING NEAR TO SIMMER AND LET THE CHOCOLATE AND BUTTER MELT TOGETHER

CREAM THE SECOND 4 OZ OF BUTTER WITH THE SUGAR UNTIL LIGHT AND FLUFFY
BEAT IN THE EGGS ONE AT A TIME
ADD VANILLA AND SALT
STIR IN THE WARM MELTED CHOCOLATE MIXTURE
GRADUALLY SIFT AND FOLD IN THE FLOUR
SPREAD THE BATTER EVENLY INTO THE PAN
BAKE AT ONCE IN MIDDLE LEVEL OF OVEN
SET TIMER FOR 25 MINUTES

BAKE ABOUT 25 MINUTES UNTIL SET BUT TOP IS STILL SPONGY
A TOOTHPICK INSERTED SHOULD COME OUT WITH A FEW SPECKS OF CHOCOLATE ON IT

REMOVE FROM OVEN AND LET COOK IN PAN FOR 10 MINUTES
TURN PAN UPSIDE DOWN OVER A CAKE RACK AND UNMOLD, GENTLY REMOVING THE WAX
PAPER

WHEN COOL, CUT INTO 3 BY 1 1/2 INCH RECTANGLES.

(*** IF NOT USING RIGHT AWAY, BEFORE CUTTING, COVER WITH WAX PAPER AGAIN
AND PLACE BACK IN THE BAKING PAN.  COVER AIRTIGHT AND STORE IN THE
REFRIGERATOR FOR A DAY OR 2, OR FREEZE)
