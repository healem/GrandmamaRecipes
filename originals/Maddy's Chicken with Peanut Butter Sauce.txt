                 Maddy's Chicken with Peanut Butter Sauce

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:30
Categories    : Chicken                         Maddy's Main Meals
                Main Dishes                     Poultry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  6                     chicken breasts, skinless and boneless
  1         tablespoon  vegetable oil
  2          teaspoons  salt
  1           teaspoon  curry powder
  1              large  onion, peeled and thinly sliced
  1              large  red pepper, cut into thin strips
  8             ounces  Newman's Own Mild Salta (1 cup)
  1              large  tomato, thinly sliced
  1 1/2           cups  uncooked rice
     1/4           cup  crunchy peanut butter
     1/4           cup  warm water

Place vegetable oil in 12 inch skillet
Add chicken and cook in batches over medium high heat for 5 minutes,
turning once, until golden, remove and set aside

Reduce heat to medium, add salt, curry powder, onion and red pepper.
Cook  1 minute, stirring
Add salsa and tomato to skillet.
Cover and simmer 5 minutes

Return chicken to skillet
Cover and simmer over medium lowheat for 25 minutes or until chicken is
cooked through

While chicken cooks, cook rice as label directs.
Spoon rice onto a large platter
Arrange chicken on top
Keep warm

Whisk peanut butter with warm water in a cup
Stir into vegetable mixture in skillet
Heat to boiling
Spoon over chicken and rice
