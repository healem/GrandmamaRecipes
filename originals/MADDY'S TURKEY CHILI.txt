                           MADDY'S TURKEY CHILI

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Casseroles                      Luncheon
                Maddy's Main Meals              Main Dishes
                Mexican

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2              tblsp  olive oil
  1             medium  onion, peel and chopped
  2             cloves  garlic, minced
     1/2        medium  green pepper, chopped
     1/2        medium  red pepper, chopped
  3             medium  italian tomatoes, chopped
     3/4           cup  beef broth
  2                 ts  sugar
     1/2        square  unsweetened chocolate
  1                 ts  chili powder
     1/2           tss  cayenne pepper
     1/4            ts  sea salt
     1/4            ts  cinnamon
  5           shavings  nutmeg
  1               dash  ground cloves
  4               cups  leftover turkey, chopped
  1                     corn tortillas

saute onion, garlic and peppers in olive oil until softened
add tomatoes, and saute for 5 more minutes
add broth and all remaining spices
stir until the cocolate has melted
stir in chopped turkey
let mixture simmer for 15 minutes
add ground tortilla to thicken and smooth out the texture

serve with plenty of steamed white rice
