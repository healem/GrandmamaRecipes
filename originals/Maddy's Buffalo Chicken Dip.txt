                       Maddy's Buffalo Chicken Dip

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 12    Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Appetizers                      Cheese
                Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  8                 oz  cheddar cheese, shredded (1 pkg)
  2               8 oz  pks cream cheese
  8                 oz  blue cheese dressing (Marie's Light)
  1                 lb  (or more) cooked chicken tenders,ngget siz
                        Frank's or Texas Pete's hot sauce
                        to taste (6 - 8 oz)
                        Fresh blue cheese to taste added -- after cooking

Put all ingredients in 3 qt saucepan 
Mix together
Cook on medium heat for 10-15 minutes or until cheeses are melted

Serve with chicken, tortilla chips, carrots and/or celery
