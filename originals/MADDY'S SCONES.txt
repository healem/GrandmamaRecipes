                              MADDY'S SCONES

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Breads                          Maddy's Breads

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  WHOLE WHEAT FLOUR
  2               TBLS  SUGAR
  4                 TS  BAKING POWDER
     2/3           cup  MILK
     1/2          TBLS  SALT
     1/2           cup  BUTTER, CUT IN PIECES (1  STICK
  1                 LG  EGG (RESERVE 1 TS WHITE)

PREHEAT OVEN TO 400
SIFT DRY INGREDIENTS TOGETHER
CUT BUTTER INTO DRY INGREDIENTS UNTIL MIXTURE IS CRUMBLY
BEAT EGG AND MILK TOGETHER
STIR QUICKLY INTO DRY INGREDIENTS
STIR IN CURRANTS (OR OTHER ADDITIVES)
PAT OUT 1/2 INCH THICK AND CUT INTO WEDGES
PLACE ON GREASED COOKIE SHEET
BEAT RESERVED EGG WHITE LIGHTLY WITH 1/2 TS WATER
BRUSH ON TOP OF SCONES AND SPRINKLE WITH ADDITIONAL SUGAR
BAKE FOR 12 TO 15 MINUTES UNTIL LOVELY GOLDEN BROWN
