                             Orange Fruitcake

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :1:30
Categories    : Cakes

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2           cup  butter
  1                cup  brown sugar
  1                     egg
                        orange rind -- of one orange
  1                cup  seedless raisins
     3/4           cup  buttermilk
  1           teaspoon  baking soda
  1 1/4           cups  all-purpose flour
     3/4      teaspoon  double-acting baking powder
     1/4      teaspoon  cinnamon

     Dissolve soda in buttermilk.  Sift together flour, baking powder, and
cinnamon.  Cream butter and sugar.  Add egg and orange rind and beat until
fluffy.  Gradually add flour mixture, alternating with buttermilk mixture.
 Stir in raisins.  Pour into greased and floured 8" square pan.
     Bake at 350 degrees for 45 minutes or until cake tester comes out
clean.  Cool 15 minutes.  Spread with orange glaze while still warm.
