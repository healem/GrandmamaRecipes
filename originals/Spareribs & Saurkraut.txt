                          Spareribs & Saurkraut

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :2:15
Categories    : Main Dishes                     Pork & Ham

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  4                     yellow onions
  2             quarts  sauerkraut
  2              small  spareribs

Cut onions in half and place in casserole.  Spread sauerkraut over onions.
 Place ribs on top.  Brown in a 500-degree oven, reduce heat to 450
degrees, cover tightly, and bake 1-1/2 to 2 hours.
