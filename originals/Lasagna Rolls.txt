                              Lasagna Rolls

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Main Dishes                     Pasta
                Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  6                     lasagna noodles -- cooked,drained
     1/4           cup  mozzarella cheese -- shredded
     1/4           cup  fontina cheese -- grated
  1                cup  broccoli flowerets -- cooked
     1/2           cup  mushrooms -- sliced
     1/4           cup  fresh parsley -- chopped
     1/4           cup  fresh basil -- chopped
     1/4           cup  fresh chives -- chopped
  1              pound  cottage cheese
  1          15 oz jar  spaghetti sauce
     1/3           cup  Parmesan cheese -- grated

     Combine cheeses,  broccoli, mushrooms and herbs in food processor and
chop coarsely.  Add cottage cheese just to blend.  Add more seasoning if
desired.
      Oil flat bottomed casserole large enough to hold rolled lasagne in
one layer.  Pour 1/2 inch of the spaghetti sauce on bottom of casserole.
      Halve lasagna noodles.  Spread each with herb-cheese mixture (about
1/4 cup on each half).  Roll up and place seam side down in casserole. 
Cover with remaining sauce. Sprinkle with grated parmesan.  Cover with
foil.
      Bake covered with foil 20 minutes in a 350-degree oven, then uncover
and bake until brown and bubbly -- about 20 minutes more.
