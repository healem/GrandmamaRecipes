                        Maddy's Peanut Butter Pie

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Maddy's Desserts                Pies & Pastry

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                     graham cracker crust
  1 1/2            cps  heavy whipping cream
     1/4           cup  sugar
  8                 oz  cream cheese
  1                cup  crunchy peanut butter
  1                cup  confection sugar

Whip cream with 1/4 cup sugar, set aside
Mix all other ingredients unti smooth 
Fold in whipped cream until well blended
Pour mixture into a graham cracker crust
Chill several hours before serving
