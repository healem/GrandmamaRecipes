              Maddy's (neiman marcus) chocolate chip cookies

Recipe By     :madeline m healey
Serving Size  : 112   Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Cookies                         Desserts
                Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2               cups  butter
  4               cups  flour
  2                tsp  baking soda
  2               cups  sugar
  5               cups  blended outmeal*
  24                oz  chocolate chips
  2               cups  brown sugar
  1                tsp  salt
  8                 oz  hersey bar (grated)
  4              whole  eggs
  2                tsp  baking powder
  2                tsp  vanilla
  3               cups  chopped nuts (optional)

*measure outmeal and blend in a blender to a fine powder
cream butter and both sugars together
add eggs and vanilla
add flour, oatmeal, salt, baking powder and soda and mix together
add chocolate chips, hershey bar and nuts
roll into balls and place 2 inches apart on a cookie sheet
bake for 10 minutes at 375
