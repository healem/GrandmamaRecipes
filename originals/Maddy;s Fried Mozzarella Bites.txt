                      Maddy;s Fried Mozzarella Bites

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 32    Preparation Time :0:00
Categories    : Appetizers                      Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
                        olive oil, for frying
  1                 lb  ball fresh mozzarella, wrapped -- not in water
  1                 lb  smoked mozzarella, (speciality cheese case
  1                cup  flour
     1/3           cup  milk
  2                     eggs
  2               cups  italian style bread crumbs
  2               tbls  chopped fresh parsley
  8              stems  or sprigs each fresh parsley, sage, thyme
                        	
                        ARRABIATA DIPPING SAUCE
  2               tbls  evoo
  4             cloves  garlic, chopped
     1/2            ts  crushed red pepper flakes
  28                oz  can tomatoes, crushed

Heat 2 inches of olive oil in a deep skillet over medium high heat
Cut each mozzarella into 16 cubes

Set up breading station:
flour, beaten eggs with milk, bread crumbs mixed with a couple of handfuls
of grated cheese

Roll plain mozzarella in flour, dip in egg, then coat in bread crumbs
set aside

Add parsley to bread crumbs
roll smoked mozzarella in flour, dip in egg, then coat in bread crumbs and
parsley
set aside

THE PARSLEY WILL DIFFERENTIATE BETWEEN PLAIN AND SMOKED MOZZARELLA ONCE
COOKED

place herbs in hot oil and fry 30 to 45 seconds
remove herbs with a slotted spoon and transfer to a paper towel lined
plate to drain
season immediately with salt
fry the mozzarella bites in batches in a single layer
cook until evenly deep golden brown, 3 to 5 minutes
drain and cool mozzarella bites on a paper towel-lined surface
let stand and cool a few minutes so that the cheese won't be too runny
when bitten into

ARRABIATA DIPPING SAUCE
heat a small saucepan over medium heat
add evoo, garlic and crushed pepper flakes
saute garlic and hot pepper 2 to 3 minutes
add tomatoes
season with salt

TO ASSEMBLE
Pile mozzarella bites on a serving plate, alternting them with the fried
herbs
set the dipping sauce in a bowl along side and pile bamboo skewerrs on
edge of the plate to spear and dip the bites

add some mixed olives, celery sticks, lng bread sticks to round out this
finger food selection
