              MADDY'S BAKED PENNE PASTA WITH WILD MUSHROOMS

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Casseroles                      Maddy's Pasta Dishes
                Pasta                           Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     1/2            OZ  DRIED PORCINI MUSHROOMS
  2               cups  HOT WATER
                        	
  6               TBLS  BUTTER
  1 3/4           cups  FINELY CHOPPED ONIONS
  8                 OZ  CHOPPED BUTTON MUSHROOMS, FRESH
  6                 OZ  SLICED SHIITAKE MUSHROOMS, FRESH
                        	
  2 1/2           TBLS  ALL PURPOSE FLOUR
  2 1/2           cups  WHOLE MILK
     3/4           cup  GRATED PARMESAN CHEESE
     1/2           cup  FINELY CHOPPED CHIVES
                        	
  12                OZ  PENNE PASTA
     1/2           cup  BREADCRUMBS

PLACE PORCINI IN SMALL BOWL
ADD 2 CUPS HOT WATER AND LET SOFTEN 20 MINUTES
WITH SLOTTED SPOON, REMOVE PORCINI
RESERVE LIQUID
COARSELY CHOP PORCINI

MELT 3 TBLS BUTTER IN HEAVY LARGE SKILLET OVER MEDIUM HIGH HEAT
ADD ONIONS AND SAUTE UNTIL SOFT, ABOUT 5 MINUTES
ADD BUTTON AND SHIITAKE MUSHROOMS AND SAUTE 5 MINUTES
MIX IN PORCINI AND RESERVED SOAKING LIQUID (LEAVING ANY SEDIMENT BEHIND IN
BOWL)
SIMMER OVER MEDIUM HIGH HEAT 10 MINUTES

MELT REMAINING 3 TBLS BUTTER IN HEAVY MEDIUM SAUCEPAN OVER MEDIUM HEAT
ADD FLOUR AND STIR CONSTANTLY UNTIL GOLDEN, ABOUT 2 MINUTES
GRADUALLY WHISK IN MILK
STIR UNTIL SAUCE THICKENS AND BOILS, ABOUT 3 MINUTES
STIR INTO MUSHROOM MIXTURE
SIMMER 2 MINUTES, STIRRING OCCASIONALLY
STIR IN 1/2 CUP PARMESAN CHEESE AND CHIVES
SEASON SAUCE TO TASTE WITH SALT AND PEPPER
SET ASIDE

PREHEAT OVEN TO 425
BUTTER 13X9X2 INCH GLASS BAKING DISH
COOK PASTA IN LARGE POT OF BOILING SALTED WATER UNTIL TENDER, BUT STILL
FIRM TO BITE
DRAIN WELL
RETURN TO POT
ADD MUSHROOM SAUCE AND TOSS WELL TO COAT
TRANSFER TO PREPARED BAKING DISH
SPRINKLE BREADCRUMBS AND REMAINING 1/4 CUP PARMESAN OVER THE TOP
BAKE CASSEROLE UNTIL HEATED THROUGH AND LIGHTLY GOLDEN, ABOUT 25 MINUTES
