                          Maddy's Broccoli Soup

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 8     Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Maddy's Soups

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2            lbs  broccoli, florets and top third of stalks, -- chopped
  2               tbls  olive oil
  1                     onion, chopped
  4                     carrots, chopped
  4             stalks  celery, chopped
  16                oz  mushrooms, chopped
  32                oz  chicken broth
  1                 ts  salt
                 pinch  cayenne
                        cheddar croutons for garnish

Saute onion, carrots, celery until softened

In crock pot, place chicken broth, broccoli, mushrooms, and softened
vegetables
Heat until vegetables are cooked through
Puree until smooth
