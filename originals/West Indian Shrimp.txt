                            West Indian Shrimp

Recipe By     :Elizabeth Powell
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :0:30
Categories    : Ethnic                          Luncheon
                Main Dishes                     Seafood

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1 1/2         pounds  shrimp, cooked -- drained and peeled
  2        tablespoons  olive oil
  1         tablespoon  Angostura bitters
  1           teaspoon  dried chervil
     1/2      teaspoon  marjoram
     1/2      teaspoon  thyme
     1/2      teaspoon  cumin seed
     1/4      teaspoon  saffron
     1/2           cup  grated Parmesan cheese

In an ovenproof skillet, heat olive oil until smoking. Quickly saute
shrimp. Sprinkle with bitters and herbs. Top with Parmesan cheese and
broil until lightly browned.
