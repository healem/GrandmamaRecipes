                         maddy's baked camembert

Recipe By     :madeline m healey
Serving Size  : 10    Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Appetizers                      Cheese
                Maddy's Appetizers

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1              round  camembert cheese
                        or brie in a wooden box
  1                     crisp baguette cut into chunks

preheat oven to 375.
remove and discard paper labels from box
unwrap cheese and place back in box - cover with lid
place on baking sheet
bake 20 - 30 minutes until completely soft
transfer box to plate and remove lid
use serated knife to slice off top rind
serve immediately with bread chunks
