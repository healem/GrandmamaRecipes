                  MADDY'S SESAME AND GINGER NOODLE SALAD

Recipe By     :MADELINE M HEALEY
Serving Size  : 14    Preparation Time :2:00
Categories    : Luncheon                        Maddy's Salads
                Salads                          Vegetarian

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
     3/4           cup  SOY SAUCE
  9               TBLS  LEMON JUICE
  6               TBLS  MINCED FRESH GINGER
  6               TBLS  TAHINI
  4 1/2           TBLS  HONEY
  2                 TS  CRUSHED RED PEPPER
  6               TBLS  SESAME OIL
                        	
  24                OZ  CHUKA SOBA (THIN YELLOW JAPANESE NOODLES
  6             medium  CARROTS, CUT IN MATCHSTICK STRIPS
  1 1/2                 CUCUMBERS, SEEDED, PEELED,
                        CUT IN MATCHSTICK STRIPS
  1 1/2        bunches  GREEN ONIONS, THINLY SLICED DIAGONALLY
  12                OZ  SNOW PEAS, THINLY SLICED DIAGONALLY
  3                     RED BELL PEPPERS, CUT INTO
                        MATCHSTICK STRIPS
     1/4           cup  SESAME SEEDS, TOASTED

WHISK FIRST 6 INGREDIENTS IN MEDIUM BOWL
MIX IN 3 TBLS SESAME OIL

COOK NOODLES IN LARGE POT OF BOILING SALTED WATER UNTIL JUST TENDER BUT
STILL FIRM TO BITE, STIRRING OCCASIONALLY, ABOUT 5 MINUTES
DRAIN
TRANSFER HOT NOODLES TO LARGE BOWL
TOSS WITH 3 TBLS OIL
ADD ALL VEGETABLES;  TOSS
TOSS WITH DRESSING
SEASON WITH SALT AND PEPPER
SPRINKLE WITH SESAME SEEDS
