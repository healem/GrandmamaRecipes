                       Maddy's cherry-apple crunch

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Desserts                        Maddy's Desserts

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  1                 lb  frozen pitted tart cherries
  1              green  apple, cored and diced
     1/4           cup  light brown sugar
     1/2      teaspoon  almond extract
  1 1/2    tablespoons  cornstarch or arrowroot
     1/2           cup  unsweetened cherry or apple juice
                        nonstick cooking spray
                        	
                        TOPPING
     1/4           cup  old fashioned rolled oats
     1/4           cup  brown sugar
     1/4           cup  nuts, chopped
  2        tablespoons  whole wheat pastry flour
  3        tablespoons  light olive oil
     1/4      teaspoon  salt (optional)

Preheat oven to 400

In a bowl, toss together cherries, apple, brown sugar and almond extract

In a cup, mix the cornstarch and juice
Add to the fruit mixture, stirring well

Pour the mixture into an 8-inch square baking dish sprayed with nonstick
cooking spray

Mix together the topping ingredients
Crumble the mixture on top of the fruit

Bake for 30 minutes
Raise heat to broil and brown topping lightly for 1-2 minutes
Remove from oven
Serve warm or cold
