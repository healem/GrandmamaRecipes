                         Maddy's Vegetable Curry

Recipe By     :Madeline M Healey
Serving Size  : 6     Preparation Time :1:00
Categories    : Ethnic                          Healthy And Hearty
                Maddy's Main Meals              Maddy's Vegetables

  Amount  Measure       Ingredient -- Preparation Method
--------  ------------  --------------------------------
  2              tblsp  vegetable oil
  1                 lg  onion, chopped
  1                 ts  curry powder
     1/2            ts  pepper
  1                 ts  salt
  1                 ts  cumin seeds
     1/2            ts  fennel seeds
     1/2            ts  mustard seeds
     1/2            ts  cinnamon
     1/2            ts  ground corriander
  2                     bay leaves
                        	
                        VEGETABLES
  2             medium  carrots, chopped
     1/2           cup  sliced snow peas
     1/2            lb  green beans, sliced to 1 inch
  1                     red pepper, chopped
  1                     yellow, pepper, chopped
     1/2            lb  broccoli florets, cut into 1/4 inch pieces
     1/2            lb  zuccini, chopped
  3                     celery stalks, chopped
     1/2           cup  lemon juice

In large skiller, add oil and heat over  medium to high heat
Add onions and saute until soft, about 3 minutes
Add all spices and cook about 1 minute
Add vegetables
Cover with lemon juice and bring to a boil
Reduce heat and simmer until vegetables are tender, about 15 or 20 minutes
Remove cover and continue cooking a few more minutes to remove some of the
liquid
Remove bay leaves befre serving

Serve with rice
